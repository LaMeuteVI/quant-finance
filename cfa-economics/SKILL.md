## Core Frameworks & Mental Models
**Microeconomics**
- **Elasticity**: `E_P = %ΔQ/%ΔP`; |E| > 1 elastic; revenue peaks at |E| = 1; income elasticity sign gives normal/inferior; cross sign gives substitutes/complements (ch01).
- **Operate/shut/exit**: produce where `MR = MC` (MC rising); operate while `P ≥ AVC`, stay long run while `P ≥ ATC`; breakeven `P = ATC` (ch01).
- **Markup rule**: `MR = P(1 − 1/ε)`, `P = MC/(1 − 1/ε)`; Cournot `q = (a − c)/[b(N + 1)]`; Stackelberg leader earns more; cartels cheat; `HHI = Σ s²` ignores entry (ch02).
- **Market structure ladder**: perfect competition (`P = MC = min ATC`), monopolistic competition (excess capacity), oligopoly, monopoly (`P > MC`, DWL); regulated natural monopoly: average-cost pricing (ch02, ch10).

**Macroeconomics**
- **GDP and sectoral balances**: `GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)`; `S = I + (G − T) + (X − M)` (ch03).
- **AD/AS diagnosis**: same-direction moves of output and prices = AD; opposite = SRAS (stagflation); gap states map to asset tilts (ch03).
- **Growth accounting**: `g_Y = g_A + α g_K + (1 − α) g_L`; potential growth = labor growth + productivity growth (ch03, ch09).
- **Cycle reading**: leading/coincident/lagging indicators; hours before heads; inventory-sales rises near the peak; markets lead by 3-6 months (ch04).
- **Price indexes**: Laspeyres upward-biased; Fisher = √(L × P); core versus headline; cost-push vs demand-pull (ch04).
- **Policy stance**: neutral rate = trend growth + inflation target; fiscal multiplier `1/[1 − c(1 − t)]`; stance = change in structural balance; debt drift `(r − g)D/Y` (ch05).

**International**
- **Comparative advantage**: lowest opportunity cost; small-country tariff DWL = B + D; `CA = Sp + Sg − I` (ch06).
- **Covered interest parity**: `F = S(1 + i_P τ)/(1 + i_B τ)`; higher-yielding currency at forward discount; forwards are not forecasts (ch07, ch08).
- **Parity ladder**: only CIP is arbitrage-enforced; UIP, forward-rate parity and PPP are long-run anchors; carry trades profit from UIP failure and carry crash risk (ch08).
- **Marshall-Lerner and absorption**: `ω_X ε_X + ω_M(ε_M − 1) > 0`; J-curve; depreciation needs saving to rise relative to investment (ch07).
- **Policy mix and FX**: tight money plus loose fiscal strengthens the currency under high capital mobility; Dornbusch overshooting (ch08).

**Growth and regulation**
- **Solow steady state**: per capita growth `θ/(1 − α)`; saving, population and depreciation change levels only; endogenous growth `Δy/y = sc − δ − n` makes saving matter (ch09).
- **Convergence**: absolute, conditional, club; institutions and openness decide (ch09).
- **Regulation rationale**: informational frictions, externalities, weak competition, social objectives; capture, competition, arbitrage; net regulatory burden (ch10).
- **Analyst filter**: probability of adoption, then revenue, cost and business risk, then forecasts and discount rate (ch10).
