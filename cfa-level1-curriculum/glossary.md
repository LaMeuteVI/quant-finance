# Glossary — CFA Level I Notes

**Absolute vs comparative advantage** — Producing with fewer resources vs at a lower opportunity cost (Ch 6)
**Accrued interest** — (t/T) × coupon owed to the seller between coupon dates (Ch 16, 18)
**ADR / GDR / GRS** — Depository receipts traded in the US / outside the US and the home market / an identical share quoted in several currencies (Ch 12)
**Automatic stabilizers** — Taxes and transfers that move against the cycle without new legislation (Ch 3, 5)
**Balloon risk** — Failure to refinance a balloon payment at maturity (Ch 17)
**Bid–ask spread** — Dealer's ask price minus bid price (Ch 7, 8)
**Carrying value** — Price on the constant-yield trajectory (amortized cost) (Ch 18)
**Contraction / extension risk** — Prepayments faster (rates fall) / slower (rates rise) than expected (Ch 17)
**Conversion ratio** — Par / conversion price (Ch 14)
**Covered bond** — Bond backed by a cover pool kept on the bank's balance sheet (dual recourse) (Ch 14)
**CPR / SMM** — Annual / monthly prepayment rate; CPR = 1 − (1 − SMM)¹² (Ch 17)
**Crowding out** — Government borrowing raises rates and displaces private investment (Ch 3, 5)
**Current yield** — Annual coupon / flat price (Ch 14, 16)
**Deadweight loss** — Welfare lost from output below the competitive level (Ch 2, 6)
**Discount margin** — Spread over the reference rate that prices an FRN (Ch 16)
**Duration gap** — Macaulay duration − investment horizon (Ch 18)
**Effective duration** — Sensitivity to parallel shifts of the benchmark curve; used for bonds with embedded options (Ch 18)
**Elasticity** — % change in quantity / % change in a driver (Ch 1)
**Enterprise value** — Market cap + preferred + debt − cash (Ch 13)
**Expected loss** — PD × loss given default (Ch 19)
**Fisher effect** — Nominal rate = real rate + expected inflation (Ch 5)
**Forward premium / discount** — Forward rate above / below spot for the base currency (Ch 7)
**Free float** — Shares available to public investors (Ch 9)
**G-spread / I-spread / Z-spread / OAS** — Spread over government bonds / swaps / the whole spot curve / the Z-spread net of option value (Ch 16)
**Giffen / Veblen good** — Upward-sloping demand driven by the income effect / by status (Ch 1)
**Haircut** — Collateral value − repo loan amount (Ch 15)
**Horizon yield** — Annualized holding-period return (Ch 18)
**Justified P/E** — Payout / (r − g) (Ch 13)
**Laspeyres / Paasche / Fisher index** — Base-period basket / current basket / geometric mean of the two (Ch 4)
**LTV / DSC** — Loan-to-value / debt-service coverage = NOI / debt service (Ch 17)
**Macaulay duration** — PV-weighted average time to cash flows (Ch 18)
**Marshall–Lerner condition** — ε_X + ε_M > 1 for a depreciation to improve the trade balance (Ch 7)
**Money multiplier** — 1 / reserve requirement (Ch 5)
**NAIRU** — Unemployment rate consistent with stable inflation (Ch 4)
**Nash equilibrium** — No player gains by deviating alone (Ch 2)
**Neutral rate** — Trend real growth + inflation target (Ch 5)
**Notching** — Adjusting issue ratings from the issuer baseline for seniority (Ch 19)
**Okun's law** — 1pp of excess unemployment ≈ 2% output gap (Ch 4)
**PAC / support tranche** — CMO tranche protected within a prepayment band / tranche absorbing prepayment variability (Ch 17)
**Pari passu** — Equal ranking of claims (Ch 19)
**Pass-through rate** — Mortgage rate − servicing fees (Ch 17)
**PVBP (PV01)** — Price change for a 1bp yield change (Ch 18)
**Quota rent** — Profit captured by holders of import quotas (Ch 6)
**Repo / reverse repo** — Sell and repurchase (cash borrower) / buy and resell (cash lender) (Ch 15)
**Seigniorage** — Profit from issuing money (Ch 7)
**Sinking fund** — Periodic retirement of principal (Ch 14)
**SPV** — Bankruptcy-remote entity holding securitized assets (Ch 14, 17)
**Terms of trade** — Export prices / import prices (Ch 6)
**Trade creation / diversion** — Integration replacing costly domestic output with cheaper member imports / replacing cheaper non-member imports with costlier member imports (Ch 6)
**Weak / semi-strong / strong efficiency** — Prices reflect past market data / all public information / all information (Ch 10)
**Yield-to-worst** — Lowest of yield-to-call and yield-to-maturity (Ch 16)
