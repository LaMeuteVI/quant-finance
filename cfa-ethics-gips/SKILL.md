## Core Frameworks & Mental Models
**Structure and method**
- **Code of Ethics (6 principles) and Standards I-VII (22 sub-standards)**: principles are aspirational, Standards are binding; apply both and follow the strictest of law, employer policy and Code (ch02, ch03).
- **Ethical decision-making framework**: Identify, Consider, Decide and act, Reflect; name situational influences and seek independent advice (ch01).
- **Violation-spotting routine**: list actors and acts, best-fit Standard each, stricter-rule check, timing of disclosure, actor vs supervisor, near-miss distractors (ch09, ch10).
- **PCP flow**: inquiry, investigation, charges, accept or reject, DRC panel, sanction (censure, suspension, revocation); cautionary letter is not a sanction (ch02).

**Standard I and II**
- **Dissociation ladder**: confront, escalate, step away, resign if extreme; document; association after knowledge equals participation (ch03).
- **I(B) gift rule**: client gifts need employer disclosure; inducements from brokers, issuers, managers are barred if they could compromise independence (ch03).
- **MNPI two gates**: material AND nonpublic; seek dissemination, else tell compliance only, do not trade, tip or change recommendations (ch04).
- **Mosaic theory** protects public plus nonmaterial inputs; keep records (ch04, ch07).
- **Firewall elements**: clearance via compliance, watch and restricted lists, no personnel overlap, passive market making, suspended risk arbitrage (ch04).

**Standard III and IV**
- **Who is the client?** Beneficiaries for plans; the mandate for funds; investing public for analysts (ch05).
- **Fair, not equal**: simultaneous dissemination, pre-set allocation, pro rata for oversubscribed issues, family fee-paying accounts treated as clients (ch05).
- **IPS-first suitability**: reasonable inquiry, written IPS, total-portfolio context, update at least annually (ch05).
- **Performance presentation**: GIPS best; include terminated accounts, label simulated, gross or net, prior-firm results (ch05, ch12).
- **Employer duties**: prepare but do not compete when leaving; written consent from all parties for competing pay; supervisors ensure compliance with a tailored system and investigate scope (ch06).

**Standards V to VII**
- **V(A)-(C)**: reasonable basis (rely on vetted research, stress models as a whole), four V(B) disclosures (process, risks and limits, factors, fact vs opinion), seven-year records owned by the firm (ch07).
- **VI(A) disclosure**: prominent, plain-language, to clients, prospects and employer; update on change (ch08).
- **VI(B) personal trading toolkit**: three-part test, preclearance, blackout, holdings reports, duplicate confirmations, IPO and private placement limits (ch08).
- **VI(C) referral fees**: disclose before agreement, with nature and estimated value, payer and recipient (ch08).
- **VII(A)/(B)**: exam topics and formulas confidential; no partial designation, expected dates or performance claims (ch08).

**Firm-level**
- **Asset Manager Code**: six principles, sections A-F, binary compliance claim with the "not verified" wording and online notification (ch11).
- **GIPS**: ex ante composites of all actual, fee-paying, discretionary portfolios; TWR with revaluation at large flows `r = Π(1+r_k) − 1`; 5 to 10 years; firm-wide verification only (ch12).

## Scope & Limits
Covers Level I 2020 (R1, R2, R4, R5), Level II 2022 (R47) and Level III 2023 (R32 Guidance for Standards I-VII, R33-R35). Standards of Practice Handbook is the 11th edition; later editions may differ, so check the current curriculum before sitting an exam. GIPS content mixes the 2010 provisions (Level I) and the 2020 edition (Level III); chapter 12 flags where they differ. Source corrections are flagged in ch09, ch10 and ch12. The Handbook's Standards I-VII practice problems are not reproduced.
