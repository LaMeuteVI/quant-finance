# Cheatsheet — CFA Ethics and GIPS

## Decision rules
| When | Do | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Law, firm policy and Code differ | Follow the strictest | I(A) (ch03) |
| Know of a client's or colleague's violation | Escalate internally, document, dissociate | Association equals participation (ch03, ch09) |
| Hold MNPI | No trade, no tip, tell compliance only | II(A) (ch04) |
| Information given for due diligence | Use only for that purpose | Exception to trading ban (ch04) |
| Gift from a broker, issuer or manager | Decline above token | Compromises independence, I(B) (ch03) |
| Gift from a client | Accept only after disclosing to employer | I(B) (ch03) |
| Outside pay that may compete or conflict | Written consent from all parties | IV(B) (ch06) |
| Paid for or paying for a referral | Disclose before agreement, nature and value | VI(C) (ch08) |
| Own trade and client trade same security | Client first, wait for completion | VI(B) (ch08) |
| Recommendation changed | Tell clients who acted on old advice; warn those placing contrary orders | III(B) (ch05) |
| Client asks for unsuitable trade | Discuss, document, update IPS or unmanaged account | III(C) (ch05) |
| Model, vendor or blog supplies the basis | Verify, stress, test whole | V(A) (ch07) |
| Material process or mandate change | Tell all clients and prospects promptly | V(B) (ch07, ch11) |
| You supervise and see a violation | Investigate scope now | IV(C) (ch06, ch10) |
| Referring to CFA status | Facts only, no promises of performance | VII(B) (ch08) |
| Claiming GIPS | All firm-wide or not at all | ch12 |

## Decision tree: which Standard owns the fact?
1. Information misuse: MNPI II(A), client info III(E), employer info IV(A).
2. Personal benefit: gifts I(B), outside pay IV(B), referrals VI(C), own trades VI(B), anything else VI(A).
3. Statements: false or misleading I(C), performance III(D), fact vs opinion V(B).
4. Client treatment: loyalty III(A), fairness III(B), fit III(C).
5. Exam or brand: VII(A) conduct, VII(B) wording.

## Trade-off matrix
| Disclosure alone enough? | Situation |
|---|---|
| Yes | Ownership, directorship, market making, fees, referral, client gifts |
| No | Unfair allocation policy, loyalty breach, MNPI trading, stricter local ban |

## Thresholds and defaults
- Records: at least 7 years if no rule (ch07, ch11).
- Personal holdings disclosure: at hire and at least annually (ch08).
- Referral fee reporting to employer: at least quarterly (ch08).
- Suitability/IPS review: at least annually (ch05).
- GIPS history: 5 years building to 10; dispersion if 6 or more portfolios; reporting within 30 days of quarter end under AMC (ch11, ch12).
- Periods under one year are never annualized (ch12).

## Tells and smells
- Bonus keyed to short-term or quarterly results (ch08, ch10).
- Favored client called before a release (ch05, ch10).
- "Blogger says" or "a source I trust" as the basis (ch07).
- Desk mates able to see client orders (ch09).
- CFA wording with "Level II" or "expected" (ch08).
- Invoice-like "sub-adviser" fees to connected parties (ch09, ch10).

## Exam traps
| Trap | Correct |
|---|---|
| "Fair" equals "equal" | Fair, not equal; premium service allowed if disclosed and open to all (ch05) |
| Client gift needs approval | Disclosure only; employer prior consent is IV(B) (ch03, ch06) |
| Whistleblowing for gain | Never; protect clients or market (ch06) |
| Exam "difficulty" talk | Allowed; topics and formulas not (ch08) |
| Candidate may cite expected charter | Never (ch08) |
| Verification certifies a composite | Firm-wide only (ch12) |
| Partial GIPS compliance | Prohibited (ch12) |
| Mosaic includes one material insider fact | Not mosaic (ch04) |
| IPS only at opening | At least annual and on change (ch05) |
| Pension client is the sponsor | Beneficiaries (ch05) |
| AMC verified by CFA Institute | Unverified claim only (ch02, ch11) |
| VI(C) disclosure at signing | Before any formal agreement (ch08, ch09) |
