# Glossary — CFA Ethics and GIPS

**Agent options** — rights received by a securities firm as compensation for corporate finance work; amount and expiry must be disclosed in research (ch08)
**Applicable law** — the law governing the member's conduct on the facts; where it is stricter than the Code, follow it (ch03, ch09)
**Asset Manager Code (AMC)** — voluntary firm-level code: six General Principles, sections A-F; compliance claim is all-or-nothing (ch02, ch11)
**Beneficial ownership** — direct or indirect pecuniary interest, or the power to vote or dispose of a security; triggers VI(A) disclosure and VI(B) priority rules (ch08, ch10)
**Best execution** — trading process that maximizes portfolio value within objectives and constraints; not simply the lowest commission (ch05, ch11)
**Blackout / restricted period** — window around client trades or publications when insiders may not trade; prevents front-running (ch08, ch11)
**Candidate (CFA Program)** — registrant enrolled for a specified exam or awaiting results; may not imply a partial designation (ch08, ch10)
**Candidate Pledge** — commitment not to give, receive or disclose exam content; violation breaches VII(A) (ch08)
**Cautionary letter** — Professional Conduct Program outcome that is not a disciplinary sanction (ch02)
**Cherry picking** — selecting or moving accounts into or out of composites, or allocating winning trades to a favored account, after the fact (ch03, ch05, ch12)
**Code of Ethics** — six aspirational principles of CFA Institute; applies with the Standards (ch01, ch02)
**Composite** — aggregation of portfolios managed to a similar mandate; return is asset-weighted; GIPS' anti-cherry-picking device (ch12)
**Conflict of interest** — divergence between client, employer and personal interests; avoid, otherwise disclose prominently in plain language (ch08, ch10)
**Custody** — direct or indirect access to client funds; heightens III(A) duties (ch05)
**Directed brokerage** — client-instructed use of a broker; acceptable with disclosure of possible loss of best execution (ch05, ch11)
**Discretion (GIPS)** — manager can implement the intended strategy; restrictions matter only if materially preventing it (ch12)
**Disciplinary Review Committee (DRC)** — volunteer panel that decides violation and sanction if the member rejects charges (ch02)
**Dissociation** — separating from known illegal or unethical activity after raising and escalating; document it (ch03, ch09)
**Duplicate confirmations** — brokers send personal trade confirmations and statements to the employer to verify personal trading (ch08)
**Duty of loyalty** — place the client first; III(A) toward clients, IV(A) toward the employer within client and market limits (ch05, ch06)
**Ethics** — moral principles guiding conduct by balancing self-interest against effects on others (ch01)
**Ethical decision-making framework** — Identify, Consider, Decide and act, Reflect (ch01, ch09)
**Expert networks** — paid outside experts; never ask for or act on confidential information (ch04)
**Fair dealing** — treat clients fairly (not necessarily equally) in recommendations and actions; III(B) (ch05)
**Fiduciary duty** — high standard of care when acting for another party; best-interest rather than suitability (ch01, ch05)
**Firewall (information barrier)** — separation of departments with compliance clearance, lists and no personnel overlap (ch03, ch04)
**Front-running** — trading personal or firm account ahead of client orders using knowledge of them (ch08, ch10)
**Full and fair disclosure** — prominent, plain-language, effective communication of conflicts (ch08)
**GIPS Report** — required presentation for a composite or pooled fund, with 5 growing to 10 years of annual data (ch12)
**GIPS compliance claim** — firm-wide, all-or-nothing; no partial claims or "in accordance with" wording (ch12)
**Internal dispersion** — spread of portfolio returns in a composite; required if 6 or more portfolios held all year (ch12)
**Investment policy statement (IPS)** — written client objectives, constraints and roles; basis for suitability (ch05, ch10)
**Knowingly** — knew or should have known (I(C)) (ch03)
**Large cash flow** — firm-defined threshold per composite at which GIPS require revaluation (ch12)
**Market manipulation** — information-based or transaction-based distortion of price or volume with intent to mislead; II(B) (ch04)
**Material nonpublic information (MNPI)** — information likely to affect price or investor decisions and not disseminated; do not act or cause others to act (ch04)
**Misconduct** — dishonesty, fraud, deceit or acts harming professional reputation, integrity or competence; I(D) (ch03)
**Modified Dietz** — day-weighted approximation of time-weighted return when no revaluation at the flow (ch12)
**Money-weighted return (MWR)** — internal rate of return including client flows; permitted only when the firm controls flows and assets are closed-end or illiquid (ch12)
**Mosaic theory** — lawful aggregation of public and nonmaterial nonpublic facts into a material conclusion; document it (ch04)
**Partial designation** — implied title from passing levels or expected completion date; prohibited by VII(B) (ch08, ch10)
**Performance fee** — fee tied to capital appreciation; disclose to clients and, if paid by a client on top of firm pay, get employer consent (ch08)
**Plagiarism** — presenting others' work, ideas or data as one's own, including oral and lightly reworded material (ch03)
**Portability (GIPS)** — linking a past firm's record requires decision makers, process, records and continuity (ch12)
**Preclearance** — supervisor review of planned personal trades before execution (ch08, ch11)
**Priority of transactions** — client and employer trades before own; VI(B) (ch08)
**Professional Conduct Program (PCP)** — CFA Institute enforcement: inquiry, investigation, charges, DRC, sanction (ch02)
**Professional Conduct Statement (PCS)** — annual member attestation; lapse removes the right to use the designation (ch02, ch08)
**Pump and dump** — promote a security with false information then sell into the inflated price (ch04)
**Pump-priming** — volume incentives at a product launch; acceptable only if disclosed (ch04)
**Record retention** — keep supporting records at least seven years absent a rule; records belong to the firm (ch07, ch11)
**Referral fee** — compensation or benefit paid or received for recommending a service; disclose before agreement, with nature and estimated value (ch08)
**Restricted list / watch list** — compliance lists controlling trading and recommendations on securities connected to confidential information (ch04)
**Soft dollars** — client commissions used to buy research or services; allowed only if they benefit the client (ch05, ch11)
**Standards of Practice Handbook** — CFA Institute guidance and examples for the Code and Standards (11th edition here) (ch02)
**Stricter-rule principle** — comply with whichever of law, employer policy or Code is most restrictive (ch02, ch03)
**Suitability** — recommendations judged against written objectives and constraints in total-portfolio context; III(C) (ch05)
**Supervisor** — anyone with control or influence over others' conduct, whether or not a CFA member; IV(C) (ch06)
**Tie-in arrangement** — IPO allocation conditioned on aftermarket purchases; manipulation and unfair dealing (ch10)
**Time-weighted return (TWR)** — return neutralizing external cash flows; geometric linking of sub-periods (ch12)
**Valuation hierarchy (GIPS)** — preference for observable quoted prices, then lower-level inputs (ch12)
**Verification (GIPS)** — independent firm-wide test of GIPS policies and procedures; not an accuracy guarantee on any composite (ch12)
**Whistleblowing** — disclosure of wrongdoing to protect clients or markets, never for personal gain (ch01, ch06)
