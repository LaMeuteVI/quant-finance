# Cheatsheet — Riguet, Ichimoku Kinko Hyo

## Decision rules
| When | Do | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud oriented up/down | trade only that direction | trend; lines act as S/R (ch04) |
| Cloud flat or repeated twists | range workflow or abstain | lines are equilibrium, not S/R (ch04, ch07) |
| Twist after a long trend | mark the range, wait for break | twist usually precedes a range (ch04) |
| Price signal but Chikou blocked | wait for Chikou to break the obstacle | filter is absolute (ch05) |
| Signal on TF X | check X+1, climb until lines appear | validation and target (ch06) |
| R:R < ~2 | skip or wait pullback | edge depends on ratio (ch02) |
| Price far from Tenkan | do not chase breakout | overbought/oversold (ch03) |
| Price crosses a sloped Kijun | not a break | only flat counts (ch03, ch08) |
| Soulèvement/ressaut + volume at bound | fade at close; stop beyond wick | high ratio (ch07) |
| Lower-TF entry, higher-TF close back through trigger | exit now | higher close prevails (ch07) |
| Stagnation at equilibrium | wait; stop to BE | no signal (ch07) |
| No valid signal anywhere | prepare bullish and bearish plans | patience (ch09) |
| Trend entry near a higher-TF range bound | skip | bound ends trends (ch08) |

## Thresholds and defaults
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Settings | 9-26-52, unchanged (ch03) |
| Risk per trade / per day | 1% / 2% (ch02) |
| Min R:R | ~2:1 (about 1.9 tolerated) (ch02) |
| Break-even win rate | 1/(1+R): 2:1 → 33%, 4:1 → 20% (ch02) |
| Prior holds before trading a flat Kijun | 2 (ch08) |
| TF ladder | M15, H1, H4, D, W, M (ch06) |
| Stop move after entry | none; 5–6 points exceptional (ch02) |
| Stop for CAD, NZD, gold, oil | wider (ch10) |
| Range share of time | ~70% (author's estimate) (ch04) |

## Trade-offs
| Setup | Ratio | Win rate | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Range fade | highest | high with filter | needs bounds and volume (ch07) |
| Kijun pullback | ~2 | medium | needs two holds (ch08) |
| Breakout | ~2 | medium | needs close and no stretch (ch08) |

## Tells and smells
- Entering "just this once" without Chikou check (ch05)
- Tight stop out of fear of missing the move (ch10)
- Candle patterns read mid-range (ch07)
- Empty higher TF taken as confirmation (ch06)
- Flats used as S/R in a range (ch03, ch07)
