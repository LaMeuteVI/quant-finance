# Patterns — Riguet, Ichimoku Kinko Hyo

## Compute the five lines
**When to use**: verify a platform or code it. **How**: Tenkan = (max H + min L)/2 over 9; Kijun same over 26; SSA = (Tenkan+Kijun)/2 plotted +26; SSB = midpoint over 52 plotted +26; Chikou = close plotted −26. **Trade-offs**: the cloud under bar t was computed at t−26. (ch03)

## First look at a chart
**When to use**: every chart. **How**: 1) cloud orientation (up, down, flat); 2) if oriented, mark flats and extend them; 3) check price distance to Tenkan; 4) check Chikou obstacles; 5) decide trend or range workflow. **Trade-offs**: cloud lags new trends. (ch04, ch05)

## Chikou validation
**When to use**: before any entry. **How**: 1) price signal; 2) list obstacles ahead of Chikou (flats, cloud, candles, trend lines); 3) free: valid; blocked: wait for Chikou to break it. **Trade-offs**: not applied to flats in a range. (ch05)

## Multi-timeframe validation and target
**When to use**: after a signal. **How**: 1) climb one TF at a time; 2) check obstacles for price and Chikou; 3) continue until lines appear; 4) trace extended flats and alignments as targets; 5) go down for timing; 6) check R:R ≥ 2. **Trade-offs**: empty frame is not confirmation. (ch06)

## Range fade after rejection
**When to use**: flat cloud, price at a bound. **How**: 1) box with closes; 2) wait for soulèvement/ressaut with strong volume; 3) enter at the candle close; 4) stop beyond the wick; 5) target just before the opposite bound. **Trade-offs**: no trade without rejection; volume is broker tick volume. (ch07)

## Equilibrium management
**When to use**: price reaches mid-range. **How**: wait for closes; reversal candles: BE or cash in; continuation candles: BE and hold. **Trade-offs**: patterns valid only at bounds and equilibrium. (ch07)

## Lower-TF anticipation with higher-close rule
**When to use**: improve entry ratio. **How**: 1) draw the higher-TF level; 2) enter on a lower-TF close after rejection; 3) check each higher-TF close; 4) exit at once if a higher close is back through the trigger. **Trade-offs**: small losses accepted. (ch07)

## Pullback on flat Kijun
**When to use**: oriented cloud. **How**: 1) two prior holds of the flat; 2) Chikou free; 3) enter at the close; 4) stop under flat Kijun and trend line; 5) target upper channel bound; 6) require R:R ≥ ~2. **Trade-offs**: Kijun is sometimes overrun. (ch08)

## Breakout of swing extreme
**When to use**: oriented cloud, close beyond horizontal resistance. **How**: 1) close beyond; 2) stop below last lows/reversal body; 3) target channel top; 4) skip if R:R < 2; 5) no lower-TF anticipation. **Trade-offs**: skip if price is stretched from Tenkan. (ch08)

## Pyramiding with stop ratchet
**When to use**: persistent trend with Kijun respected. **How**: add at each pullback to the next flat Kijun, move older stops to the new stop, exit all on the opposite Kijun break. **Trade-offs**: not usable when Kijun is overrun. (ch08)

## Top-down plan building
**When to use**: no live signal. **How**: 1) weekly flats traced; 2) copy to lower TFs; 3) delete levels inside ranges; 4) set triggers, stops, targets for long and short; 5) wait. **Trade-offs**: patience, plans may never trigger. (ch09)

## Position sizing
**When to use**: every order. **How**: stop from structure; size = capital × 1% ÷ stop pips; daily loss max 2%. **Trade-offs**: wide-stop assets need small size. (ch02)
