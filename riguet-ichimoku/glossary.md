# Glossary — Riguet, Ichimoku Kinko Hyo

**Abstention** — staying out when no signal is validated or R:R < 2; a valid, frequent outcome (ch01, ch09)
**Alignment of flats** — several flats (Tenkan, Kijun, SSA, SSB) at the same price; the more lines, the stronger the S/R and target (ch04, ch06)
**Aggressive entry** — entry anticipated on a lower TF to improve the ratio; never waives rules (ch02, ch07)
**Bearish/bullish scenario** — both prepared in a plan, none favoured (ch01, ch09)
**Borne haute / borne basse** — upper / lower bound of a range rectangle, drawn through closes/bodies (ch07)
**Break even (BE)** — stop moved to entry price (ch02, ch07, ch08)
**Candle-close confirmation** — a signal exists only at the close, never intra-candle (ch05, ch06, ch08)
**Channel (canal)** — trend line plus parallel upper bound; upper bound is the usual target in trends (ch08)
**Chikou span (lagging span)** — close of the current bar plotted 26 bars back; the filter of the system; closes only (ch03, ch05)
**Chikou filter rule** — a price signal is valid only if Chikou is free of obstacles in the trade direction (ch05)
**Cloud (Kumo)** — area between Senkou span A and B; its orientation defines trend vs range (ch04)
**Cloud compass** — read cloud orientation first on every chart (ch04)
**Complete / partial rotation** — price moves bound to bound in a range / reverses at the equilibrium (ch07)
**Daily loss cap** — 2% of capital per day (ch02)
**Elastic effect (effet élastique)** — a flat Kijun attracts distant price like a magnet (50% retracement) (ch03)
**Equilibrium zone (zone d'équilibre)** — middle of a range where Ichimoku lines gather (ch07)
**Extension of a flat (plat en extension)** — horizontal projection of an old flat to the right; future S/R (ch03, ch06)
**Flat (plat)** — horizontal stretch of Tenkan, Kijun, SSA or SSB; the only part acting as S/R, in trends only (ch03)
**Higher TF close prevails** — if a higher-TF close contradicts a lower-TF entry, exit immediately (ch07)
**Ichimoku Kinko Hyo** — "equilibrium chart at a glance", attributed to Goichi Hosoda (ch03)
**Kijun-sen (base line)** — midpoint of highest high and lowest low over 26 bars; medium-term equilibrium (ch03)
**Kumo twist** — SSA crosses SSB; usually prelude to a range, not a reversal (ch04)
**Marubozu** — long-bodied candle with almost no wicks; preferred breakout candle (ch06, ch08)
**Momentum (sloped line)** — a sloped Tenkan or Kijun shows trend strength; crossing it is not a break (ch03, ch08)
**Obstacle** — anything blocking price or Chikou: flats, cloud, candles, trend lines (ch05)
**Overbought / oversold (surachat / survente)** — price far from Tenkan; do not chase, wait for pullback (ch03, ch09)
**Pip value / size** — money per pip; size = capital × risk% ÷ stop pips (ch02)
**Plan de trading (trading plan)** — pre-analysed trigger, stop and targets for bullish and bearish cases (ch09)
**Pointe / right angle** — corner where an Ichimoku flat meets a slope; strong S/R and trap (ch04, ch06)
**Pullback / throwback** — return to a broken level or flat Kijun (ch08)
**Pyramiding (pyramidage)** — adding at each pullback on the next flat Kijun, raising prior stops (ch08)
**R:R (risk/reward)** — target distance ÷ stop distance; minimum about 2:1 (ch02)
**Range (dérive latérale)** — flat/twisting cloud; sideways market; lines become equilibrium (ch04, ch07)
**Ressaut (spring)** — wick below the lower bound closing back above it; buy signal with volume (ch07)
**Risk per trade** — 1% of capital (ch02)
**Senkou span A (SSA)** — (Tenkan + Kijun)/2 plotted 26 bars ahead; faster cloud edge (ch03, ch04)
**Senkou span B (SSB)** — midpoint over 52 bars plotted 26 bars ahead; slower edge with strongest flats (ch03, ch04)
**Settings 9-26-52** — default windows; keep them since the crowd uses them (ch03)
**Soulèvement (upthrust)** — wick above the upper bound closing back below it; sell signal with volume (ch07)
**Stop at invalidation** — stop placed where the signal is invalidated, size adapts (ch01, ch08)
**Sub-trend in range** — tradeable channel inside a range with known start/end (ch07)
**Tenkan-sen (conversion line)** — 9-bar midpoint; short-term equilibrium, hugs price (ch03)
**Tick volume** — broker transaction count used to confirm rejection; contested but used by author (ch07)
**Time frame (UT, unité de temps)** — chart period; ladder M15, H1, H4, D, W, M (ch06)
**Trigger level (seuil de déclenchement)** — level whose close beyond fires a plan (ch09)
**Wick risk (mèches assassines)** — spikes on CAD pairs, NZD crosses, gold, oil; need wider stops (ch02, ch10)
