## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Computation (ch03).** Tenkan-sen = (highest high + lowest low)/2 over 9 bars; Kijun-sen same over 26; Senkou span A (SSA) = (Tenkan + Kijun)/2 plotted 26 bars ahead; Senkou span B (SSB) = 52-bar midpoint plotted 26 ahead; Chikou span = close plotted 26 back. Cloud (Kumo) = band between SSA and SSB. Keep 9-26-52: the levels work because the crowd uses them.

**Discipline first (ch01).** Execute in cold blood: signal, stop at invalidation, structural target, then hands off. No signal means no trade. Prepare bullish and bearish plans instead of opinions.

**Money management (ch02).** Risk 1% per trade, 2% per day. Size = capital × risk% ÷ stop pips. Enter only if R:R ≥ about 2:1. Break-even win rate = 1/(1+R). Never move the stop after entry.

**Cloud compass (ch04).** Read cloud orientation first: up means longs only, down means shorts only, flat or twisting means range. A twist after a long trend usually leads to a range, not a reversal.

**Flats are the levels (ch03, ch04).** Only horizontal portions of Tenkan, Kijun, SSA, SSB are support/resistance, and only in trends. Extend them to the right; longer flats and alignments of several lines are stronger; Kijun flats beat Tenkan flats. A sloped line is momentum, not a level.

**Distance from Tenkan (ch03, ch09).** Price far from Tenkan means overbought/oversold: do not chase breakouts, wait for a pullback to a flat Kijun (elastic effect, 50% retracement).

**Chikou filter (ch05).** A price signal is valid only if Chikou is free of obstacles (flats, cloud, candles, trend lines) in the trade direction. If blocked, wait for Chikou to break the obstacle. In a range, lines do not block.

**Multi-timeframe loop (ch06).** Detect on TF X, climb one TF at a time to check obstacles for price and Chikou, keep climbing until lines appear, trace extended flats and alignments as targets, descend to refine timing. Enter only at candle close. An empty frame is not confirmation.

**Range trading (ch07).** Flat cloud: box the range with closes. Sell a soulèvement (wick above the upper bound, close back inside) and buy a ressaut (wick below the lower bound, close back inside), with strong volume. Enter at the close, stop beyond the wick, target just before the opposite bound. At the equilibrium: reversal candles mean BE or cash in; continuation candles mean BE and hold. Golden rule: the higher-TF close always prevails over the lower-TF close.

**Trend trading (ch08).** Oriented cloud only. Pullback to a flat Kijun that held twice, or close beyond a swing extreme; stop at invalidation (flat Kijun plus trend line, or last lows); target the upper channel bound; skip if R:R < 2 or a higher-TF range bound is just ahead. Pyramid at each new flat Kijun with a stop ratchet; exit on the opposite Kijun break.

**Plans (ch09).** Top-down: trace weekly flats, copy to lower TFs, drop levels inside ranges, set trigger, stop, chain of targets, wait. Scan 20 to 25 assets to find one valid signal, abstain otherwise.

**Lessons (ch10).** Violent instruments (CAD pairs, NZD crosses, gold, oil) need wider stops. A loss from an emotional tight stop is the real error, not a loss within the plan.
