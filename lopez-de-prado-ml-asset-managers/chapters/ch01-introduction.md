# Chapter 1: Introduction

> López de Prado, *Machine Learning for Asset Managers*, Cambridge UP 2020, Chapter 1.

## Core Idea

This short companion volume to *AFML* (2018) introduces six problems of financial ML: (1) **the need for ML is greater** (the volume of financial data is growing exponentially), (2) **the structure of financial data is unique** (information-driven, not time-driven), (3) **the math is the easy part** (the institutional, operational, and regulatory challenges are harder), (4) **ML systems require a holistic approach** (data, features, model, evaluation, execution), (5) **the most important ML model is the loss function** (the loss encodes the user's preferences), (6) **backtest overfitting is the central danger** (DSR, PBO, MinTRL are the institutional discipline).

## Key Concepts

- **The five sources of investment edge** — informational, analytical, computational, structural, psychological.
- **The 4 types of ML** — supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, reinforcement.
- **The 5 components of an ML system** — data, features, model, evaluation, execution.
- **The role of the loss function** — encodes the user's preferences.
- **The overfitting problem** — the central danger; the institutional response is DSR/PBO/MinTRL.

## Key Takeaways

1. ML is necessary but not sufficient; the institutional and operational challenges are harder.
2. The loss function is the most important ML model.
3. The DSR/PBO/MinTRL discipline is essential.
