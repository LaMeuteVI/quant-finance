# Chapter 7: Bitcoins

> Chan, *Machine Trading*, Chapter 7. Crypto trading: the unique aspects.

## Core Idea

**Bitcoin and crypto trading** have unique features: (1) **24/7 trading**, (2) **high vol** (~80% annualised), (3) **no shorting constraint** in some markets, (4) **exchange-specific** (different prices on different exchanges), (5) **regulatory uncertainty**. The **statistical arbitrage** between exchanges and the **momentum / mean-reversion** intraday are the institutional strategies.

## Key Concepts

- **Bitcoin** — the original crypto; 21M cap; halving every 4 years.
- **Crypto exchanges** — Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, OKX, Bitfinex; each with its own order book.
- **24/7 trading** — no market close; weekend and holiday trading.
- **High volatility** — annualised vol 60-100% historically.
- **Stat arb across exchanges** — the same crypto at different prices; the institutional opportunity.
- **Stablecoin** — a crypto pegged to a fiat currency (USDC, USDT, DAI).
- **DeFi** — Decentralised Finance; smart contracts on a blockchain.
- **Halving** — the Bitcoin supply event every ~4 years; historically a price catalyst.
- **Mining** — the proof-of-work consensus; the source of new Bitcoin.
- **On-chain analysis** — analysing the Bitcoin blockchain (Glassnode, CryptoQuant).
- **Funding rate** — the periodic fee paid between longs and shorts in perpetual futures.

## Key Takeaways

1. Crypto is 24/7, high-vol, exchange-specific.
2. Stat arb across exchanges is the most consistent opportunity.
3. On-chain analysis is the institutional data source.
