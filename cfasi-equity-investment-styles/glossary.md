# Glossary — CFA Society Italy Equity Investment Styles Guides

**Active share** — sum of absolute weight differences from the benchmark, halved; discretionary funds run higher active share than systematic ones (ch 09, 25)
**Ageing Population (theme)** — index of companies with at least 50% of revenue tied to demographic ageing; also the longevity-risk hedge (ch 13, 21)
**A-shares** — yuan-denominated mainland Chinese shares, added to MSCI EM via Stock Connect in 2017; about 5% of the index, heading toward 15% (ch 23)
**ATVR (annualised traded value ratio)** — MSCI liquidity test: 15% for emerging, 20% for developed (ch 22)
**Availability cascade (ease of recall)** — bias where ideas repeated in media or conversation seem more plausible (ch 11)
**Black box (myth)** — belief that quant models are unintelligible; inputs and rules are in fact explicit (ch 09)
**Blue / grey / green hydrogen** — fossil with carbon capture / fossil without capture (about $1-1.5 per kg) / electrolysis with renewable power; about 99% is still fossil-based (ch 20)
**Book-to-market (B/M)** — inverse of P/B; the Fama-French value sort variable (ch 03, 06)
**Carhart four-factor model** — Fama-French three factors plus prior 12-month momentum (PR1YR) (ch 06)
**Carry** — return from holding higher-yielding assets; a factor in bonds, currencies and commodities (ch 08, 10)
**Carbon Earnings at Risk** — S&P Trucost measure simulating carbon-price scenarios to show profit at risk (ch 18)
**Catalyst (value with)** — event that forces the market to re-rate a cheap stock; cheapness alone is not enough (ch 05)
**Climate leader / laggard** — transition score above 50 / below 50; above 75 flags opportunity (ch 18)
**Cognitive dissonance** — discomfort when evidence contradicts a held belief, leading to avoidance of new information (ch 11)
**Complementary combination** — fund mix of enhanced index, intermediate and high-active decorrelated manager (ch 25)
**Composite score** — weighted sum of sector-relative factor scores used to rank stocks (ch 05, 12)
**Confirmation bias** — seeking evidence that supports a position already wanted (ch 11)
**Correlation of excess returns** — correlation of returns net of the benchmark; the right input for combining funds within a theme (ch 08, 21)
**Crowding** — too many models or investors on the same anomaly, causing correlated exits (ch 05, 10)
**CTA (trend following)** — systematic futures strategy buying breakouts and selling breakdowns; 30-50% of trades profitable, winners large (ch 10)
**Data mining** — finding patterns present only in the sample; the main source of the factor zoo (ch 06)
**De-biasing** — methods that reduce behavioural errors: education, advice, self-awareness, automation (ch 11)
**Decoupling (factor weighting)** — breaking the link between index weight and price or debt outstanding (ch 08)
**Deep value** — strict Graham/Piotroski criteria yielding few, concentrated stocks (ch 05)
**Defensive stock (Graham)** — stock passing prudence criteria on size, balance sheet and record, not a defensive sector (ch 01)
**Direct vs indirect multiple** — yield-type ratios (higher better) vs price-over-quantity ratios (lower better) (ch 02)
**Disposition effect** — selling winners early and keeping losers (ch 10, 11)
**Diversified Risk Weighted (DRW)** — smart-beta weights proportional to 1/sigma, assuming equal correlations (ch 06)
**Dividend yield / payout ratio** — DPS/price; DPS/EPS (ch 02)
**Downside deviation** — dispersion of returns below a target; denominator of Sortino (ch 13)
**Earnings yield** — EPS/price, inverse of P/E; also years-to-recover-price at constant earnings (ch 01, 02)
**Edge AI / NPU** — AI inference run locally on a device with a neural processing unit (ch 17)
**Electrolyser / fuel cell** — splits water into hydrogen and oxygen / recombines them into electricity and water (ch 20)
**Emerging market (MSCI)** — country classed by MSCI on development, size and liquidity, and accessibility; 27 countries in 2021 (ch 22)
**EMBI / GBI-EM / CEMBI** — J.P. Morgan indices for hard-currency sovereign, local-currency sovereign and hard-currency corporate EM debt (ch 22, 24)
**Enhanced index** — fund with low tracking error and modest alpha target (ch 25)
**Equal Risk Contribution (ERC)** — smart-beta weights equalising each stock's risk contribution; risk parity (ch 06, 10)
**Equity duration** — effective duration of equity cash flows, about 8-12 years; longer for growth (ch 03)
**EV/EBITDA** — enterprise value over EBITDA; debt-aware indirect multiple (ch 02)
**Excess return (fund)** — fund minus benchmark return (ch 25)
**Expected duration of a regime** — 1/(1 - P_ii) quarters (ch 07)
**Factor** — systematic return driver shared by many securities (ch 06)
**Factor mix** — equal-weight combination of complementary factor indices (Value, Quality, Momentum, Minimum Volatility) (ch 08)
**Factor zoo** — proliferation of published, often spurious, factors (Cochrane) (ch 06)
**Fallen angel** — bond downgraded from investment grade to high yield, often cheap after forced selling (ch 06)
**Fama-French three- / five-factor model** — market, SMB, HML; adds RMW and CMA in 2015 (ch 03, 06)
**Five Pillars due diligence** — Parent, Process, People, Price, Performance (ch 08, 25)
**Floor value** — intrinsic value seen as a minimum the market will recognise (ch 02)
**Goldilocks regime** — rising growth with decelerating inflation; the most persistent regime (ch 07)
**Graham valuation formula** — V = E x (8.5 + 2g) x 4.4 / Y (ch 01)
**Greenblatt Magic Formula** — rank sum of earnings yield (EBIT/EV) and return on capital; buy top 30 of the largest 2,500 (ch 10)
**Hard-currency vs local-currency debt** — issued in USD/EUR vs domestic currency; FX is the main local risk (ch 22, 24)
**Heating Up regime** — accelerating inflation with rising growth; highest market return (ch 07)
**HML / SMB** — high-minus-low book-to-market and small-minus-big return factors (ch 03, 06)
**Hybrid process** — fundamental process using quantitative screens or scores (ch 10)
**Information ratio** — active return over tracking error (ch 08, 09, 25)
**Intrinsic value** — worth of a business from assets, earnings power and prospects, independent of price (ch 01, 02)
**Investable vs academic factor** — long-only, liquid, capped-turnover index tilt vs long/short pure portfolio (ch 08)
**Investor vs speculator** — investor values the business; speculator forecasts the price (ch 01)
**KIID** — key investor document stating objectives and constraints; check before ranking funds (ch 25)
**Loss aversion / regret aversion** — losses weigh more than equal gains; fear of regretting a sale or a hold (ch 10, 11)
**Low-volatility drift** — low-vol products gaining tech weight (about 15% vs 6%), changing the defensive label (ch 08)
**Machine learning / deep learning** — systems learning from data; layered neural networks (ch 17)
**Maintenance capex** — spending needed to keep current capacity; subtracted in owner earnings (ch 04)
**Mandate change** — change in a fund's strategy that invalidates its track record (ch 25)
**Margin of safety** — gap between intrinsic value and price, 1 - P/V; rule of thumb 20-33% (ch 01, 02)
**Markov-Switching model** — regime model with state-dependent mean and covariance (ch 07)
**Moore's Law** — transistor count doubling about every two years (ch 14)
**Mr. Market** — Graham's moody partner offering daily prices (ch 01)
**Nifty Fifty** — high-quality US stocks at very high P/Es in the early 1970s; lost about 60% in ten years (ch 01)
**Overprice (sovraprezzo)** — price minus book value, recovered through book growth and dividends (ch 03)
**Owner earnings** — NI + D&A +/- non-cash - maintenance capex +/- working capital (ch 04)
**P/B = (ROE - g)/(K - g)** — justified price-to-book (ch 03)
**Perez technological cycle** — irruption, frenzy, turning point, synergy, maturity; guides value-trap diagnosis (ch 03)
**Physical / transition / liability risk** — climate risk triad (ch 18)
**Piotroski F-score** — nine binary quality signals, 0-9, buy 8-9 and short 0-1 among cheap stocks (ch 05, 10)
**PRI** — UN Principles for Responsible Investment; supplies ESG factor lists for sovereign and corporate credit (ch 24)
**Quality (QMJ)** — profitable, growing, safe, well-managed firms (ch 06)
**Quantamental** — fundamental analysis augmented with large-scale quantitative data (ch 05)
**Range anxiety** — fear of insufficient EV range; 300 miles is the replacement threshold (ch 19)
**Regret aversion** — see Loss aversion (ch 11)
**Return per unit of duration** — yield or spread efficiency measure for EM debt (ch 24)
**RMW / CMA** — robust-minus-weak profitability; conservative-minus-aggressive investment (ch 06)
**Risk parity** — allocation by risk contribution, w proportional to 1/sigma (ch 06, 10)
**Robo-advisor** — algorithmic advice platform; weak in crises and suitability (ch 17)
**Sector-specific multiple** — choosing the valuation ratio by backtest per sector (ch 05)
**Sharpe / Sortino ratio** — excess return over volatility / over downside deviation (ch 13)
**Slowing Growth regime** — falling growth and decelerating inflation; highest volatility (ch 07)
**Smart beta** — rules-based factor index between passive and active (ch 06, 08)
**Solid-state battery** — lithium-metal-anode battery with more than twice the energy density, next decade (ch 19)
**Stagflation regime** — falling growth with accelerating inflation; worst for the market (ch 07)
**Statistical arbitrage** — market-neutral convergence bet on co-moving securities (ch 10)
**Statistical factor vs investment style** — Fama-French sort vs Graham/Lynch style; stock lists can differ (ch 12)
**Stock Connect** — link between mainland China and Hong Kong exchanges (ch 23)
**Stop loss / profit target** — price levels fixed ex ante at which to cut a loss or bank a gain (ch 10, 11)
**Systematic investing** — decisions by pre-defined rules, models and data (ch 09)
**Theme** — structural growth trend crossing sectors and countries (ch 13, 21)
**TINA** — "there is no alternative", low rates pushing investors into equities (ch 03)
**Tracking error** — volatility of active return versus the benchmark (ch 08, 09, 25)
**Transition-risk score** — 0-100 style score of low-carbon exposure (MSCI scale 0-10) (ch 18)
**Value premium** — excess return of low-P/B over high-P/B stocks, about 4.7% a year over 1928-2018 (ch 03)
**Value trap** — cheap multiple masking collapsing ROE, high leverage or a shrinking industry (ch 03, 04, 05)
**Value x profitability matrix** — cheap and profitable earned the best monthly return across regions (ch 03)
**Wellness (theme)** — defensive health-related theme, beta 0.58, best VaR (ch 13)
