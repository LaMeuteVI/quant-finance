# Glossary — CFA Society Italy × Advisor Private Portfolio Construction Guides

**Ability vs willingness to take risk** — objective capacity (income, assets, horizon) vs subjective appetite; the lower binds (ch04)
**Absolute-return fund** — UCITS fund aiming at low equity correlation and positive 12-month returns via derivatives; judge over about 3 years (ch15)
**Active share** — degree to which holdings differ from the benchmark; meaningful only for long-only equity (ch06, ch11)
**AIF / AIFMD** — any fund that is not a UCITS; the directive regulates the manager (authorization, depositary, valuation, leverage) (ch16)
**Alpha, pure** — manager return beyond the natural benchmark of its persistent style; the input of manager optimization (ch13)
**Alpha risk / alpha-risk aversion** — risk that an active manager lags its passive counterpart; Vanguard VAAM prices it separately from market risk (ch02, ch03)
**Alternative risk premia** — rule-based long-short value, quality, momentum, carry premia across asset classes, packaged as UCITS ETFs (ch12)
**Attribution (Brinson-Fachler)** — active return = allocation + selection + interaction by segment (ch03)
**Behaviour gap** — difference between fund return and what the investor earned because of timing and plan deviations (ch04, ch11)
**Benchmark validity (seven properties)** — unambiguous, investable, measurable, appropriate, reflective of current views, specified in advance, accepted (ch03)
**Best-in-class** — selects top ESG scorers per sector, keeping sector weights near the parent (ch20, ch23)
**Black-Litterman** — blends market-implied equilibrium returns with views weighted by confidence; no views means the market portfolio (ch02, ch03)
**Blind pool** — new private fund with no portfolio yet; secondaries avoid most of it (ch17, ch18)
**Calmar ratio** — annualised return over maximum drawdown (ch03)
**Capital call / unfunded commitment** — promised capital not yet drawn; follows the stake in a secondary sale (ch17, ch18)
**Carbon footprint** — attributable emissions per EUR million invested; WACI is per EUR million revenue (ch27)
**Cash equitization** — using futures on cash awaiting distribution to stay fully exposed (ch06)
**Club deal / investment club** — deal-by-deal syndication with opt-in per deal; lower fees, higher selection risk (ch15, ch19)
**Co-investment** — GP-syndicated deal alongside a fund; lower cost, moral hazard on deal size (ch19)
**Cognitive vs emotional bias** — information-processing errors (moderate them) vs feeling-driven decisions (adapt the plan) (ch04)
**Component VaR** — additive decomposition of VaR by factor; ESG share about 5.5% in the guide's test (ch29)
**Core-satellite** — passive core (typically 60-80% of liquid assets) plus active satellites; frees cost and risk budget (ch02, ch06, ch10)
**Counterparty risk (swap)** — loss of unrealised swap gain on issuer default; capped by UCITS 10% rule, collateral, daily reset (ch06, ch07)
**CPPI / constant priority** — risky exposure = m × (value − floor); protects a floor, whipsaws in range markets (ch02)
**DNF (Dichiarazione non finanziaria)** — Italian non-financial statement under Legislative Decree 254/2016 for public-interest entities above size thresholds (ch26)
**Denominator effect** — fall in listed assets pushes the private-equity weight above its cap (Harvard 2008) (ch17)
**Direct Alpha** — IRR of cash flows compounded at index returns; annualised excess return over the index (ch17)
**Disintermediation** — investors build in-house direct investing teams instead of paying fund fees (ch19)
**Drag-along / tag-along** — majority right to force a sale / minority right to sell on the same terms (ch28)
**DPI / RVPI / TVPI** — distributions, residual NAV, total value over paid-in capital; TVPI = DPI + RVPI (ch17)
**Dry powder** — raised but uninvested capital; signals buyer capacity in secondaries (ch18)
**Efficient frontier of managers** — minimum active risk per level of pure alpha, with index funds at the origin (ch13)
**Engagement** — structured investor dialogue with investees plus voting; value rests on sustainability analysis (ch30)
**ESG integration** — systematic inclusion of ESG factors in financial analysis and valuation (ch20)
**ESG momentum** — direction of change of ESG scores as an early-warning signal (ch23)
**ESG risk (RESG)** — entropy-based uncertainty of a portfolio's ESG profile, distinct from its average score (ch29)
**ETC / ETN** — commodity or note products; not funds and not the same as ETFs (ch06, ch10)
**Evergreen fund** — closed-end structure with perpetual life; judged marginal by the guide's panel (ch15)
**Factor mixing / multi-factor** — combining factors with complementary cycles; timing alpha disappears net of turnover (ch08)
**Factor zoo** — proliferation of weak or data-mined factors; require rationale, persistence, investability (ch08)
**FAR (fees for active risk)** — management fee divided by tracking error volatility; lower is better, check at portfolio level (ch10)
**Feeder fund / wrapper** — pooled vehicle investing in one master fund / structure adding liquidity services (ch19)
**Five Ps** — People, Process, Performance, Parent, Price (Morningstar Analyst Rating pillars) (ch11)
**Fund mortality** — mergers and closures that remove laggards from track records, inflating averages (ch11)
**Funding risk** — inability to honour capital calls; default can forfeit paid-in capital (ch17)
**Global market portfolio** — market-value-weighted portfolio of all investable classes; null hypothesis for deviations (ch02)
**Greenwashing** — presenting marginal initiatives as sustainability performance (ch25)
**GSIA seven strategies** — exclusion, best-in-class, norms-based, thematic, impact, integration, engagement (ch20)
**Haircut** — discount applied to collateral value (ch07)
**Information ratio** — active return over tracking error volatility (ch03, ch14)
**IPS** — Investment Policy Statement: objectives, constraints, horizon, benchmark, rebalancing (ch02, ch04)
**IRR** — money-weighted rate; non-additive and inflated by short holdings; read with multiples (ch17)
**KS-PME** — future value of distributions over future value of contributions at index returns; above 1 beats the index (ch17)
**Liquid alternatives** — hedge-fund-style strategies under UCITS with daily/weekly liquidity (ch09, ch15)
**LDI / ALM** — liability-driven investing; joint asset-liability management (ch01, ch02)
**Look-through** — transparency of underlying holdings and factor exposures (ch08)
**Materiality (SASB)** — ESG information whose trend likely affects operating performance in an industry (ch21, ch25, ch26)
**Maximum drawdown** — worst peak-to-trough loss; test as drawdown over standard deviation, flag above 2-2.5 (ch14)
**Misfit risk** — standard deviation of natural minus declared benchmark; style mismatch risk (ch13)
**Modified Dietz** — money-weighted return approximation with day-weighted flows (ch04)
**MRR** — joint relative-risk indicator combining relative VaR and ESG risk (ch29)
**Multimanager portfolio** — managers treated as securities in active-risk and alpha space (ch13)
**Natural benchmark** — best-fit style index from regression of manager returns (ch13)
**Negative screening / exclusion** — removing sectors or companies on ESG criteria; largest strategy (USD 15tn) (ch20, ch22)
**Norms-based screening** — exclusion by breach of OECD, UN or ILO norms (ch20)
**Optimized sampling** — physical replication minimising expected tracking error using covariances (ch07)
**Overcommitment ratio** — commitments versus available resources; typically 105-115% (ch17)
**Over/underweight ESG index** — parent constituents with weights tilted to ESG ratings; no exclusions (ch23)
**PME (public market equivalent)** — restates index returns in money-weighted logic; KS-PME, PME+, mPME (ch17)
**PRI** — UN Principles for Responsible Investment, created 2005 (ch21)
**Plateau optimization** — combining several optimisation solutions to avoid fragile single-point optima (ch14)
**Portfolio Sustainability Score** — Morningstar asset-weighted ESG score less controversy deduction; 50% coverage minimum (ch24)
**Product governance (MiFID II)** — producer defines the target market, distributor verifies the client fits (ch16)
**Quantsheet** — one-page statistical dossier of a fund (beta, R², TE, IR, drawdown, capture) (ch14)
**Ramo I / Ramo III** — Italian guaranteed life policies / unit-linked policies (ch01)
**Reverse enquiry** — unsolicited investor request, outside the EU definition of marketing (ch16)
**Reverse optimization** — implied returns backed out of market weights (ch03)
**Risk Appetite Framework (RAF)** — strategy, appetite, soft limit, hard limit, capacity with pre-agreed actions (ch01)
**Risk parity** — weights set so each asset contributes equal risk; often needs leverage (ch01, ch03)
**Rebalancing band / calendar** — tolerance-based vs date-based restoring of weights (ch02, ch03)
**SASB / SICS** — US sector-specific sustainability standards and their industry classification (ch21, ch26)
**Science-based targets (SBT)** — emission targets aligned with climate science (ch30)
**SDGs** — 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals to 2030 (ch27)
**Secondary market (private equity)** — OTC trading of LP stakes, priced as % of last NAV (ch18)
**Securities lending** — fund lends holdings for fee against 102-105% collateral; can offset up to about 90% of a small-cap ETF fee (ch06, ch07)
**Sharpe's arithmetic** — average active manager earns market return before costs and trails after them (ch05, ch10)
**Smart beta** — rules-based non-cap-weighted index tilts to factors (ch08)
**Solvency II ratio** — Own Funds divided by SCR (ch01)
**SPIVA** — scorecard of active funds underperforming benchmarks; failure rises with horizon (ch05)
**Stratified sampling** — physical replication by strata of sector, style, region (ch07)
**Stranded assets** — reserves that lose value under climate constraints (ch20)
**Swing pricing / clean NAV** — mechanisms charging dealing costs to transacting investors (ch06)
**Synthetic (funded/unfunded) ETF** — swap-based replication; funded holds cash and external collateral, unfunded holds a substitute basket (ch06, ch07)
**TCFD** — climate disclosure task force; metrics WACI, attributable emissions, footprint (ch27)
**TCO (total cost of ownership)** — recurring (TER, sampling, swap, tax drag less lending) plus transaction costs (ch06, ch12)
**Tracking difference vs tracking error** — realised return gap vs its volatility; low TEV can hide steady drag (ch06, ch12)
**TWRR vs MWRR** — time-weighted (manager) vs money-weighted (investor) return (ch04, ch11)
**UCITS** — EU retail fund framework with eligible-asset, diversification and liquidity rules (ch16)
**UVaR** — undiversified VaR, sum of stand-alone VaRs (ch29)
**VAAM** — Vanguard Asset Allocation Model, utility-based with alpha-risk aversion; vendor method (ch02, ch03)
**Valuation lag** — private NAVs arrive 1-6 months late, about 4 on average (ch17)
**WACI** — weighted average carbon intensity, tCO2e per EUR million revenue (ch27)
**Yale model** — contribution rate, distribution rate and growth projecting private-fund cash flows (ch17)
**Z-score (peer)** — standardised deviation versus peer group, used in score-based selection (ch14)
