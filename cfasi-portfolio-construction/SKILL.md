## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Asset allocation**
- **Classify the investor**: liability-driven (ALM), asset-only, or goal-based; the class fixes risk appetite. Write the IPS before choosing weights (ch02, ch04).
- **Insurer allocation** is value x risk x capital under Solvency II: Solvency ratio = Own Funds / SCR; RAF levels (appetite, soft limit, hard limit, capacity) trigger pre-agreed actions; optimum comes from joint stochastic asset-liability simulation, not Markowitz (ch01).
- **Top-down SAA then bounded TAA**: Markowitz is unstable, so use constraints or Black-Litterman (market equilibrium plus views weighted by confidence); TAA bands plus a relative VaR cap (ch02, ch03).
- **Benchmark = SAA**: test seven properties (unambiguous, investable, measurable, appropriate, current, specified in advance, accepted). Attribution: allocation + selection + interaction, always reconcile the sum (ch03).
- **Risk parity** looks at risk shares: a 60/40 portfolio carries about 92% equity risk (ch03).
- **Rebalancing**: calendar vs tolerance bands; CPPI E = m x (V - F) when a floor is mandatory (ch02).
- **Private clients**: risk is the probability of missing the goal; moderate cognitive biases, adapt to emotional ones within +/-3-15% per asset; TWRR measures the manager, MWRR the investor (ch04).

**Passive and ETF selection**
- **Passive is a per-sleeve efficiency decision**, not just cost: SPIVA shows 77-98% of active funds trail over 5-10 years in efficient classes (ch05).
- **TCO** = TER + sampling + turnover + swap fee + tax drag - lending income, plus spread and impact. Rank on tracking difference first, tracking error second; benchmark choice comes before cost (ch06, ch12).
- **Replication**: full for few liquid names; optimized sampling for lowest TE; stratified for lowest TCO; synthetic for illiquid indices, with counterparty limits (UCITS 10%) (ch07).
- **Smart beta** sits between active and passive; mix factors with complementary cycles because timing alpha is eaten by turnover (ch08).
- **Core-satellite**: passive core (60-80% of liquid assets) funds active satellites (ch06, ch10).

**Fund and manager selection**
- **Add-an-asset test**: accept if SR_new > rho x SR_portfolio (ch09).
- **Sharpe arithmetic and FAR**: average active manager loses by costs; FAR = fee / tracking error, judged at portfolio level (ch10).
- **Four phases**: plan (IPS, category odds from the Active/Passive Barometer), screen (cost first), due diligence (five Ps), manage the outcome (written thesis) (ch11).
- **Multimanager optimisation**: in active-risk space using pure alpha, misfit risk and pure information ratio; if you cannot forecast alpha, hold the index (ch13).
- **Quant-to-portfolio workflow**: peer quartiles, Quantsheet, qualitative DD, Quant Ranking Score, plateau optimization (ch14).

**Alternative investments**
- **UCITS vs AIF perimeter**: an AIF is any non-UCITS fund; AIFMD regulates the manager; Italian retail access needs Consob authorisation or a EUR 500,000 ticket; MiFID II target market (ch16).
- **Private performance**: read IRR with TVPI/DPI/RVPI; use KS-PME or Direct Alpha vs an index; funding risk, overcommitment 105-115%, denominator effect (ch17).
- **Illiquidity is the price of the premium**; secondaries are quoted as % of NAV and settled with call/distribution adjustments (ch18).
- **Wrapper liquidity is not asset liquidity**: listed vehicles trade at NAV discounts; clubs and co-investment trade fees for selection risk (ch19).

**ESG investing**
- **GSIA seven strategies** (exclusion, best-in-class, norms, thematic, impact, integration, engagement) label any ESG product (ch20).
- **ESG fund selection**: objectives, strategy, DD, benchmark, rating agency; more ESG content means more tracking error (ch23).
- **Materiality** (SASB) separates value-relevant ESG from noise; combined value + material ESG beat either alone in the guide's sample (ch25, ch26).
- **Impact report**: SDG-mapped indicators, delta = (a - b)/b, TCFD metrics WACI, attributable emissions, footprint (ch27).
- **ESG risk**: entropy-based RESG next to VaR and relative VaR (ch29). **Engagement** cycle: policy, plan, targets, dialogue, scoring, report (ch30).

## Scope & Limits
Covers five Italian practitioner guides (CFA Society Italy x Advisor Private, 2018-2020), 30 chapters, not a CFA curriculum. Italian source translated to English; PDF tables reconstructed, so figures were rechecked and several source arithmetic errors corrected and flagged in chapters (ch03, ch06, ch08, ch13, ch24, ch27). Sponsor inserts (Vanguard, Amundi, J.P. Morgan, Jupiter) are only briefly summarised and attributed. Italian tax, regulatory and market data date from 2018-2020 (26% flat tax, AIFMD, MiFID II, pre-SFDR ESG); verify current rules before advising. Authors' own methods (RESG, Pramerica scoring, VAAM) are single-provider approaches, not standards.
