# Glossary — Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives (Hull)

**American option** — Exercisable at any time up to maturity (Ch 1, 11)
**Arbitrage** — A riskless profit from simultaneous trades in two or more markets (Ch 1)
**Arrow–Debreu price** — The PV of $1 received if a given tree node is reached (Ch 32)
**Asian option** — Payoff depends on the average price of the underlying (Ch 26)
**Backwardation (normal)** — Futures price below the expected future spot price; also used for a downward-sloping futures curve (Ch 5)
**Barrier option** — Knocks in or out when the underlying hits a barrier (Ch 26)
**Basis** — Spot price minus futures price (Ch 3)
**Bermudan option** — Exercisable on specified dates only (Ch 26, 34)
**Beta** — The CAPM sensitivity of a portfolio to the market (Ch 3, 17)
**Black's model** — Lognormal-forward formula for options on futures, bonds, caps and swaptions (Ch 18, 29)
**Bootstrap method** — Iteratively solving for zero rates (or hazard rates) from instrument prices (Ch 4, 24)
**Box spread** — Bull call spread + bear put spread; worth (K₂ − K₁)e^{−rT} (Ch 12)
**Butterfly spread** — Long K₁ and K₃, short 2 × K₂ (Ch 12)
**Cap / caplet** — A portfolio of call options on a floating rate / one element of that portfolio (Ch 29)
**CCP** — A central counterparty that novates OTC trades and requires margin (Ch 2)
**CDS** — Credit default swap: insurance against default of a reference entity (Ch 25)
**CDO** — Collateralized debt obligation: a tranched portfolio of credit exposures (Ch 8, 25)
**Cheapest-to-deliver** — The deliverable bond minimizing quoted price − futures price × conversion factor (Ch 6)
**CIR model** — Mean-reverting short rate with √r volatility (Ch 31)
**Contango** — Futures above expected spot, or an upward-sloping futures curve (Ch 5)
**Convenience yield** — The benefit of holding a physical commodity rather than futures (Ch 5, 35)
**Conversion factor** — Price per $1 face value of a deliverable bond at a 6% yield (Ch 6)
**Convexity (bond)** — Second-order sensitivity of price to yield (Ch 4)
**Convexity adjustment** — Correction when the expected rate ≠ the forward rate (Ch 6, 30)
**Cost of carry** — Storage + financing − income (Ch 5)
**CVA / DVA** — Value adjustments for counterparty default / own default (Ch 9, 24)
**Delta** — ∂f/∂S (Ch 13, 19)
**Duration** — Weighted average time of cash flows; the price sensitivity to parallel yield shifts (Ch 4)
**EWMA** — Exponentially weighted moving average variance estimate (Ch 23)
**Expected shortfall** — Expected loss conditional on the loss exceeding VaR (Ch 22)
**FRA** — Forward rate agreement (Ch 4)
**FVA / MVA / KVA** — Funding / initial-margin / capital valuation adjustments (Ch 9)
**Gamma** — ∂²f/∂S² (Ch 19)
**GARCH(1,1)** — Variance model with mean reversion to V_L (Ch 23)
**Hazard rate** — Instantaneous default intensity (Ch 24)
**HDD / CDD** — Heating / cooling degree days (Ch 35)
**HJM** — Heath–Jarrow–Morton forward-rate framework (Ch 33)
**Hull–White model** — dr = [θ(t) − ar]dt + σdz (Ch 32)
**Implied volatility** — The σ that makes the BSM price equal the market price (Ch 15, 20)
**Itô's lemma** — The stochastic chain rule (Ch 14)
**LMM / BGM** — LIBOR (forward) market model (Ch 33)
**Maintenance margin** — The balance level that triggers a margin call (Ch 2)
**Market price of risk** — (μ − r)/σ shared by all derivatives on a variable (Ch 28)
**Martingale** — A zero-drift stochastic process (Ch 28)
**Netting** — Treating all trades with a counterparty as one on default (Ch 9, 24)
**Novation** — The CCP replacing a bilateral trade with two trades facing itself (Ch 2)
**Numeraire** — The unit in which security prices are measured (Ch 28)
**OAS** — Option-adjusted spread for MBS (Ch 33)
**Open interest** — Number of contracts outstanding (Ch 2)
**Put–call parity** — c + Ke^{−rT} = p + S₀ (Ch 11)
**Quanto** — A payoff in a currency different from that of the underlying (Ch 30)
**Real option** — An option embedded in a capital project (Ch 36)
**Recovery rate** — The bond value just after default as a % of face value (Ch 24)
**Risk-neutral valuation** — Expected payoff under drift r, discounted at r (Ch 13, 15)
**SOFR** — Secured Overnight Financing Rate, the US risk-free benchmark (Ch 4, 6)
**Stack and roll** — Rolling short-dated futures to hedge a long exposure (Ch 3)
**Straddle / strangle** — Call + put at the same strike / at different strikes (Ch 12)
**Swap rate** — The fixed rate making a swap worth zero (Ch 7)
**Swaption** — An option to enter a swap (Ch 29)
**Theta** — Rate of change of value with time (Ch 19)
**Total return swap** — Asset total return exchanged for floating + spread (Ch 25)
**VaR** — Loss not exceeded with X% confidence over N days (Ch 22)
**Variance swap** — Exchanges realized variance for a fixed variance (Ch 26)
**Vasicek model** — dr = a(b − r)dt + σdz (Ch 31)
**Vega** — ∂f/∂σ (Ch 19)
**Volatility smile / surface** — Implied volatility vs strike (and maturity) (Ch 20)
**Wiener process** — A Brownian motion with Δz = ε√Δt (Ch 14)
**Wrong-way risk** — Exposure rising with the counterparty's default probability (Ch 9, 24)
