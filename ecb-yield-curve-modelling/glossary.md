# Glossary — ECB Yield Curve Modelling (Nymand-Andersen)

**Asymptote (β₀)** — Long-run level of the Nelson-Siegel/Svensson forward and spot curve; must be positive (ch03, ch08)
**B-spline (basis spline)** — Locally supported cubic building block; a spline is a linear combination, built by De Boor recursion (ch02)
**Bid-ask threshold** — Liquidity filter: spread of at most 3 bp (ch05, ch08)
**Break-even inflation** — Nominal curve minus comparable inflation-linked curve (ch01)
**Central government sector (S.1311)** — ESA 2010 sector retained; state, local and social security excluded (ch05)
**Credit-risk yield curve** — Curve for a credit-homogeneous set (AAA or all-rated); the ECB publishes two daily (ch08)
**Decay factor (τ)** — Maturity scale of the exponential terms; sets hump position (ch03)
**Descriptive statistics** — Mandatory first step: know the sample before modelling (ch05)
**Diagnostic check** — Test of how well a model reproduces observed values (ch06)
**Discount function** — Price of a unit zero at each maturity; McCulloch splines fit it (ch02)
**ESA 2010** — European System of Accounts sector classification (ch05)
**Executable price** — Binding quote immediately hit-able; differs from real trade price by about 1-1.8% (ch05)
**Fisher-Nychka-Zervos (FNZ)** — Smoothing spline on the forward function, constant roughness penalty (ch02, ch04)
**Flexibility** — Ability to follow genuine bends, notably at the short end (ch01, ch04)
**Forward rate (instantaneous)** — Marginal rate for a very short loan t years ahead; what N&S/Svensson specify (ch01, ch03)
**Hit rate** — Share of observed yields within a fixed band of the curve; binary, band-dependent (ch06, ch07)
**Hybrid model (Banque de France)** — Svensson if β₃ is inside its confidence interval, else N&S; Svensson 405 of 502 days (ch03, ch07)
**In-sample / out-of-sample** — Bonds used to fit versus held out (1 in 10) to test (ch05, ch06)
**ISMA formula 6.4** — Yield convention used by the ECB (ch08)
**Knot point** — Maturity where spline segments join; spaced by equal bond counts (about every sixth bond) (ch02, ch06)
**Level, slope, curvature** — Shock decomposition: parallel move, tilt, medium-term bend (ch01, ch03)
**Liquidity preference / pure expectations / segmented markets / preferred habitat** — Four term-structure theories (ch01)
**McCulloch spline** — Cubic regression spline on the discount function; unstable forwards (ch02)
**MAE / RMSE / WMAE** — Mean absolute, root mean squared and duration-weighted (1/√D) mean absolute yield error (ch06)
**Mid-price** — Average of best bid and ask near 17:00 CET with spread within 3 bp (ch05)
**Minimum support** — A B-spline is non-zero over few segments, so local data changes stay local (ch02, ch07)
**Nelson-Siegel (N&S)** — 4-parameter forward-curve form: level, slope, one hump (ch03)
**Outlier rule** — Drop bonds beyond 2σ of the maturity-bracket mean yield; repeat once (ch05)
**Over-parameterisation** — Different parameter sets give almost the same curve (ch03, ch07)
**Par yield curve** — Coupon at which a bond prices at par, from the spot curve (ch01, ch03)
**Parsimonious model** — Few-parameter functional form (N&S, Svensson) (ch03)
**Residual maturity** — Time to redemption; x-axis of the curve (ch01)
**Robustness** — Insensitivity to small data changes or outliers; tested by bumping prices (ch06)
**Roughness penalty λ(m)** — Weight on ∫[f″(m)]²dm; higher means a stiffer forward curve (ch02)
**Smoothness** — No abrupt breaks in maturity spreads over time (ch06)
**Spot (zero-coupon) rate** — Discount rate for a single cash flow at maturity T; average of the forward rate over [0,T] (ch01, ch03)
**Svensson model** — 6-parameter N&S extension with a second hump; the ECB's published model (ch03, ch08)
**TARGET calendar** — Business days on which the ECB produces curves (ch05, ch08)
**Variable roughness penalty (VRP)** — Anderson-Sleath continuous penalty with L, S, μ (ch02)
**Waggoner model** — Forward spline with step-wise penalty, steps at 1 and 10 years (ch02)
**Yield minimisation** — Objective on yield errors (ECB); price objectives are weighted by 1/D² (ch03, ch08)
**Zero lower bound** — Regime not tested in the paper; flagged for research (ch04, ch08)
