# Glossary — CFA Society Italy Fixed Income & Unconstrained Fixed Income Guides

**Absolute return (mandate)** — target a cash-plus return (e.g. 3-month Euribor +1% to +3%) with a volatility cap rather than beat an index (ch 07, 09)
**Additional Tier 1 (AT1)** — perpetual, junior bank capital with discretionary non-cumulative coupons and a CET1 trigger (5.125% or 7%) for conversion or write-down (ch 03, 15)
**Amortized cost (book value)** — insurance segregated-account valuation: BV(t) = BV(t−1) + BV(t−1) × IRR − coupon; no mark-to-market (ch 07)
**Asset swap (ASW) spread** — fixed spread over Euribor/Libor that makes an asset swap NPV zero at inception (ch 05)
**Asset-liability management (ALM)** — setting the portfolio from the liabilities; liabilities typed 1-4 by known/unknown amount and timing (ch 08)
**Bail-in / BRRD** — resolution regime making unsecured bank creditors absorb losses; deposits to EUR 100,000 and covered bonds excluded (ch 03, 15)
**Basis (CDS-bond)** — CDS spread minus Z-spread; non-zero values allow arbitrage (ch 13)
**Bid-offer (bid-ask) spread** — price gap between dealer bid and offer; a liquidity proxy that widens with lower size, rating or foreign currency (ch 02, 04)
**Bid-to-cover ratio** — auction demand ÷ supply; well above 1 signals strong demand (ch 04)
**Bond floor** — PV of a convertible's straight bond cash flows; minimum value barring distress (ch 06, 14)
**Bookbuilding** — syndicate-run corporate bond placement: roadshow, initial price talk, order book, launch (ch 04)
**Breakeven inflation** — nominal yield minus real yield; inflation at which a linker and a nominal bond tie (ch 01, 06)
**Bullet vs amortizing** — principal at maturity vs in instalments (ch 01, 17)
**Call risk** — early redemption forces reinvestment at lower yields and caps price gains (ch 02)
**Carry trade** — borrow a low-rate currency, hold a high-rate one, usually unhedged; works because uncovered interest parity fails (ch 16)
**Cash flow matching** — immunize a liability with bonds whose cash flows replicate it (ch 08)
**CDS (credit default swap)** — premium paid for protection against a credit event; XOVER (EU HY, 75 names), CDX (US, 100 names) are indices (ch 13)
**Clean vs dirty price** — dirty = clean + accrued interest (ch 01)
**Contingent immunization** — manage actively while a surplus over the liability remains; switch to full immunization at the floor (ch 08)
**Convertible bond** — straight bond plus a call on shares: parity, premium, conversion ratio, delta (ch 06, 14)
**Conversion premium / payback period** — market conversion price over share price; premium per share ÷ favourable income differential (ch 06)
**Coupon (distributing) class** — fund share class paying periodic distributions; not the same as an Income fund (ch 10)
**Covenant** — contractual protection: financial (leverage tests), information, general (limits on assets, dividends, M&A) (ch 01, 06, 17)
**Covered bond (OBG)** — bank bond with dual recourse to a segregated, bankruptcy-remote cover pool and the bank (ch 03)
**Credit spread** — PD × (1 − recovery) + excess spread (ch 13); also yield over risk-free (ch 02)
**Current yield** — annual coupon ÷ price (ch 06)
**Delta (convertible)** — convertible price change per share price change; ~0 busted, ~50% balanced, ~100% quasi-equity (ch 14)
**Directional active / total return** — active mandates that beat a market index; total return has lower correlation and higher tracking error (ch 07)
**Discount margin** — average margin over the reference rate a floater earns at its price (ch 06)
**Dividend pusher / stopper** — hybrid clauses linking coupon to dividends (ch 15)
**Duration matching** — immunization with portfolio Macaulay duration equal to the liability's (ch 08)
**Effective duration / key rate duration** — price sensitivity via repricing; KRD isolates one curve point and sums to effective duration (ch 08)
**Equity credit** — share of a hybrid counted as equity by rating agencies (ch 03, 15)
**Event / legal risk** — hard-to-forecast shocks to the issuer or to the rules (ch 02)
**Excess spread** — spread beyond expected loss, for unexpected loss and illiquidity (ch 13)
**Expectations theory** — long rate = geometric average of expected short rates; forwards unbiased (ch 05)
**Fallen angel / rising star** — IG downgraded to HY / HY about to become IG (ch 03, 13)
**Flattener / steepener** — duration-neutral curve trades (ch 12)
**Forward rate** — implied future rate: (1+S2)² = (1+S1)(1+1f2) (ch 05, 06)
**Frontier market** — less liquid, smaller economy than EM (ch 11)
**Gamma trade** — delta-hedging a convertible to harvest volatility (ch 14)
**Grey market (when-issued)** — pre-issue trading of a new bond quoted as discount/premium to re-offer price (ch 04)
**Hybrid (corporate)** — perpetual or 30-50 year subordinated bond with call, step-up and deferral (ch 03, 15)
**Index ratio** — CPI today ÷ CPI at issue; scales a linker's coupon and principal (ch 01, 06)
**Investment grade / high yield** — Baa3/BBB- and above vs below (ch 02, 13)
**Liquidity preference theory** — long bonds need a premium; forwards are biased estimates (ch 05)
**Liquid Alternatives / Newcits** — hedge-style strategies in UCITS wrappers (ch 09)
**Market segmentation theory** — investor groups stay in preferred maturities (ch 05)
**Mezzanine** — hybrid debt with equity kicker, used in buyouts (ch 17)
**Modified duration / convexity** — ΔP/P ≈ −MD Δy + ½ C Δy² (ch 06)
**Negative convexity (callable)** — price compression near the call price (ch 06)
**Nominal spread / Z-spread / OAS** — yield difference to a government bond / constant spread over spot curve / spread after removing option value (ch 05)
**NDF (non-deliverable forward)** — cash-settled FX forward used for BRL, CLP, COP, PEN carry (ch 16)
**Option cost** — Z-spread − OAS (ch 05)
**Peer group (unconstrained)** — subjective set of comparable funds (ch 09)
**Primary dealers / specialists** — government bond dealers obliged to bid and quote (ch 04)
**Private debt** — non-listed senior, structured or direct-lending debt; closed funds 6-10 years; ELTIF for wealthy retail (ch 17)
**Quasi-sovereign** — state-controlled issuer; trades vs the sovereign (ch 13)
**Rating outlook / watch** — 6-12 month direction / imminent review (ch 06)
**Real yield** — fixed yield on an inflation-linked bond (ch 06)
**Reinvestment risk** — coupons reinvested at lower rates; minimal for zeros (ch 02, 10)
**Reset spread (back-end)** — spread added to the swap rate after a missed AT1 call (ch 15)
**Senior non-preferred** — senior debt ranking below senior preferred, above T2/AT1; TLAC-eligible (ch 03)
**Spot rate** — yield on a zero-coupon bond; bootstrapped from coupon bonds (ch 06)
**Spread duration** — ΔP ≈ −SD × Δspread; key HY risk (ch 13)
**Style drift** — manager departs from declared approach (ch 09)
**Term premium** — yield beyond expected short rates; 2Y10Y fair value from the risk factor in EM Local (ch 12, 16)
**Tier 2** — dated subordinated bank debt (min 5 years), bail-in and extension risk (ch 03, 15)
**TLAC** — total loss-absorbing capacity (18% of RWA from 2022) (ch 03)
**Tracking error** — standard deviation of portfolio minus benchmark returns (ch 07, 08)
**UIP / CIP** — uncovered / covered interest parity (ch 16)
**Yield to maturity / yield to worst** — IRR of promised flows; minimum of YTM and all call/put yields (ch 06)
**Yield curve risk** — non-parallel shifts affect maturities differently (ch 02)
