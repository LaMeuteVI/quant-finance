# Chapter 2: Investment Manager Selection

> CFA L3 2024, V3 LM2. The institutional manager search process.

## Core Idea

The **manager search** is the institutional process of identifying, evaluating, and selecting an investment manager. The stages: (1) **define the mandate** (the investment objective, the constraints, the benchmark), (2) **identify candidates** (the universe), (3) **screen** (the initial filter), (4) **due diligence** (the deep evaluation: investment philosophy, process, people, performance, risk, operations, compliance), (5) **negotiate** (the contract, the fees, the termination), (6) **monitor** (the ongoing review).

## Key Concepts

- **The investment policy statement (IPS)** — the document defining the mandate.
- **The manager universe** — the set of all potential managers.
- **Screening criteria** — the initial filter (size, style, geography, vehicle).
- **Due diligence** — the deep evaluation.
- **Investment philosophy** — the manager's belief about how to add value.
- **Investment process** — the manager's process for implementing the philosophy.
- **People** — the team; the institutional focus.
- **Performance** — the historical track record (TWR, IR, attribution).
- **Risk** — the risk management framework.
- **Operations** — the operational infrastructure.
- **Compliance** — the regulatory and compliance framework.
- **The GIPS standards** — the GIPS compliance for performance reporting.
- **The monitoring framework** — the ongoing review.
- **The termination criteria** — the conditions for terminating the manager.

## Key Takeaways

1. The manager search is a six-stage process.
2. Due diligence is the most important stage; it covers philosophy, process, people, performance, risk, operations, compliance.
3. The monitoring framework is the ongoing discipline.
