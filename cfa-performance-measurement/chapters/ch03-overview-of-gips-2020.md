# Chapter 3: Overview of the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS 2020)

> CFA L3 2024, V3 LM3. The GIPS 2020 standards.

## Core Idea

The **GIPS 2020 standards** are the global standard for performance reporting, managed by the CFA Institute. The standards require: (1) **GIPS compliance** for the firm, (2) **the definition of the firm** (the entity, the definition of the firm), (3) **the composite construction** (the grouping of portfolios with similar strategies), (4) **the calculation of the composite return** (TWR, gross and net of fees), (5) **the disclosure** (the GIPS Report), (6) **the presentation** (the GIPS Composite Report).

The 2020 update reflects: (a) the **private equity** and **real estate** extensions, (b) the **verifier** requirements, (c) the **pooled funds** and **fund-of-funds** provisions, (d) the **ESG** integration.

## Key Concepts

- **The GIPS standards** — the global standard for performance reporting.
- **The definition of the firm** — the entity for which GIPS compliance is claimed.
- **The composite** — the grouping of portfolios with similar strategies.
- **The composite return** — the asset-weighted TWR of the composite.
- **The GIPS Report** — the disclosure to prospective clients.
- **The GIPS Composite Report** — the presentation to prospective clients.
- **The verifier** — the independent third party that verifies the GIPS compliance.
- **The GIPS 2020 update** — the private equity, real estate, ESG, and verifier updates.

## Key Takeaways

1. The GIPS 2020 standards are the global standard.
2. The composite construction and return calculation are the core.
3. The verifier is the independent third party.
