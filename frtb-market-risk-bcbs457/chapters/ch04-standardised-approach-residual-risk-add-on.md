# Chapter 4: Standardised Approach — Residual Risk Add-On (SA-RRAO)

> BCBS 457, MAR23.

## Core Idea

The **Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO)** is the FRTB SA capital charge for instruments with **non-modellable risk** — risk factors that the SBM cannot capture (e.g. exotic options, gap risk, correlation risk in structured products). The RRAO is a flat 1.0% of the notional of the instrument; it is not risk-sensitive but is the only practical response to the non-modellable component.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Residual risk** — the risk not captured by delta, vega, curvature.
- **Non-modellable risk factors (NMRFs)** — risk factors for which the bank does not have sufficient data to model.
- **Exotic options** — barrier, lookback, Asian, digital; the standard NMRF examples.
- **Gap risk** — the risk of a price gap (e.g. over a weekend); NMRF for some instruments.
- **Correlation risk** — the risk of correlation breakdown; NMRF for multi-asset products.

## Formulas & Tables

- **RRAO**: 1.0% of the notional of the residual-risk-bearing instrument.
- **Notional**: the notional exposure of the instrument.

## Key Takeaways

1. The RRAO is 1.0% of notional.
2. It applies to non-modellable risk factors.
3. It is a flat charge, not risk-sensitive.
