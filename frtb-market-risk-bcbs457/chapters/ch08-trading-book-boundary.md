# Chapter 8: Trading Book / Banking Book Boundary

> BCBS 457, MAR25.

## Core Idea

The **boundary rules** define which instruments belong to the trading book (and are subject to FRTB market risk capital) and which belong to the banking book (and are subject to credit risk capital). The default is the **banking book**; the trading book is for instruments held for **trading intent** (short-term resale, arbitrage, hedging other trading book positions). Switching between books is restricted.

## Key Concepts

- **Trading book** — instruments held for trading intent; subject to FRTB market risk.
- **Banking book** — instruments held to maturity; subject to credit risk capital.
- **Presumptive list** — the list of instruments presumed to be in the trading book.
- **Internal risk transfer** — the documentation of a transfer between the trading book and the banking book.
- **Switching restrictions** — limited ability to switch instruments between books.
- **Supervisory approval** — required for material changes to the boundary.

## Key Takeaways

1. The default is the banking book.
2. The trading book is for trading intent.
3. Switching is restricted; supervisory approval is required.
