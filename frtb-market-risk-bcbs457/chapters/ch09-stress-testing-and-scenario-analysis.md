# Chapter 9: Stress Testing and Scenario Analysis

> BCBS 457, MAR99 (Guidance on use of IMA).

## Core Idea

The FRTB requires **stress testing** in addition to the IMA capital. The bank must run a 12-week stressed-window ES and compare to the current ES; the binding ES is the worse. The bank must also run **reverse stress tests** (find the scenario that breaks the model) and **scenario analyses** under hypothetical conditions (2008-style crisis, COVID-style crisis).

## Key Concepts

- **The 12-week stressed-window ES** — a parallel stressed ES computation.
- **Reverse stress test** — find the scenario that causes a given loss.
- **Historical scenarios** — 2008, 2020, 1987; the canonical reference set.
- **Hypothetical scenarios** — constructed by the bank; e.g. parallel rate shift, equity crash.
- **Combined stress** — the binding scenario.

## Key Takeaways

1. The 12-week stressed ES is required.
2. Reverse stress testing is required.
3. Scenario analysis is required.
