# Chapter 7: Simplified Standardised Approach

> BCBS 457, MAR40.

## Core Idea

The **Simplified Standardised Approach** is a reduced version of the FRTB SA for smaller, less complex banks. It uses a single global multiplier on the SA capital, eliminating the bucket-by-bucket calculation. The eligibility is restricted to banks with limited market risk exposure.

## Key Concepts

- **Simplified SA** — a reduced version of the FRTB SA.
- **Eligibility criteria** — small banks with limited market risk exposure.
- **Global multiplier** — a single multiplier instead of bucket-by-bucket.
- **Reduced reporting requirements** — less detailed reporting.

## Key Takeaways

1. The Simplified SA is for smaller banks.
2. The global multiplier is the simplification.
3. The eligibility is restricted.
