# Glossary — CFA Asset Allocation, CME and Behavioral Finance (L3 2023 R1-R7, L2 2022 R42)

**Active share** — share of portfolio weights that differ from the benchmark; complements tracking risk (ch 06)
**After-tax optimization** — optimize with `r_at = r_pt(1 − t)` and `σ_at = σ_pt(1 − t)`, correlations unchanged (ch 08)
**Anchoring and adjustment** — start from an arbitrary number (cost basis, last forecast) and adjust too little; an information-processing error (ch 01, 02, 03)
**Appraisal (smoothed) data** — valuations replacing transactions; volatility and correlations biased down, so de-smooth: `var(r) = [(1+λ)/(1−λ)]·var(R)` (ch 03, 04)
**Asset class criteria (five)** — homogeneous, mutually exclusive, diversifying, preponderance of world wealth, able to absorb capital (ch 06)
**Asset-only / liability-relative / goals-based** — three allocation approaches defined by objective and risk concept: volatility, shortfall vs liabilities, probability of failing goals (ch 06, 07)
**Asynchronicity** — observations labelled the same date are not simultaneous (time zones); distorts correlations (ch 03)
**Automatic stabilizers** — progressive taxes and means-tested transfers that dampen the cycle without new legislation (ch 03)
**Barnewall two-way model** — passive investors (need for security above risk tolerance) vs active investors (risked own capital) (ch 02)
**Behavioral Investor Types (PP, FF, II, AA)** — Pompian types: Passive Preserver, Friendly Follower, Independent Individualist, Active Accumulator (ch 02)
**Black-Litterman** — blends reverse-optimized equilibrium returns with views weighted by confidence; a return-generation model (ch 04, 07)
**Break-even inflation (BEI)** — nominal minus real zero yield of the same maturity = expected inflation + inflation-uncertainty premium (ch 05)
**Business cycle phases (five)** — initial recovery, early expansion, late expansion, slowdown, contraction (ch 03, 05)
**CAPE** — real price over 10-year average real earnings (Shiller); high starting CAPE preceded poor 10-year returns (ch 04, 05)
**Cognitive error vs emotional bias** — reasoning error (moderate it) vs feeling-driven bias (adapt to it) (ch 01)
**Conditioning information** — current economic state used to adjust risk and return; forecasts that ignore it are unconditional (ch 03)
**Conjunction fallacy** — treating a joint event as likelier than a component; for independent events `P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B)` (ch 02)
**Credit premium** — expected return above expected default loss; spread = premium + `PD × (1 − RR)` (ch 04, 05)
**Cross-sectional / intertemporal consistency** — forecasts coherent across asset classes / across horizons; more important than point accuracy (ch 03)
**Current account / capital account** — goods, services, income and transfers vs FDI and portfolio flows; mirror images; `(X−M) = (S−I) + (T−G)` (ch 03, 04)
**Data-mining bias** — repeated searching until a significant pattern appears; test with economic rationale and out-of-sample data (ch 03)
**De-smoothing** — recovering true variance from smoothed private-asset returns; λ = 0.8 triples the standard deviation (ch 04)
**Degree of integration (φ)** — weight on the globally integrated premium in Singer-Terhaar (ch 04)
**Disposition effect / loss aversion** — sell winners too soon, hold losers too long; losses weigh more than equal gains (ch 01)
**Economic balance sheet** — financial assets and liabilities plus human capital, PV of pensions, contributions and goals; economic net worth = assets − liabilities (ch 06, 07)
**Endowment bias** — attachment to owned assets; emotional, but moderate it when goals are at risk (ch 01)
**Equity value anchor** — `V_e = GDP × S_k × PE`; long-run equity value grows with nominal GDP (ch 03)
**Exogenous shocks (six)** — policy changes, new technology, geopolitics, natural disasters, natural resources, financial crises (ch 03)
**Framing / mental accounting** — answers depend on question presentation; money treated as non-fungible by source or use (ch 01, 02)
**Funded ratio / surplus** — `A/L`; `A − PV(L)`; both depend on the liability discount rate (ch 06, 07)
**Global market portfolio (GMP)** — market-value-weighted portfolio of all investable assets; default baseline for SAA (ch 06)
**Goals-based allocation** — sub-portfolio per goal with horizon and required probability; minimum expectation `E(R)T − zσ√T` (ch 06, 07)
**Grinold-Kroner** — `E(R) ≈ D/P + %ΔE − %ΔS + %ΔP/E`; long run `D/P + nominal GDP growth` (ch 04)
**Hindsight / confirmation / conservatism / representativeness** — belief-perseverance biases; conservatism overweights the prior, representativeness underweights it (ch 01)
**Home bias** — heavy domestic equity weight versus the global market portfolio (ch 02, 06)
**Human capital** — PV of mortality-weighted future earnings; about 30% equity-like, 70% bond-like on average (ch 06, 07)
**Illiquidity premium** — extra return for locking up capital (0.6% quarterly liquidity up to 6% for trading every 10 years); only for owners without near-term needs (ch 04, 08)
**Impossible trinity** — free capital flows, fixed exchange rate and independent monetary policy cannot coexist (ch 03)
**Inertia / default / 1/n** — DC plan behaviors: keep defaults, split equally across funds (ch 02)
**Inflation-uncertainty premium (π)** — extra nominal yield because the real payoff is uncertain (ch 05)
**Investment governance (six elements)** — objectives, decision rights, IPS process, SAA process, reporting, independent audit (ch 06)
**Investment policy statement (IPS)** — foundation document of objectives, constraints, responsibilities and rebalancing rules (ch 06)
**Leading indicators / diffusion index / look-ahead bias** — indicators ahead of the cycle; share of components rising; revised history fits better than real time (ch 03)
**Liability-relative approaches** — surplus optimization, hedging/return-seeking, integrated asset-liability (ch 07)
**Mean-variance optimization (MVO)** — maximize `E(R) − 0.005λσ²`; error-sensitive without good inputs (ch 07)
**Model / parameter / input uncertainty** — wrong structure, estimation error, wrong inputs; model uncertainty most serious (ch 03)
**Momentum / herding / recency / bubble** — return persistence 1-2 years with reversal at 2-5; bubble = more than 2σ from trend (ch 02)
**Neutral policy rate** — real equilibrium rate plus target inflation when gap is zero (ch 03, 05)
**Nowcasting (GDPNow)** — real-time forecast of the current-quarter advance GDP estimate (ch 03)
**Output gap** — L3 (ch 03): trend GDP minus actual (positive = slack); L2 (ch 05): log actual minus log potential (positive = overheating); check the sign convention (ch 03, 05)
**Overconfidence / self-attribution** — narrow ranges and over-trading; credit for wins, blame elsewhere for losses (ch 01)
**Overshooting (Dornbusch)** — currency jumps past long-run value then retraces (ch 04)
**Peso problem** — a feared event that never occurred biases ex post returns up and risk down (ch 03)
**Pricing kernel (SDF)** — `m = δ·MU(C_{t+s})/MU(C_t)`; premium arises from negative covariance of payoff with m (ch 05)
**Quantitative easing (QE)** — central bank purchases funded by new reserves (ch 03)
**Rebalancing corridor** — no-trade band; widen for costs, taxes, illiquidity, correlation; after-tax `R_at = R_pt/(1 − t)` (ch 07, 08)
**Regime change / nonstationarity** — shift in governing relationships; use only relevant history (ch 03)
**Resampled MVO (Michaud)** — average optimal weights over simulated inputs (ch 07)
**Reverse optimization** — implied returns `μ = r_f + λΣw_mkt` from market weights (ch 07)
**Risk budgeting / MCTR / ACTR** — `MCTR_i = β_i,P·σ_P`; optimal when excess return per MCTR is equal (ch 06, 07)
**Risk parity** — equal risk contribution; ignores expected returns (ch 06, 07)
**Seven-step CME framework** — specify, research history, choose methods, find sources, interpret, document, monitor (ch 03)
**Shrinkage estimator** — `w·target + (1−w)·sample`; biased, lower MSE (ch 04)
**Singer-Terhaar** — `RP = φ·ρσ·SR_GM + (1−φ)·σ·SR_i` for partly integrated markets (ch 04)
**Strategic / tactical / dynamic allocation** — long-run policy mix; short-term tilts; longer-term valuation tilts (ch 06, 08)
**Status quo / prudence / availability bias** — perpetuate recent observations; dampen forecasts; overweight vivid events (ch 03)
**Survivorship bias** — only survivors in the series; overstates returns (ch 03)
**Taylor rule** — `i* = r_neutral + π_e + 0.5(Y_e − Y_trend) + 0.5(π_e − π_target)` (ch 03; L2 form in ch 05)
**Term premium** — extra expected return for duration; procyclical, not read directly from the curve (ch 04, 05)
**Time diversification** — the belief that risk fades with horizon; shortfall probability falls but magnitude grows (ch 08)
**Total factor productivity (TFP)** — output growth not explained by labor and capital inputs (ch 03)
**Trend growth decomposition** — labor input growth plus labor productivity (capital deepening plus TFP) (ch 03)
**Zero lower bound** — belief rates cannot go negative; negative policy rates proved sustainable (ch 03)
