# Chapter 7: Transaction Costs and Market Impact

> Quant Backtest Hygiene, Chapter 7.

## Core Idea

Transaction costs are the difference between the backtest and the live P&L. The institutional standard: the **square-root law of impact**  (see  ch16 and  ch14). **The test**: the net IR (after cost) must be positive; the breakeven turnover must exceed the strategy's actual turnover; the capacity must be reported.

## Key Takeaways

1. This check is mandatory for every backtest.
2. The cross-references (above) provide the methodology.
3. A backtest that fails this check is not a strategy.
