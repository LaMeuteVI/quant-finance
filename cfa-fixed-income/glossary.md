# Glossary — CFA Fixed Income (L1 2020, L2 2022, L3 2023)

Terms aligned with the official CFA glossary; `ch NN` = chapter number.

**Accrued interest (AI)** — coupon earned since the last payment, (t/T) × PMT; independent of yield (ch 03)
**Affirmative covenant** — what the issuer must do (pay, insure, comply with laws); cheap and administrative (ch 01, ch 06)
**Arbitrage-free valuation** — pricing so that no zero-cost riskless profit exists; each cash flow is discounted at its own spot rate (ch 08)
**Asset-driven liability (ADL)** — assets are given, the liability structure is chosen to match (ch 13)
**Asset swap spread (ASW)** — bond coupon minus swap rate for the same cash-flow dates; a tradable spread over MRR (ch 15)
**Attachment point** — loss level at which a tranche starts to lose; sum of everything below it, plus OC and reserves (ch 04)
**Barbell / bullet / ladder** — maturities at two extremes / around one point / evenly spread; barbell has the highest convexity (ch 13, ch 14)
**Basis (CDS–bond)** — CDS spread minus bond spread over MRR; negative basis = bond spread wider than CDS (ch 11, ch 15)
**Basis point value (BPV, PVBP, DV01)** — price change in currency for a 1 bp yield move = money duration × 0.0001 (ch 05, ch 13, ch 14)
**Bermudan / European / American call** — exercisable on scheduled dates / one date / any time after the first call date (ch 01, ch 09)
**Bond equivalent yield (BEY)** — add-on rate on a 365-day year; common basis for money-market comparisons (ch 03)
**Bootstrapping** — deriving spot rates from par yields by forward substitution, shortest to longest maturity (ch 07, ch 08)
**Bullet bond** — principal repaid entirely at maturity (ch 01)
**Busted convertible** — share price far below conversion price; trades on straight-bond value (ch 09)
**Call protection period** — initial period when the issuer cannot call (ch 01, ch 09)
**Capital-indexed bond** — fixed coupon on an inflation-adjusted principal (TIPS) (ch 01)
**Carrying value** — price on the constant-yield trajectory at the purchase YTM; capital gains are measured against it (ch 05)
**Cash flow matching** — dedicated bond portfolio whose coupons and principal cover each liability, built from the last liability backward (ch 13)
**Cash flow yield** — IRR of a portfolio's aggregate cash flows; the rate immunization locks in (ch 05, ch 13)
**Cheapest-to-deliver (CTD)** — lowest-priced deliverable obligation of equal or higher seniority (CDS) or the bond that sets the futures price (ch 11, ch 13)
**Clean (flat) price / dirty (full) price** — price without / with accrued interest; quote flat, settle full (ch 03)
**CoCo (contingent convertible)** — converts or is written down automatically on a trigger such as a capital-ratio breach (ch 01, ch 09)
**Collateralized debt obligation (CDO)** — SPE issuing tranches against a managed pool of bonds (CBO), loans (CLO) or ABS (ch 04)
**Commercial paper (CP)** — short-term unsecured note; rollover risk is covered by backup lines (ch 02)
**Conditional prepayment rate (CPR)** — annualized SMM, 1 − (1 − SMM)¹² (ch 04)
**Condor** — four-leg curvature trade made of two duration-neutral pairs (ch 14)
**Contingent immunization** — active management while the surplus exceeds a trigger, then passive immunization (ch 13)
**Contraction risk** — rates fall, prepayments speed up, reinvestment at lower rates and capped price upside (ch 04)
**Conversion ratio / price / value / premium** — par ÷ conversion price / par ÷ ratio / share price × ratio / price − conversion value (ch 01, ch 09)
**Convexity** — second-order price sensitivity; positive for option-free bonds, negative for callables near the money (ch 05, ch 09)
**Coupon effect / maturity effect** — lower coupon or longer maturity gives a larger % price change (exception: long low-coupon discount bonds) (ch 03)
**Covenant** — contract term restricting or obliging the issuer; affirmative or negative (ch 01, ch 06)
**Covered bond** — dual recourse to the issuer and a dynamic cover pool; non-performing assets are replaced (ch 01, ch 10)
**Credit curve** — spreads by maturity for one issuer, typically upward sloping (ch 06, ch 10, ch 11)
**Credit default swap (CDS)** — protection buyer pays a premium; seller pays (1 − RR) × notional after a credit event (ch 11)
**Credit enhancement** — internal (subordination, OC, reserve accounts) or external (guarantee, surety, LOC, cash collateral) (ch 01, ch 04)
**Credit event** — bankruptcy, failure to pay, or restructuring (excluded in standard North American contracts) (ch 11)
**Credit-linked note (CLN)** — bond whose redemption falls after a credit event on a reference asset; investor is protection seller (ch 02, ch 10)
**Credit migration risk** — downgrade risk that widens spreads; lowers expected return because losses outweigh gains (ch 06, ch 10)
**Credit valuation adjustment (CVA)** — PV of expected default losses; fair value = VND − CVA (ch 10, ch 15)
**Cross-default** — default on one obligation triggers default on all (ch 06)
**Current yield** — annual coupon ÷ flat price (ch 01, ch 03)
**Curve duration** — sensitivity to a benchmark curve shift: effective duration, key rate durations (ch 05)
**Day-count convention** — actual/actual (governments), 30/360 (corporates), actual/360 or 365 (FRNs, money market) (ch 03)
**Default probability (POD)** — probability of missing a contractual payment; hazard rate is the conditional per-period POD (ch 06, ch 10)
**Defeasance** — accounting: dedicated securities cover the debt; CMBS: borrower funds a government portfolio replicating the loan (ch 04, ch 13)
**Discount margin (DM)** — FRN spread over the index that reprices the note; DM > quoted margin implies price below par (ch 03, ch 10, ch 15)
**Discount rate vs add-on rate** — interest ÷ face value (understates return) vs ÷ price (ch 03)
**Dispersion** — PV-weighted variance of cash-flow times around Macaulay duration (ch 13)
**Duration gap** — Macaulay duration minus investment horizon (ch 05)
**Duration times spread (DTS)** — spread duration × spread; risk yardstick because spreads move proportionally (ch 12, ch 15)
**Effective duration / convexity** — model-based sensitivity to a parallel benchmark curve shift at constant OAS (ch 05, ch 09)
**Embedded option** — contingency in the indenture, not separable (unlike a warrant) (ch 01, ch 09)
**Enhanced indexing** — index-like risk factors, small deviations, modest outperformance (ch 12, ch 13)
**Excess spread** — asset yield above note coupons and losses; the first line of defense in auto ABS (ch 01, ch 04)
**Expected exposure (EE)** — amount at risk at a default date, including the coupon due (ch 10)
**Expected loss** — PD × LGD; credit risk is ranked by expected loss (ch 06, ch 10, ch 15)
**Extendible bond** — holder can extend maturity; economically equivalent to a putable bond (ch 09)
**Extension risk** — rates rise, prepayments slow, the security lengthens as its price falls (ch 04)
**Flight to quality** — government yields fall while spreads widen (bull flattening) (ch 07, ch 12, ch 15)
**Floating-rate note (FRN)** — coupon = reference rate + quoted margin, set in advance, paid in arrears (ch 01, ch 02)
**Forward rate** — rate agreed today for a future loan; breakeven reinvestment rate, "2y5y" = 5-year rate starting in 2 years (ch 03, ch 07)
**Haircut (repo margin)** — collateral value minus loan amount; rises with term and falls for scarce collateral (ch 02, ch 12)
**Hazard rate** — probability of default in a period given survival to its start (ch 10, ch 11)
**Holding-period (horizon) yield** — IRR from purchase price to reinvested coupons plus sale price (ch 05)
**I-spread** — YTM minus interpolated swap rate (ch 03, ch 07, ch 15)
**Immunization** — replicating a zero-coupon bond so a known liability is funded whatever the rate move (ch 13)
**Indenture (trust deed)** — legal contract of issuer obligations and bondholder rights, enforced by a trustee (ch 01)
**Interbank offered rate (IBOR)** — unsecured interbank term rates; being replaced by RFRs such as SOFR (ch 02, ch 07)
**Inverse floater** — coupon = C − L × R; leveraged if L > 1 (ch 01, ch 02)
**Key rate duration (KRD)** — sensitivity to one maturity point; sum equals effective duration; measures shaping risk (ch 05, ch 07, ch 14)
**Liability-driven investing (LDI)** — assets managed against given liabilities (ch 12, ch 13)
**Liquidity** — trading quickly near fair value; proxied by bid–offer spread (ch 02, ch 12)
**Loan-to-value (LTV) / DSCR** — loan ÷ property value / NOI ÷ debt service (ch 04)
**Loss given default (LGD)** — exposure × (1 − recovery rate) (ch 06, ch 10)
**Macaulay duration** — PV-weighted average time to cash flows; the immunizing horizon (ch 05)
**Make-whole call** — call price = PV of remaining flows at a sovereign yield plus a narrow spread (ch 01, ch 09)
**Market conversion premium** — market conversion price minus share price, per share (ch 09)
**Matrix pricing** — estimating an illiquid bond's yield from comparable liquid bonds (ch 03, ch 12)
**Modified duration** — MacDur/(1 + r); % price change per unit change in the bond's own YTM (ch 05)
**Money duration** — modified duration × full price; currency price change per unit yield (ch 05, ch 13)
**Negative pledge** — covenant barring new debt that ranks ahead of existing bonds (ch 01, ch 06)
**Notching** — issue ratings moved from the issuer rating for seniority, larger at lower ratings (ch 06, ch 10)
**Option-adjusted spread (OAS)** — constant spread to tree rates that equates model value to price; Z-spread minus option cost (ch 03, ch 09, ch 15)
**Overcollateralization (OC)** — collateral exceeding issued principal; internal enhancement (ch 01, ch 04)
**Par curve** — YTMs at which bonds price at par (ch 03, ch 07)
**Pari passu** — same ranking regardless of maturity or size (ch 06)
**Pass-through rate** — WAC minus servicing and guarantee fees (ch 04)
**Path dependence** — cash flows depend on the path of rates (MBS burnout); needs Monte Carlo (ch 08)
**Planned amortization class (PAC)** — tranche with a protected principal schedule inside a collar; support tranches absorb variability (ch 04)
**PSA benchmark** — CPR rises 0.2% per month to 6% at month 30; x PSA scales the path (ch 04)
**Pull to par** — flat price converges to par on the constant-yield trajectory (ch 03)
**Put (investor)** — raises price and lowers yield versus a straight bond (ch 01, ch 09)
**Receiver / payer swaption** — right to receive fixed (long duration) / pay fixed (short duration) (ch 13, ch 14)
**Recovery rate** — fraction of exposure recovered; ranks by seniority (ch 06, ch 10)
**Repo / reverse repo** — sale with agreed repurchase = collateralized loan (dealer borrows cash / lends cash) (ch 02, ch 12)
**Risk-neutral vs actual POD** — risk-neutral includes timing premium, liquidity and tax; used for pricing only (ch 10)
**Rolldown (riding the curve)** — return from a bond aging along a static upward curve (ch 07, ch 12, ch 14)
**Rolling yield** — coupon income plus rolldown; return if nothing changes (ch 12, ch 14)
**Securitization** — moving loans to a bankruptcy-remote SPE that issues tranches (ch 04)
**Shaping risk** — sensitivity to curve shape (slope, curvature) rather than level (ch 05, ch 07, ch 14)
**Single monthly mortality (SMM)** — prepayment ÷ (beginning balance − scheduled principal) (ch 04)
**Sinking fund** — scheduled principal retirement, often by lottery at par; reduces credit risk (ch 01, ch 02, ch 09)
**SOFR** — secured overnight rate from US repo trades; USD Libor successor (ch 02, ch 07)
**Special collateral** — in-demand security financed at a below-market repo rate (ch 02)
**Special purpose entity (SPE/SPV)** — bankruptcy-remote issuer in securitization, holding assets by true sale (ch 01, ch 04)
**Spot (zero) rate** — YTM on a zero-coupon bond (ch 03, ch 07)
**Spread duration** — % price change for a 1% change in credit spread (ch 12, ch 15)
**Step-up coupon** — coupon rises on set dates, often paired with a call (ch 01)
**Structural (twist) risk** — risk that non-parallel shifts break an immunization (ch 13)
**Structural model / reduced-form model** — default when assets fall below debt (why) / exogenous intensity (when) (ch 10)
**Structural subordination** — holding-company debt ranks behind subsidiary creditors (ch 01, ch 06)
**Swap spread** — swap rate minus same-maturity government yield (ch 07)
**TED spread / Libor–OIS** — stress gauges of bank credit and funding liquidity (ch 07)
**Term structure theories** — unbiased expectations, local expectations, liquidity preference, segmented markets, preferred habitat (ch 07)
**Tracking error** — standard deviation of active return versus benchmark (ch 12, ch 13)
**Tranching (credit vs time)** — losses bottom-up vs prepayment variability redistributed (ch 04)
**Underwritten vs best-efforts offering** — underwriter buys the issue vs sells as broker for a commission (ch 02)
**Value additivity / dominance** — the two arbitrage types: whole = sum of parts; riskless payoffs earn the same return (ch 08)
**Value assuming no default (VND)** — bond value discounted on benchmark rates as if default-free (ch 10)
**Warrant** — attached, separately tradable option on the issuer's shares (ch 01)
**Weighted average life (WAL)** — average time to receipt of projected principal under a prepayment assumption (ch 04)
**Yield to maturity (YTM)** — IRR of promised flows; equals realized return only with hold-to-maturity, no default, reinvestment at YTM (ch 03, ch 05, ch 07)
**Yield-to-worst** — lowest of YTM and all yields-to-call (ch 03)
**Z-spread** — constant spread over the spot curve that reprices a bond; OAS at zero volatility (ch 03, ch 07, ch 09, ch 15)
