## Interpretation

This is a useful rejection: a positive Sharpe and high DSR did not meet the desk's survival constraint. The loss path is too deep for the registered threshold. The failure is not repaired by changing the lookback, target volatility or threshold after seeing this result. Such a change would be a new hypothesis in the same family and its trial count would remain visible.

The sign-inverted and permutation controls specified in the research memo were not calculated after the primary pipeline gate failed; they cannot revive a rejected strategy and would create avoidable additional research degrees of freedom. The candidate did not earn a holdout read.
