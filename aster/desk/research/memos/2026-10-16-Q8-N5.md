## Sample

- Q3 local funding-rate store: 538 monthly Parquet partitions, 12 brief symbols.
- **4,929 aligned 8-hour fixing rows**, all strictly pre-cutoff.
- Symbols: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, AAVEUSDT, DOGEUSDT, SUIUSDT,
  XRPUSDT, BNBUSDT, LINKUSDT, NEARUSDT, XMRUSDT and FETUSDT.
- Funding is `last_funding_rate` per fixing. Positive funding means longs pay
  shorts under the desk convention.

## Results from the artefact

- Panel mean funding rate: **0.0001234 per 8-hour fixing** (1.234 bps).
- Median symbol positive-fixing fraction: **84.1%**.
- Mean simple annualised carry equivalent: **13.5%** before fees, basis,
  borrow, margin and execution. This is a carry *quote*, not a strategy return.
- Mean funding autocorrelation: **lag 1 = 0.737**, **lag 3 = 0.593**,
  **lag 7 = 0.496**.
- Mean cross-sectional dispersion: 1.97e-4 rate units; median 1.12e-4.
- Cross-sectional rank persistence: lag 1 = 0.590, lag 3 = 0.477,
  lag 7 = 0.383.
- Conditional high-minus-low funding spread at the next fixing: all 12 × 3
  horizon cells are positive; the median is **4.82 bps of funding rate**.
  This is mainly persistence of funding levels, not a return prediction.

## Economic reading and falsification

The prior that funding is positive and persistent is supported. The extreme-
funding conditional grid is also persistent, but it does **not** establish
cash-and-carry profitability: it measures future funding rate, not the joint
spot/perp basis, hedge slippage, borrow, margin or net execution P&L.

The preregistered weak-predictability expectation is therefore interpreted
carefully: funding persistence is strong, but the artefact contains no premium,
open-interest or perp-price series with which to test crowding or net basis
risk. Q8 V1 does not claim an alpha and does not register a ledger hypothesis.

The carry quote must be compared with costs correctly. The Q4 cost grid is a
round-trip execution hurdle; it cannot simply be subtracted once from every
8-hour funding observation. A real delta-neutral strategy needs a separate
funding-aware pipeline path (the F1 V1 lesson), spot/perp mark prices, borrow
and basis-risk accounting. Therefore there is **no capacity claim** and no
backtest from Q8 V1.

## Limitations and next gate

- No premium-index, mark-price or open-interest series is present in this
  local input; no proxy was invented.
- No spot/perp simultaneous return panel was constructed.
- The 13.5% annualised figure is a descriptive carry equivalent only.
- A future F2/F1v2 must preregister a funding injection channel, use the
  existing ledger trial counter, include fees/impact/basis/borrow, and remain
  subject to the dev gate, walk-forward, PBO and (human-only) holdout/paper
  gates.

**Verdict: Q8 V1 DONE as descriptive funding evidence; no strategy candidate.**
