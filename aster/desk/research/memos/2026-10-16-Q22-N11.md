**Other observables**:

- **Kyle λ t-stat** ranges from 16.6 (ETH 15min) to 47.3 (BTC 1min) — every axis is highly significant; the impact mechanism is real.
- **Amihud → next-bar |ret| Spearman** is small and often negative (−0.04 to +0.04) — illiquidity does not predict the next bar's absolute return.
- **Roll spread** invalid on all axes (cov > 0; the 2022-01..04 sample is trending for BTC, the 2022-01..02 sample is ranging for ETH).
- **VPIN** is low (0.028–0.031) on every axis.

**Cross-asset signed flow lead-lag (BTC ↔ ETH)**:

- 5min: Spearman is **symmetric** around 0 and flat at 0.0706–0.0708 across lags −5..+5 (n = 16 992). The contemporaneous correlation is 0.0708; lead-lag is **0** (the same value at lag = 1, −1, 2, −2, etc.).
- 15min: same pattern, flat at 0.0892–0.0898. Contemporaneous = 0.0898; lead-lag = 0.

This means BTC and ETH signed flow are essentially **synchronous** at 5min/15min: there is **no information diffusion** from BTC to ETH (or vice versa) in the signed flow. The H-20261022-002 lead-lag prediction (ρ > 0.05 at lag = +1) is **falsified** as a *directional* signal; the 0.07 is just contemporaneous co-movement.

## Verdict

- **H-20261022-001** (signed flow → next-bar return, gross > 60 bps): **falsified on all 6 axes.** The Spearman grows with bar size (correct), but the gross return spread grows too slowly: 14.37 bps at 15min ETH is the maximum, still 45.63 bps below the 60 bps 3× taker hurdle.
- **H-20261022-002** (BTC → ETH 5m lead-lag ρ > 0.05): **falsified as a directional signal** — the cross-asset Spearman is symmetric and flat. There is contemporaneous co-movement (0.07 at 5m, 0.09 at 15m) but **no lead-lag**.
- **No ledger hypothesis, no candidate.** The microstructure signal is real and well-documented (Kyle λ t-stats of 16-47), but the per-trade cost floor (60 bps conservative 3× taker) is not cleared at any of {1m, 5m, 15m} on either BTC or ETH.

## Why V3 still has no candidate

The mechanism is solid (Easley-O'Hara: prices move with order flow, in the right direction, with the right magnitude). The cost of one round-trip on Binance spot BTCUSDT is ~5–10 bps/side, so the conservative 6 bps/side round-trip × 3× = 60 bps is a **high** hurdle. The signal at 15-minute bars is 14 bps gross spread, which is ~25 % of the hurdle. To clear 60 bps the bar size would need to grow to ~1h (4×), at which point the trade count collapses to ~720 bars / month and capacity becomes the binding constraint.
