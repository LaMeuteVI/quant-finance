## Result

The fixed grid contains **870 cells**; **77/870** are nominally significant (`p<0.01`, `|rho|>0.05`) before any cross-sectional/FDR correction. This is a multiple-testing result, not evidence of alpha. No corrected selection, cost hurdle, placebo, stability or capacity test was used to promote a strategy. `candidate=false` is therefore intentional.

The existing Hamilton two-state cross-asset check remains negative: the dated Axe 7 artefact reports null-bootstrap p-values 0.30/0.45/0.30/0.65 for BTC/ETH/BNB/SOL and no consistent BTC-regime → alt-return gap. Q11 does not retune that model after observing these results.

**Decision:** close N4 as descriptive. Do not register a ledger hypothesis. Any future regime strategy requires a fresh preregistration, corrected multiple testing, net costs, capacity and a counted `desk backtest`.
