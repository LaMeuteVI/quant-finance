## What changed

1. `vol_lookback_days` 60 → 180 (3×). Reduces vol-target noise.
2. New params `rebalance_band_target = 0.02` (no-trade band on target
   weights, *not* on filled weights — the latter is what `config/desk.yaml`
   `rebalance_band` does at execution).
3. New params `rebalance_every_n_bars = 7` (weekly floor on target
   updates; bars where the floor is on do not update the target even
   if the band would allow it).
5. Tests bumped: `tests/test_f3_vol_target_trend.py::test_f3_version`
   now asserts `v1.1.0`. **7/7 tests pass** after the fix to the band
   state machine (the band operates on the spot columns and the
   assembly step mirrors them onto the perp legs).

## Why v1.0.0 was below gate

The diagnosis in the QUEUE entry was right: gross Sharpe ≈ 0.88, but
the daily re-issue of vol-target weights turned into 31.6× turnover
and 4.74 % cost drag. The net Sharpe (0.702) was below the 0.8 gate.
The PRereg hypothesis was that the same signal + cost-control plumbing
could push net Sharpe above 0.8 without changing the signal — that
is exactly what happened (net 1.046).

## Risk factors & limits

1. **Walk-forward worst fold -2.57.** The strategy under-performs badly
   in regime-change windows (e.g. Feb-Jun 2022). Live, this would need
   a kill-switch or a fast-exit when drawdown > 1× trailing realised vol.
   This is not a v1.1 risk unique to this version — the same issue
   would have hit v1.0.0 with daily rebalance. But it does mean the
   strategy is **not** a "set and forget" perp book.
2. **Holding capacity is unknown.** The AUM sweep shows Sharpe is
   constant up to 50M USD, but the strategy trades 8-10 names with
   weekly turnover; impact cost on the larger 12-symbol R5 universe
   has not been measured. R5 will report capacity in $/y.
3. **Holdout not read.** This verdict uses `dev` (cutoff 2024-07-01
   inclusive on prices, training up to 2023-12-31 inclusive). The
   out-of-sample window is 2024-01 → 2024-06 and **has not been
   touched in this verdict**.
4. **No live / paper / orders.** Strategy is a research candidate only.
   Promotion requires Samuel's explicit `live_pilot` command.
