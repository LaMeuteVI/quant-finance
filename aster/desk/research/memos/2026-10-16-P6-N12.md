## 1. What was wrong

F1 v1 (`H-20261016-002`) dev run produced zero weights on every
fold and `sharpe_ann / DSR / walk_forward / PBO = None` because:

- `pipeline.run` never passed the funding panel to the strategy.
- The P1 `MarketData` reindex dropped perp-suffixed funding
  columns.
- `store.load_funding` looks for `data/funding/binance/<SYM>/8h.parquet`
  but the partitions live at
  `data/r2/market/futures/um/funding_rate/symbol=<SYM>/year=.../...`
  (no materialiser ever wrote the standard layout).
- The engine had no `perp_close` argument; a delta-neutral book
  earned zero return on the perp leg and free put option on the
  spot leg (an artefact, not a strategy).

This was a pipeline-engineering failure, **not a rejection**.
H-20261016-002 is recorded as *invalid run, infrastructure*; the
trial stays in the family count for DSR.

## 2. What P6 changes (engine + pipeline + materialiser)

- `src/aster/data/funding_materialise.py`: `materialise_funding_panel`
  reads the R2 Hive tree, reindexes to the bar frequency with a
  causal `ffill` (no `bfill` from future fixings), and writes
  `data/funding/binance/<SYM>/<timeframe>.parquet`. The helper
  `perp_fixing_rate_per_bar(idx)` returns a mask equal to 1.0 on
  00/08/16 UTC fixings, 0 elsewhere.
- `src/aster/backtest/engine.py`: `run_backtest` gains `perp_close`
  and `perp_fixing_mask` arguments. The perp leg's per-bar return
  is `perp_close.pct_change()`; the funding charge is
  `held × perp_panel × fixing_mask` (no triple-counting). A perp
  leg without a price source raises `ValueError` (the cd1f6df DoD:
  "a perp leg without a perp price series makes the run **raise**,
  never 'tradable with zero return'").
- `src/aster/backtest/pipeline.py`: `pipeline.run` materialises
  the funding store, loads it, builds the fixing mask, mirrors
  spot funding to the perp column, injects `funding=...` into the
  strategy kwargs, and raises `PipelineEmptyWeightsError` on
  all-zero weight frames.
