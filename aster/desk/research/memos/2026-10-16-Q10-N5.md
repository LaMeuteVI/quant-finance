# Q10 — N3 per-alt volatility forecast grid (memo V1)

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Preregistration**: `research/notes/2026-10-16-Q10-prereg.md`.
**Artefact**: `research/atlas/q10_volatility.json` (30,758 bytes).
**Code/tests**: `src/aster/research/q10_volatility.py`,
`tests/test_q10_volatility.py`; targeted N3/Q10 tests **6 passed**.
**Cutoff**: strictly `< 2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout read.

## Method and amendment

The initial implementation re-estimated GARCH at every daily origin and
exceeded the local compute budget before producing an artefact. Before any
successful data result, the implementation was amended in the preregistration:
GARCH(1,1) parameters are estimated once on the fixed pre-2023 training
sample, then filtered recursively through the 2023-01-01 to 2024-06-30
evaluation window. HAR remains recursively fitted. The target, loss functions,
comparison family and falsification criteria were unchanged.

The grid contains 10 symbols, 5,129 forecast rows and 60 fixed comparison
cells: HAR/naive, GARCH/naive and HAR/GARCH under squared and absolute log-RV
loss. BH-FDR is applied across the 60 cells.

## Findings

- BH q < 0.05: **52/60** comparison cells.
- BH q < 0.05 and same sign in 2023 and 2024: **50/60**.
- HAR versus naive: **18/20** cells pass BH q < 0.05, and **18/20** are
  sign-stable; SUI has no valid forecast origins under the 720-active-minute /
  1,000-consecutive-pair gate and is not imputed.
- HAR beats the naive forecast on every other symbol under both loss functions:
  mean loss differences are negative in the artefact convention.
- GARCH versus naive: 16/20 cells pass BH q < 0.05; 14/20 are sign-stable.
  GARCH is not a trading signal and does not establish a volatility premium.
- Inverse-leverage diagnostic is mixed across the ten assets: next-day RV after
  magnitude-matched positive versus negative returns changes sign by asset.
  This is a descriptive result, not a leverage filter.

## Verdict

The volatility-forecast prior generalises to the alt panel: HAR-RV materially
improves forecast loss over the fixed naive benchmark in 9/10 assets. This is
forecast value, not alpha. Q10 produces **no strategy candidate**, no ledger
hypothesis and no `desk backtest`; costs, return direction, capacity and net
Sharpe were not established. The compression-to-expansion and vol-targeting
ideas remain separate strategy hypotheses and cannot inherit this forecast
result.

## Limitations

- The panel is survivorship-bounded; XMR is retained as the delisting guard.
- SUI has only 424 valid days and cannot satisfy the fixed training/evaluation
  gate; it is explicitly missing rather than backfilled.
- A future volatility strategy must preregister a return mechanism, costs and
  sizing, then pass the normal desk backtest, walk-forward/PBO and capacity
  gates. The sealed holdout remains untouched.
