## Ladder definitions

Recorded in `research/atlas/q17_atlas.json`:

- N0 — raw data hydration (klines 1m).
- N1 — TA panel (32 signals × 4 horizons).
- N2 — multi-timeframe descriptive.
- N3 — volatility product (HAR-RV / VRP).
- N4 — regime grid (cross-sectional).
- N5 — causal flow features (kline flow).
- N6 — primary-feature ML (out-of-sample).
- N7 — synthesis & preregistration of mature hypotheses.

## Lesson

Per-symbol dossiers with a "level reached" header compress the per-asset evidence base into a single indexable page. The atlas is **axis-keyed** (Q6, Q7, ..., Q17), not symbol-keyed; the dossier adds the symbol-keyed pointer the atlas lacks. The dossier is regenerated from the on-disk artefacts — it is a **view**, not a source of truth — and it is reproducible from `uv run python -m aster.research.q17_atlas`.
