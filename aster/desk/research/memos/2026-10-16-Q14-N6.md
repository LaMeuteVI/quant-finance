# Q14 — Panel and relative value (6.3, N6)

**Status:** done, descriptive only. No candidate, no ledger hypothesis, no holdout read.

## Protocol recap

Preregistration in `research/notes/2026-10-16-Q14-prereg.md` declared:

- universe: 8 approved alt symbols (BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, AAVE, SUI, NEAR) on the panel intersection starting 2023-05-05 (driven by SUI/AAVE/NEAR availability in R2).
- 3 sub-blocks: panel fixed-effects on pre-declared signal proxies, 5 pre-declared sector pairs (L1, payments, DeFi) with ADF/KPSS/half-life, cross-sectional momentum/reversal on residuals.
- falsification: pair candidate requires ADF p<0.01 + KPSS p>0.05 + half-life in [1, 30] trading days; FE signal requires cross-sectional BH-surviving p<0.01.

## What actually ran

`src/aster/research/q14_panel.py` (single, prereg-conformant run):

- Panel: 423 days × 8 symbols, 2023-05-05 → 2024-06-30.
- **Sector pairs**: all 5 pairs pass ADF strongly (p ≈ 1e-13 to 1e-30) and KPSS (p=0.1, fail to reject stationarity) ⇒ the spreads are highly stationary. But all 5 half-lives are **0.63–0.71 days**, below the 1-day minimum declared in the prereg. **No pair meets the candidate bar.**
- **Panel FE**: lag-1 return, 5-day momentum, and 22-day reversal, all within-entity and within-time demeaned. None is significant at the 0.01 level (p-values 0.67, 0.91, 0.89). **No cross-sectional alpha at the daily horizon after fixed effects.**
- **Cross-sectional signal grid**: 12 cells (4 horizons × 3 lags), 8 dates per cell — n too small to be meaningful at the daily horizon on the 423-day panel. Reported for completeness; not a candidate.

## Why no candidate

1. The 5 prereg sector pairs are mean-reverting but too fast: half-lives under 1 day mean any trade is dominated by execution noise, not the spread signal. The prereg's 1-30 day half-life window was specifically designed to exclude this regime; the data correctly fails it.
2. The FE test on the three price-only proxy signals (lag-1, 5-day momentum, 22-day reversal) returns p>0.6 for all three. After removing the common market mode and the time-fixed effect, no within-entity predictability remains.
3. The cross-sectional grid is too small (8 dates per cell) to be a candidate even if any single IC was non-zero.

## Decision

No hypothesis registered. Q14 records the *absence* of an exploitable pairwise or panel structure on the alt panel at the daily horizon, consistent with the prior Q6–Q13 nulls.

## Self-critique

- The "spread half-life" test on log-prices is the standard co-movement diagnostic, but a proper pairs test would co-fit the hedge ratio β (e.g. via Johansen or rolling OLS) before computing the spread. The prereg declared the simple log-spread for speed; this is a known conservative approximation.
- The panel is short (423 days). 2+ years of daily data is the minimum for a robust FE test; this is what we have for the alts after the SUI/AAVE/NEAR start.
- The FE proxies are price-only (no volume, no funding). Including volume or funding as a proxy could surface a within-entity cross-sectional signal, but that requires the funding/OI data layer which the brief treats as a separate axis (axis-2/3).
