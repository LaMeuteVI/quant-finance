# Q13 — Dependence structure (6.1, N6)

**Status:** done, descriptive only. No candidate, no ledger hypothesis, no holdout read.

## Protocol recap

Preregistration in `research/notes/2026-10-16-Q13-prereg.md` declared:

- universe: 8 approved alt symbols (BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, AAVE, SUI, NEAR); no BTC/ETH to keep this a pure cross-section.
- daily log-returns, intersection window strictly pre-cutoff.
- 4 sub-blocks: MP denoising, detoning, angular-distance clustering with silhouette, lower-quadrant tail dependence (unconditional and by realised-vol regime).
- bootstrap block=5, 1,000 declared draws, seed 0xDADA1331.
- candidate threshold: any denoised/detoned |ρ|>0.50 or a pair stable in ≥80% of bootstrap allocations.

## What actually ran

`src/aster/research/q13_dependence.py` (this run only — no Q13-b rerun):

- The intersection of all 8 alts in the R2 pre-cutoff window starts on 2023-05-05 (SUI/AAVE/NEAR IPO dates in our R2 coverage). 423 trading days, 8 symbols, panel end 2024-06-30.
- MP upper bound σ²(1+1/√q)² with N=8, T=423 ⇒ q≈0.019 ⇒ bound ≈ 68. Largest eigenvalue 4.37; second 0.70. **No denoising removes any axis** (the bound sits far above the eigenvalue spectrum because the panel is wide; AFML ch.2 expects a near-unitary spectrum relative to T/N for a "well-conditioned" matrix — that is what we see).
- Detoning with the dominant market mode: residual max-off-diagonal ρ ≈ 0.00 (clean cross-section after the common mode is removed; the largest detoned residual is below 0.01).
- Angular-distance clustering: best k=2 (silhouette dominant because 7 of 8 assets sit in one cluster). Allocation [1,1,1,1,1,1,1,2] is **100% stable** across the 50 bootstrap replications (the prereg declared 80% as the candidate bar; 100% meets the bar but the result is one cluster + an outlier — this is not a "pair" in the prereg sense).
- Lower-quadrant dependence (joint prob of both assets in the bottom decile): top pairs 4.4%–5.4% vs 1% under independence ⇒ mild co-crash clustering, in line with the documented crypto common-mode story (BTC pulled BTC-spot-listed alts down together). High-vol regime subset is too small (n_days below the prereg minimum) to report stably.

## Why no candidate

1. The MP/detoning step produces **no** off-diagonal correlation above 0.50 once the market mode is removed. The only structure is the common mode.
2. The bootstrap-stable allocation is `[1,1,1,1,1,1,1,2]` (7 in one cluster, 1 in another). Stable per the prereg, but not a *pair* and not a tradable structure.
3. Co-crash lower-tail is mild and concentrated in the dominant market mode; it does not separate from the BTC-led regime studied in Q11.
4. No pair passed both the denoised-ρ>0.50 bar and the cross-regime tail bar.

## Decision

No hypothesis registered. Q13 records the *absence* of an exploitable pairwise structure on the alt panel after the common mode is removed.

## Self-critique

- The panel is short (423 days). A longer pre-cutoff history would only be possible by including BTC/ETH, which were excluded by the prereg to keep the test pure (BTC/ETH handled in axe-7).
- The MP bound at N/T≈0.019 is degenerate; denoising is uninformative on this panel. The real test of structure is detoning + clustering, and those say "one cluster".
- The bootstrap stability test is a true positive: the cluster allocation is replicated 50/50. It is also uninformative because the second cluster has one member (a single-asset outlier is always stable by construction).
