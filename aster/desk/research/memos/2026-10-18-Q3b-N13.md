## 4. The 4 schemas (verified against real Vision archives)

| Layout | Columns | Source |
|--------|---------|--------|
| `monthly/klines/<SYM>/1h/<SYM>-1h-YYYY-MM.zip` | 12 (open_time, open, high, low, close, volume, close_time, quote_volume, count, taker_buy_volume, taker_buy_quote_volume, ignore) | real Vision, header row present, 1640995200000 = 2022-01-01 00:00 UTC |

| `monthly/markPriceKlines/<SYM>/1h/...` | same 12 | real Vision, header row present |
| `daily/metrics/<SYM>/<SYM>-metrics-YYYY-MM-DD.zip` | 8 (create_time, symbol, sum_open_interest, sum_open_interest_value, count_toptrader_long_short_ratio, sum_toptrader_long_short_ratio, count_long_short_ratio, sum_long_short_ratio) | real Vision, header row present |

The kline importer strips the header row by content (`"open_time"` in the first cell) and assigns the column names itself. The metrics importer validates the 8-column schema and raises on mismatch.

## 5. The pipeline integration

`pipeline.run` now materialises two things when `perp_legs` is set:

1. The funding panel (P6) via `materialise_funding_panel`, with the per-8h fixing mask built from `perp_fixing_rate_per_bar(prices.index)`.
2. The perp close panel (Q3b) via `load_perp_close` (Hive → wide panel → `data/perpetuals/<ex>/<SYM>/klines_1h.parquet` + manifest).

The strategy receives the funding panel (F1 v1) **and** the perp close panel (F1v2) via kwargs, with a `TypeError` fallback to the legacy path for strategies that don't accept the new kwargs. The engine receives the same perp close through `perp_close` and the fixing mask through `perp_fixing_mask`, so funding is charged at 00/08/16 UTC only, not on every bar.

A prereg that sets `perp_legs` with no perp close on disk now raises `PipelineEmptyWeightsError` instead of silently passing `perp_close=None` to the engine (which would then raise `ValueError` at the engine's perp-set check).
