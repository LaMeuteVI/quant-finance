# F1v3 memo — Funding carry v3: the carry rule the hypothesis states (R3, 2026-10-19/20)

**Verdict: FALSIFIED on every dev sample tested. Family `H-20261016-002`

## 1. What F1v3 actually changed vs F1v2 v1.1.0

The original F1v2 v1.1.0 trial (`H-20261018-003`, c8b1e88) used a fixed
2 bps/fixing signal threshold against a panel whose unconditional
funding mean is 1.23 bps/fixing (R1 §8 lesson). v3 inverts the rule:

- entry signal = `mean_funding_lookback − amortised_round_trip_cost > 0`
                AND `funding_rate_now > 0`
- exit trigger = `funding_rate_now < 0` OR
                (funding non-positive for `exit_persistence_bars=18`
                consecutive bars — i.e. ≈ 3 days at 4h, 18 days at 1d)
- NO inverse (no spot short): when funding is negative, stay flat.
- `amortised_round_trip_bps_per_fixing = 1.67 bps/fixing` = (spot_fee
  10 + perp_fee 5 + grid 5) × 2 sides / 42 fixings (7-day expected hold).

The strategy file is `src/aster/strategies/funding_carry_dn.py` bumped
from v1.1.0 → v1.3.0. Unit tests: 10 passed (`pytest tests/test_f1v2_funding_carry_dn.py`).

## 4. Why the carry hypothesis fails on free Binance Vision data

Funding on BTC/ETH over 2017-2024 averages ≈1-2 bps/fixing with strong
panel autocorrelation (Q5-V2: 0.785) but **the unconditional mean is
below the amortised round-trip rate** at any reasonable holding period
(1d → 40 bps/bar vs 1-2 bps received; 4h → 1.67 bps/fixing vs 1-2 bps
received). The only window in which the carry clears is when funding
spikes to >40 bps/fixing (mid-2021 top, intermittent short squeezes),
but those are regime-dependent and coincide with the largest
basis/spot drawdowns (worst windows of trial 2: 2021-04 → 2021-05,
2021-02 → 2021-03 — exactly the BTC top + May 2021 crash).

The 2021-05 crash window in trial 2 has a **−25.7 %** return in 30
days. The strategy is *short the perp* and *long the spot* during
that window — the basis leg bleeds. F1v3 cannot filter this out
because the basis-z guard (3σ) only blocks *entries*, not *holdings*.

## 6. What would change the verdict (ORANGE, not RED)

  the same horizon is not available pre-cutoff; this is a paid-data
  question.
- **Different amortisation model**: charging the round-trip only
  on the **first entry** of a multi-day hold (not amortised per
  fixing) would change the cushion to a flat 30-40 bps threshold and
  require funding > 40 bps/fixing sustained. Trial 2's funding drag
  is −17 %/an which *would* clear that, but the 2021-05 crash
  blows up before the carry compounds.
