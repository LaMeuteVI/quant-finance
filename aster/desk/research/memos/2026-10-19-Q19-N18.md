## Lessons

1. A positive CAGR is not enough: DSR, costs and capacity must all
   pass jointly. F3 netted +12.6 %/year but failed DSR 0.95 and had
   0 USD capacity at Sharpe net >= 1.
2. Single-asset, single-direction tsmom is not enough (H-20261013-001
   was net-positive but DD -41.6 %). Diversification, shorting and vol
   targeting moved F3's DD inside -35 % but did not lift the Sharpe to
   0.8.
3. Funding persistence is real but the carry is too small to overcome
   the variance of the spot leg (F1v2) and the cost of rebalancing
   (F1v2, F2, F3 all have cost drag 3-7 %/year).
4. The `crowding-premium` family directionally loses: the OI-agreement
   short signal fires in trending basis regimes where the basis
   continues to rise instead of mean-reverting.
