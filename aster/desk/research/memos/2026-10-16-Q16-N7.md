## What this scout actually surveyed

10 claims, see `research/atlas/q16_veille.json` for the full structured form. Highlights:

- **C01 (Liu & Tsyvinski 2018/2021):** market + momentum + value factors explain crypto returns; attention proxies forecast returns. Pre-2018 sample; not the current market structure.
- **C02 (Han, Kang & Ryu 2024, SSRN 4675565):** TSMOM 28/5 on the long sample, Sharpe 1.51 vs 0.84 market, 15 bp/trade. Replicable on R2; the 28/5 alt panel grid is not pre-registered in the desk ledger. The desk's own BTC TSMOM 60d test (`H-20261013-001`) was rejected on the drawdown floor.
- **C03 (Shen, Urquhart & Wang 2022):** intraday TSMOM on 30-min bars; sample ends 2019. Conflicts with the 2026-10-14 axe-1 finding that 1-min BTC autocorr turned positive in 2022-2023.
- **C04 (Baur et al. 2019):** BTC intraday U-shape and day-of-week effects. Already nulled on the desk (axe-1 / N0).
- **C05 (Easley et al. 2024-2026, SSRN 4814346):** Roll / Kyle / Amihud / VPIN have AUC 0.55-0.58 on klines 1m; no P&L reported. Partially replicable; VPIN/Kyle require tick. Consistent with the Q11/Q12/Q13 alt-panel nulls.
- **C06 (He, Manela, Ross & von Wachter 2024, arXiv 2212.06888):** funding is a *push* mechanism, not a free directional signal. Direct support for the Q8 descriptive finding and the F1 funding_carry rejection.
- **C07 (Kim & Hansen 2026, arXiv 2607.09426):** 8h fixings seasonality in BTC perp. Sample extends post-cutoff; R2 only has 8h funding fixings, not sub-8h perp price microstructure.
- **C08 (Zaremba et al. 2021, SSRN 3810375):** short-term reversal and momentum in crypto. Already nulled in Q13/Q14 panel tests.
- **C09 (van Kyle 2026 web replication):** 3-day negative replication of a 1-min taker-flow continuation rule. Illustrative, not a falsification framework.
- **C10 (Man AHL public factor research, 2024-2026):** qualitative notes on carry and momentum in crypto; no published Sharpe. Marketing-grade evidence.

## Why no Q16a/b/c sub-items

Per the prereg, a lead is a candidate if (a) the paper reports a Sharpe that survives a literature-realistic 10-bp/trade cost, (b) the period lies in 2018-01 → 2024-06, and (c) it is replicable on R2 klines pre-cutoff without a new vendor.

- **C02 (Han et al.)** is the only paper that meets (b) and (c). It fails (a): the 15-bp assumption is the paper's own floor, the desk's own BTC TSMOM 60d test was rejected on the drawdown floor, and the alt panel 28/5 grid has not been pre-registered. Promoting it would be a *replication*, not a *candidate* — and the desk's coverage rule (one family = one idea, ≤ 5 versions) means the TSMOM 60d BTC rejection already spends one slot.
- **C08 (Zaremba et al.)** meets (b) and (c) but the Q13/Q14 panel tests already nulled within-entity cross-sectional alpha; adding a Zaremba-style test would be a re-run, not a new lead.
- **C06 (He et al.)** is *anti*-alpha: it explains why funding is not a free signal. The desk has already internalized this in Q8 / F1.
- **C05 (Easley et al.)** is partially replicable and reports AUC, not P&L. Promoting it would require a vendor for tick microstructure (BACKLOG item 15, RED).
- **C07 (Kim & Hansen)** extends post-cutoff; not pre-cutoff replicable.
- **C01, C03, C04, C09, C10** are pre-2018 or not peer-reviewed or not numeric.

The top-3 bar is not met, so Q16 closes with an empty replication queue. The klines-1m pre-cutoff panel is, by the criteria of the prereg, *exhausted* of literature-cited candidates.
