**Cutoff**: strictly `< 2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout read.

## Sample and method

- Five materialised symbols: BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB and XRP.
- Four available timeframes: 5m, 15m, 1h and 4h. The 1m grid was not fabricated because the current R2 bridge does not materialise it.
- Four directed lags: 1, 2, 5 and 15 bars.
- Raw returns and a residual robustness grid. For the residual grid, each alt return is residualised on contemporaneous BTC and ETH returns; BTC/ETH leader columns remain observable controls and are not residualised to zero.
- 24 directed pairs × 4 lags × 4 timeframes × 2 variants = **640 cells**.
- 100 deterministic moving-block bootstrap draws per cell, block length 24 bars; BH-FDR over the complete 640-cell grid.
- `effect_bps` is the correlation scaled by the follower return standard deviation, not a tradable P&L estimate. Cost hurdle is the **minimum** 100k-USDT round-trip cost over UTC hours from `research/atlas/cost-grid.csv`, therefore optimistic.

## Results (from the JSON artefact)

- Raw grid: 104/320 joint survivors (32.5%); 10 positive and 94 negative. Only 9 had a common sign across calendar half-years. Maximum absolute effect: 18.45 bps, versus an optimistic BTC/alt cost hurdle of 56.77–85.61 bps.
- Residual grid: **59/320 joint survivors (18.4%)**; 15 positive and 44 negative. Only 5 had a common sign across calendar half-years. Maximum absolute effect: 10.33 bps, versus an optimistic cost hurdle of 44.76–85.61 bps.
- **Zero** joint survivors exceeded the cost hurdle; **zero** survived both cost and half-year sign stability.
- The largest raw magnitudes were concentrated at 4h lag 1 and were negative (for example BTC→SOL −18.45 bps); they were not stable across half-years and remained below cost.

## Preregistered decision

The prediction that fewer than 5% of residual cells would survive BH-FDR is **falsified**: 18.4% survived the joint BH + bootstrap screen. This is not evidence of a tradable lead-lag edge because the survivors are predominantly small, negative, unstable and below costs. The result is consistent with common-flow / asynchronous-bar effects rather than an investable directional mechanism.

The Q7 falsification rule therefore closes this descriptive class as **no candidate**. No ledger hypothesis and no backtest are authorised from this grid. A future Q7 V2 would need explicit frozen-minute handling, non-overlapping calendar alignment and an independent venue check before any strategy registration.
