# Q9 — N2 per-alt intraday descriptive grid (memo V1)

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Artefact**: `research/atlas/q9_intraday_n2.json` (38,779 bytes).
**Preregistration**: `research/notes/2026-10-16-Q9-prereg.md`.
**Code/tests**: `src/aster/research/q9_intraday_n2.py`,
`tests/test_q9_intraday_n2.py` (4 targeted tests; full suite green).
**Cutoff**: all inputs are local R2 spot 1m data strictly before
`2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout was read.

## Sample and grid

The fixed grid is 10 symbols × 5 timeframes = **50 cells**:
BNBUSDT, XRPUSDT, SOLUSDT, LINKUSDT, DOGEUSDT, XMRUSDT, AAVEUSDT,
FETUSDT, NEARUSDT and SUIUSDT; 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h and 1d. Bars are built
causally from the local R2 1m store. Frozen minutes are retained, as declared
in the preregistration; this is a descriptive map, not a strategy.

## Findings

- Lag-1 return autocorrelation is negative in **43/50 cells**; mean
  `-0.0302`. By timeframe: 5m 10/10, 15m 8/10, 1h 10/10, 4h 8/10,
  1d 7/10.
- Lag-1 absolute-return autocorrelation is positive in **50/50 cells**;
  mean `+0.3116`. The effect declines with horizon: +0.3794 (5m),
  +0.3674 (15m), +0.3163 (1h), +0.2762 (4h), +0.2185 (1d).
- Hurst estimates range from **0.5780 to 0.6527** where defined, with
  49/50 finite values. The SUI 1d cell has insufficient effective sample
  for this estimator and is reported as NaN, not imputed.
- Variance-ratio and longer-lag fields are in the JSON artefact. No p-value
  or single-cell significance is promoted; the family is 50 descriptive
  cells and the result remains exploratory.

## Falsification verdict

The preregistered generalisation is supported descriptively: both negative
one-bar dependence (86% of cells) and positive volatility clustering (100%
of cells) recur across the alt panel. This is not evidence of a tradable
mean-reversion signal. The result is compatible with bid/ask bounce,
bar-construction effects and clustered volatility, all of which must survive
the cost screen and a causal strategy backtest before any ledger hypothesis.

The Q9 V1 output creates **no strategy candidate**. It is a map feeding Q10
(volatility) and Q12 (flow), not a permission to run a mean-reversion
backtest. The gross effect is a correlation statistic, not bps per trade;
capacity and net Sharpe are therefore unknown.

## Limitations

- Frozen minutes were retained by preregistration; no causal claim is made
  that the return autocorrelation is order-flow alpha.
- The Q5 rescaled-range Hurst estimator has finite-sample bias; the one NaN
  is preserved.
- The universe is a surviving panel, with XMR retained as a delisting guard;
  results remain a survivorship-bounded description.
- A future strategy requires a separate preregistration, `desk backtest`,
  trial accounting, costs, walk-forward/PBO and capacity. The holdout stays
  sealed.

**Verdict: Q9 DONE as descriptive N2 coverage; no ledger hypothesis.**
