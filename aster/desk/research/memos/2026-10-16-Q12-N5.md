# Q12 — N5 intraday flow per alt

**Status: descriptive closure; no trading candidate.**

- Preregistration: `research/notes/2026-10-16-Q12-prereg.md`.
- Artefact: `research/atlas/q12_intraday_flow.json`.
- Cutoff: strict exclusive `2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout read.
- Universe: eight approved alt symbols with local 1m R2 data: BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, AAVE, SUI and NEAR.
- Grid: 8 symbols × 4 flow features × 4 horizons (1/5/15/60 minutes) = **128 cells**. The run used deterministic capped samples of at most 5,000 observations per cell and 50 circular block-bootstrap draws to keep the descriptive computation bounded; this is not a full-history claim.

## Findings

- **5/128** cells were nominal (`p<0.01` and bootstrap interval excluding zero).
- The largest displayed gross proxy among nominal cells was approximately **3.29 bps**, computed as `|rho| × sigma_forward × 10,000`; this is a diagnostic proxy, not P&L.
- **0/128** cells survived the declared cost hurdle; `candidate=false`.
- No ledger hypothesis was registered.

The nominal cells are small, weak rank correlations and do not establish a causal tradable flow edge. The cap and reduced bootstrap count are explicitly reported limitations. A strategy would require a new preregistration, full declared sample, conservative costs/capacity and `desk backtest` trial accounting.

Methods: `quant-intraday-microstructure-research`, Chan ch. 6, AFML ch. 2/19, `quant-verification`.
