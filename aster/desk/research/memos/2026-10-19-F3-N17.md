## Interpretation

Diversification + shorting + volatility targeting improved the specific
H-20261013-001 failure mode: max drawdown moved inside the -35% floor.
However, the net Sharpe did not clear 0.8 and the DSR did not clear 0.95
once the existing family trial was counted. The improvement is therefore
not sufficient for promotion. Turnover and cost drag remain material.

This is a useful negative result: drawdown control alone did not create a
capacity-bearing edge. The strategy has positive CAGR but fails the desk's
joint return, uncertainty and capacity standard.

## Lessons

1. Keep the family trial count at 2; do not reset DSR by renaming the family.
2. Vol targeting helped DD but did not lift the net Sharpe above the gate.
3. A positive CAGR is not enough: DSR, costs, capacity and drawdown must all
   pass jointly.
4. No holdout, paper or live action is justified.
