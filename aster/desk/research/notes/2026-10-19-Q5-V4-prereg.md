**Month-end and turn-of-month** are still open in §3 seasonality list. The classic Turn-of-Month effect (Hernandez et al., 2001; Ktirioglu 2019 on equities) shows positive abnormal returns in the last 3 trading days of the month and the first 3 of the next month. There's no consensus on crypto (small samples, mixed evidence).

## Methodology (pre-registered)

### Universe and pre-cutoff

- Spot klines `data/ohlcv/binance/{BTC-USDT, ETH-USDT, BNB-USDT, SOL-USDT, XRP-USDT}/{1d, 4h}.parquet`.
- Cutoff 2024-07-01. **No new download.**
- Timeframes: 1d (15 040 bars total before filter), 4h (75 045 bars). For 4h we use **calendar month** boundaries in UTC.

### Definitions

- **Last-N-bars of month (LOM):** the last `N` bars where bar index is in the last `N` of its calendar month (bar.date ≥ month_calendar_end - N).
- **First-N-bars of next month (FON):** the first `N` bars where bar.date.month differs from bar.date.shift(1).month.
- **Mid-month placebo:** bars at day 8 to day 23 of the month, exclusive.
- Primary `N = 3`. Placebo on `N ∈ {2, 5}`.

### Test

For each (symbol, timeframe, N, side) cell, compute mean `fwd_1` (next-bar return after LOM/FON events). The fwd_1 is `close[t+1] / close[t] - 1`. We do **not** simulate costs in Q5-V4; this is descriptive.

Two BH-FDR families (q=0.05):
- `fwd_LOM` family = 5 symbols × 2 TF × 3 N values = 30 cells.
- `fwd_FON` family = same 30 cells.

### Hypotheses

- **H1 (LOM positive):** `fwd_LOM[1d, N=3] > 0` for ≥ 4/5 symbols with BH retention on the LOM family.
- **H2 (FON positive):** `fwd_FON[1d, N=3] > 0` for ≥ 4/5 symbols with BH retention on the FON family.
- **H3 (asymmetry):** `fwd_LOM + fwd_FON` (the 6-bar window turn-of-month) is positive after cost-free descriptive at 1d for ≥ 4/5 symbols. The "asymmetry" cell is the actual turn-of-month phenomenon.

### Verdict

- **RETAIN** if H1 ∧ H2 ∧ H3.
- **FALSIFIED** if all 3 fail.
- **NEUTRAL** otherwise.

### Guard-rails

- Descriptive only. No ledger hypothesis.
- No new download.
- No live / paper / holdout / order / key.
- BH-FDR is the only statistical correction (no Bonferroni, no bootstrap — N is large enough for t-test given 30 cells × 5 symbols).
