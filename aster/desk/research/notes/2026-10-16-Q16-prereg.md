### Pre-declared top-3 candidate replication leads (if any)

After the digest is written, exactly the top 3 leads by *ladder + replication feasibility on the existing data* (not by raw reported Sharpe, which is the publication-bias trap) are appended to QUEUE.md as new sub-items (`Q16a`, `Q16b`, `Q16c`) with `READY` status and a one-line preregistration pointer. Each sub-item is exploratory; it does **not** register a ledger hypothesis.

### Falsification / null

- A "candidate replication lead" is one that (a) can be implemented on the existing pre-cutoff klines panel without a new data vendor, (b) the paper reports a Sharpe that survives a literature-realistic 10-bp-per-trade cost (i.e. a real cost, not the 15-bp Han et al. literature floor), and (c) the paper's period lies in 2018-01 → 2024-06.
- If zero of the surveyed papers meet all three bars, Q16 still closes as `DONE` with `top3=[]` and the digest explains why (publication bias, single-period sample, etc.).
