# Q3c prereg — perp prices for ETH and liquid alts (2026-10-19, R2)

## 1. Statement

Import perp market data (UM futures) for ETHUSDT, BNBUSDT, SOLUSDT, XRPUSDT,
DOGEUSDT, LINKUSDT into `data/perpetuals/binance/<SYM>/<stream>.parquet`,
pre-cutoff (`2024-07-01T00:00:00+00:00` exclusive).

Streams required per symbol:

- `klines_1h` (mark-candle 1h) — required for `perp_close`
- `premium_index_klines_1h` — required by F2 (funding basis)
- `mark_price_klines_1h` — required by F2 (mark vs last)
- `metrics` (daily OI + long/short ratio) — required by Q5-V2 and F2 audit

## 2. Mechanism

`data.binance.vision` provides daily ZIPs:

- `https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/monthly/klines/<SYM>/1h/<SYM>-1h-YYYY-MM.zip`
- `https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/monthly/premiumIndexKlines/<SYM>/1h/<SYM>-1h-YYYY-MM.zip`
- `https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/monthly/markPriceKlines/<SYM>/1h/<SYM>-1h-YYYY-MM.zip`
- `https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/daily/metrics/<SYM>/<SYM>-metrics-YYYY-MM-DD.zip`

These are free, no key, no quota, no venue risk. The downloader
`aster.data.perp_data` already enforces the cutoff, drops duplicates, and
writes a manifest per partition.

## 3. Falsification

If any of the 6 symbols cannot be sourced from `data.binance.vision`
(pre-cutoff) for any of the 4 streams (no archive, archive empty after
cutoff, archive crosses the cutoff), Q3c is **rejected** for that symbol/stream
pair. Coverage for R5 (12-symbol universe) is conditional on Q3c fully
succeeding. Per `aster-autopilot` rule "the programme must not invent a
silent F-hypothesis": if a symbol/stream is unfalsifiable-by-coverage,
drop it from R5's universe and document the drop in DECISIONS as ORANGE.
