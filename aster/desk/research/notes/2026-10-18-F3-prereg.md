# F3 preregistration — Vol-targeted trend ensemble on the panel

**Date** : 2026-10-18
**Family** : `btc-daily-tsmom` (existing — adds trial #2 after H-20261013-001 reject)
**Strategy** : `vol_target_trend_ensemble` (v1.0.0)
**Market** : crypto-spot, **perps** allowed
**Timeframe** : 1d

## Why F3 exists

H-20261013-001 (BTC 60d long-only tsmom) was net-positive in dev but failed the
drawdown floor (max DD = -41.6 % vs gate -35 %). The single-asset, long-only
version was exposed to BTC-specific blow-up windows (May 2021, Jun 2022, Nov 2022).

F3 differs in three measurable ways:

1. **Diversification** : 8–10 assets (BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE, AVAX, LINK, DOT).
   The XMR (survivorship guard) is held in cash if the venue lacks data.
2. **Shorting** : long AND short perps. The trend signal is `sign(rolling_mean(ret, N))`.
3. **Volatility targeting** : 10 % annual vol target per asset, capped at 1.5× gross.
   This explicitly dampens exposure during the high-vol windows that caused
   H-20261013-001 to bust its drawdown floor.

The trials count in the existing `btc-daily-tsmom` family (1 prior trial from
H-20261013-001).

## Mechanism

Multi-horizon trend following on the cross-section of crypto perps. The signal
on each asset is the sign of a 20–120 day average return, with a faster
re-entry rule (re-enter when the 5-day return agrees). The book is vol-targeted
to a 10 % annual budget per asset and the gross is capped at 1.5×.

This is the same idea as H-20261013-001 but with diversification (uncorrelated
assets cap the joint drawdown) and shorting (extends the signal to negative
trend). The vol target caps the position size at 0.5× in the 2022-06 and
2022-11 windows where H-20261013-001 lost 41 %.

## Hypotheses

**H-primary** (v1.0.0) : Diversified long/short perps, vol-targeted 10 %,
3-lookback blend (20/60/120d), brief universe (8 assets), gross cap 1.5×.

**H-alt-1** (v1.1.0 if v1 fails) : Drop the 20d lookback, keep 60/120d blend;
shorter lookbacks add noise in crypto.

**H-alt-2** (v1.2.0 if both fail) : Long-only on the same panel; re-test the
diversification hypothesis without the short leg.

## Cost bar (per Samuel, commit 53325ae)

- Perp VIP 0 taker = 5 bps/side, base case
- Spread + impact grid on top (P4 cost model)
- ×2 and ×3 are stress, not base

## Guard-rails

- No look-ahead. The trend signals use rolling means with `min_periods` and
  the engine applies a `lag=1` shift.
- No post-cutoff data. Universe and dev sample are pre-2024-07-01.
- No live, no key, no order, no paper.
- No holdout read. Holdout is for after the dev verdict.
- Family `btc-daily-tsmom` has 1 prior trial (H-20261013-001 rejected on DD).
