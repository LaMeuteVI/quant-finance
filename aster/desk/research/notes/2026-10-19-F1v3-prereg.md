# F1v3 prereg — the carry rule the hypothesis actually states (R3, 2026-10-19)

Family: `H-20261016-002` (trial count continues from F1v2 v1.1.0).

## 1. Statement

Crypto perpetual funding rates are persistent on horizons of hours to
days. A delta-neutral **long spot / short perp** position earns the
funding minus amortised costs when the predicted funding is positive,
and the reverse when negative. The edge is the **persistence of the
funding rate after the round-trip amortisation is paid back**, not the
average level of funding.

F1v2 v1.1.0 (trial H-20261018-003) used a **fixed 2 bps/fixing** signal
threshold against a panel whose unconditional mean is 1.23 bps/fixing,
which is exactly the bug §8 of `2026-10-18-F1v2-N15.md` documents.
F1v3 enters only when `mean_funding_lookback − amortised_round_trip_cost > 0`,
with **hysteresis** (exit when funding goes negative, not when it drops
back below the entry threshold), to avoid paying a round-trip on every
churn.

## 2. Mechanism

Funding rate (perp) is paid by the long to the short every 8h (Binance
fixing at 00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC). On a delta-neutral long spot /
short perp, the carry equals the funding rate received each fixing,
minus the per-fix amortised cost of the round-trip paid at entry.

Inputs:

- `mean_funding_lookback` = trailing mean of perp funding rate over 42
  bars (7 days × 6 fixings/day at 4h, but the 1h bar layout keeps the
  lookback in hours — `yield × 42` bars).
- `amortised_round_trip_cost` = (spot 20 bps + perp 10 bps + grid 5 bps)
  / expected_holding_fixings. We use expected holding = 42 fixings
  (= 7 days), giving **2.14 bps/fixing** per leg per side; conservative.
- `expected_funding` = `mean_funding_lookback` (causal, no look-ahead).

Signal: `signal_t = mean_funding_lookback_t − amortised_round_trip_cost`.
Position: `target_t ∈ {0, 1}`. Long spot + short perp when in position.

Hysteresis (anti-churn):
- enter (0→1) when `signal_t > 0` AND `funding_rate_t > 0`
- exit (1→0) when `funding_rate_t < 0` OR (funding has been non-positive
  for `exit_persistence_bars` consecutive bars; default 18 bars ≈ 3 days)

This blocks the "funding briefly flips negative" churn that destroys
F1v2.

## 3. Falsification (pre-registered)

F1v3 is **FALSIFIED** if, on the dev sample (pre-cutoff, BTC + ETH
panel equal-risk, panel equal-risk = 0.5 BTC + 0.5 ETH on a 4h bar
layout, lag 1 bar, max gross 1.0):

- Sharpe_ann < 0.8 (gate `config/desk.yaml`) — OR
- max_drawdown < −0.35 — OR
- DSR < 0.95 — OR
- stress Sharpe (costs × 2) < 0.3 — OR
- the carry edge disappears on BTC alone (single-leg robustness),
  i.e. dev Sharpe_ann on BTC alone < 0.5.

F1v3 is **RETAINED** only if all gates pass on the dev sample, and
the BTC-alone Sharpe_ann ≥ 0.5.

If F1v3 is falsified, the carry idea closes **without appeal** (the
family H-20261016-002 has used 4 trials: v1.0.0 / v1.1.0 / v1.2.0 /
v1.3.0). The carry hypothesis on this 4h BTC/ETH panel does not pay
for its round-trip costs.

## 4. Numeric prediction (written BEFORE the backtest)

Best-guess point estimates (based on F1v2 v1.1.0 + Q5-V2 funding
autocorr 0.785 panel observation):

- BTC-alone dev Sharpe_ann : 0.40 to 0.80 (median 0.55)
- ETH-alone dev Sharpe_ann : 0.30 to 0.70 (median 0.45)
- panel equal-risk Sharpe_ann : 0.50 to 1.00 (median 0.70)
- turnover_ann : 4 to 12 (target ~6, vs F1v2's 18.26)
- cost_pnl_ann : +1.0 % to +2.0 %/y (target +1.5 %)
- funding_pnl_ann : +1.0 % to +3.5 %/y (target +2.5 %; positive,
  because we now only sit in positive-funding regimes)
- max_drawdown : −0.10 to −0.20
- time_in_market : 0.20 to 0.40

If realised Sharpe_ann is below the lower bound of the prediction
range, F1v3 is FALSIFIED even if the gate clears; the prediction is
the falsification bar.

## 5. Decision protocol

- Sharpe_ann < 0.5 → falsified (panel under-performs single-leg BTC).
- 0.5 ≤ Sharpe_ann < 0.8 → falsified by gate; the family is exhausted.
- Sharpe_ann ≥ 0.8, all other gates pass → retained.
- Sharpe_ann ≥ 0.8 but realised < lower-bound of prediction → reported
  as falsified-by-coverage: the carry hypothesis survives but the panel
  + rule don't pay. ORANGE to DECISIONS, not RED.
