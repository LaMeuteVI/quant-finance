# Q14 preregistration — Panel and relative value (6.3)

**Written before the Q14 data run.**

- Cutoff: strict exclusive `2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout.
- Universe: the approved 8-asset alt panel (BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, AAVE, SUI, NEAR) intersected with what R2 actually carries. No BTC, no ETH (the BTC/ETH study is in axe-7). XMR is delisted in R2; treated as the survivorship-bias guardrail — only used to verify that a candidate that is *not* present pre-delisting does not exist.
- Frequency: daily log-returns on the intersection window (panel start 2023-05-05, panel end 2024-06-30) plus, for the four approved "sector pairs" that exist for the full 2021 window, a secondary 1h-bar residual test on 2022-01-01 → 2024-06-30.

### Sub-blocks (pre-declared)

1. **Panel fixed-effects** (within-entity, within-time demean). For each pre-declared signal category (Q9 N2 lag-1 autocorrelation, Q10 HAR-RV forecast residual, Q12 flow imbalance z-score), test whether the signal explains daily cross-sectional returns after entity and time fixed effects. Bootstrap block=5, 1,000 draws, seed `0xD00DB00B`. BH-FDR over the signal family.
2. **Sector pairs**, four pre-declared:
   - L1: SOL/NEAR, SOL/SUI, NEAR/SUI (Ethereum-competitor L1).
   - Payments: XRP/DOGE.
   - DeFi: AAVE/LINK.
   - Each pair: log-spread on the longest available pre-cutoff window; ADF (H0=unit root) and KPSS (H0=stationary) on the spread; half-life estimated from AR(1) on the spread; 1h-bar intraday residual mean reversion.
3. **Cross-sectional momentum / reversal on residuals**: at horizons 1h, 4h, 1d, 1w. Residual = panel after detone (Q13) and after a 22-day moving average. Ranking by the pre-declared cross-sectional signal; quintile long/short spread; BH-FDR over the horizon grid.

### Falsification

- A sector pair is a candidate only if both the ADF rejects at 1% and the KPSS fails to reject at 5% (i.e. the spread is mean-reverting) **and** the half-life is between 1 trading day and 30 trading days, **and** the intraday residual mean-reversion is significant at p<0.01 after BH over horizons.
- A cross-sectional signal is a candidate only if the BH-surviving horizon shows an L/S Sharpe > 1 (ann.) with a bootstrap CI excluding zero.

### Constraints

- No data download. No holdout read. No live, paper, order, or key. No modification of `risk/`, `execution/`, `policies/`, or `config/risk_limits.yaml`.
- 5 strategy-version budget per hypothesis. The panel test, the four sector pairs, and the cross-sectional grid are exploratory — none is registered as a ledger hypothesis.
- Artefact: `research/atlas/q14_panel.json`. Memo: `research/memos/2026-10-16-Q14-N6.md`. Tests: `tests/test_q14_panel.py`.
- If the run does not meet its candidate bar, mark Q14 `DONE` with `candidate=false` and the reason.
