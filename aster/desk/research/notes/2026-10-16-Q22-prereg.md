- Source: free public Binance Vision (`data.binance.vision`). **No new vendor, no key, no order, no holdout, no live, no paper, no spend.**

## Why V3

Q21 V2 falsified the **1-minute** BTC cost hurdle: signed flow imbalance Spearman = +0.130 (real), but Q4 − Q0 return = +2.05 bps gross, **net of 3× taker = −3.95 bps**. The signal is real, the per-trade cost is fixed (~6 bps round-trip conservative), and at 1-minute bar size the signal is below the cost.

V3 tests the natural extension:
1. **5-minute and 15-minute bars** (same BTC data already on disk). The signed flow imbalance accumulates over 5×/15× more trades per bar, so the bar-level impact grows while the cost per trade is unchanged ⇒ the net improves.
2. **ETH leg** (free Binance Vision aggTrades, 2022-01 + 2022-02, ≤ 700 MB raw, **stream-and-discard**: download one ZIP, import, delete the ZIP, never accumulate > 1 GB on disk).

## Scope

- (a) Re-run Q21 observables on the existing BTCUSDT 4 partitions at `5min` and `15min` bars. Expected bar counts: 5min ≈ 35 000, 15min ≈ 12 000.
- (b) Stream-download ETHUSDT aggTrades 2022-01 and 2022-02 from `https://data.binance.vision/data/spot/monthly/aggTrades/ETHUSDT/ETHUSDT-aggTrades-2022-01.zip` and `-02.zip` (≤ 700 MB raw combined). Each ZIP is deleted after `aster.data.aggtrades.import_archive` writes its Parquet + manifest.
- (c) Re-run Q21 observables on ETH at 1m, 5m, 15m.
- (d) Cross-asset signed flow lead-lag BTC↔ETH at 5m and 15m.

## Hypotheses

- **H-20261022-001 (extends H-20261016-005)**: BTCUSDT signed flow imbalance at 5m/15m bar size has Q4 − Q0 return > 6 bps gross (3× taker hurdle) ⇒ tradable net of cost.
  - Falsification: Q4 − Q0 ≤ 6 bps at both 5m and 15m.
- **H-20261022-002 (extends H-20261016-006)**: ETH follows BTC signed flow at 5m lead-lag with ρ > 0.05.
  - Falsification: |ρ| ≤ 0.05 at all lags ≤ 5 bars.

## Falsification bar (joint)

A signal is a candidate only if **all three** are true:
1. Spearman of `signed_flow_t` vs `ret_{t+1}` has the expected sign AND |ρ| > 0.05 (causal link, not noise).
2. Q4 − Q0 gross return > 6 bps (3× taker hurdle).
3. Placebo: shuffle the trade order within bar ⇒ Q4 − Q0 collapses to ≤ 1 bp. (Sanity check that the signal lives in real signed flow, not bar-end timing.)

If the joint filter fails at 5m and 15m on both BTC and ETH, V3 has no candidate.
