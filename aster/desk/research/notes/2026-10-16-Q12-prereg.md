# Q12 preregistration — N5 intraday flow per alt

**Written before the Q12 data run.**

- Cutoff: strict exclusive `2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout.
- Universe: all approved symbols with local 1m R2 data: BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, AAVE, SUI, NEAR plus BTC/ETH controls where available. Missing inventory is reported, never substituted.
- Bars: fixed 1m time bars from active minutes; no forward fill. A deterministic 10% sample is allowed only for a declared runtime-bounded aggTrades extension; its exact partitions and bar count will be recorded.
- Features: taker buy ratio imbalance, signed quote flow, volume shock, trade-count shock, Amihud and Roll; horizons 1/5/15/60 minutes and 1/4/12 hours where data supports them.
- Grid: symbol × feature × horizon × direction, with raw and BTC-residualised returns where controls exist. BH-FDR is applied across the complete predeclared grid.
- Uncertainty: circular moving-block bootstrap, block length equal to max(5, horizon bars), deterministic seed; calendar half-year sign stability.
- Placebo: one-period shifted flow/target control with explicit causal alignment; inverse direction included.
- Economic hurdle: gross effect in basis points must exceed the conservative cost table before a cell can be considered a candidate. No annualisation of selected conditional means.
- Falsification: zero cost-and-stability survivors, or effect below costs, closes Q12 as descriptive with no ledger hypothesis. A survivor would require a fresh ledger preregistration and counted `desk backtest`; this Q item itself never registers a strategy.
- Artefacts: `research/atlas/q12_intraday_flow.json`, `research/memos/2026-10-16-Q12-N5.md`; targeted tests under `tests/test_q12_intraday_flow.py`.
