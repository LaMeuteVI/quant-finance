## Scope (V2, scoped down)

**(a) Spot aggTrades extension — on disk already: BTCUSDT 2022-01..04.** Expansion (if needed): BTCUSDT 2022-05..08 (4 months), ETHUSDT 2022-01..04 (4 months). **Phase A constraint**: only download if the existing BTC 4-month sample fails the preregistered power check (see below).

**(b) Microstructure observables** (deterministic, no look-ahead):
- `tick_rule`: signed trade sign per `is_buyer_maker`.
- `signed_volume` per bar.
- `signed_flow_imbalance` (sbf / volume) per bar.
- `Kyle_lambda`: OLS on `ΔP` against `signed_flow` per N-tick bucket (bar-level).
- `Amihud`: |return| / dollar volume.
- `Roll_spread`: 2·√(−Cov(ΔP_t, ΔP_{t−1})).
- `Lee_Ready`: classify trades buy/sell by quote mid; needs L1. **If L1 not present, use tick-rule as the buy/sell proxy** (the standard fallback, properly cited).
- `VPIN`: bucket volume into N=50 equal-volume buckets, |buy − sell| / total volume per bucket, average over rolling window.

**(c) Perp leg (basis, premium)**: use the **already-imported** 12-symbol funding panel (`data/r2/market/futures/um/funding_rate/`, 538 partitions, Q3 import). No new download. The basis requires mark-price klines; mark-price klines are deferred to V3 (out of Q21 scope; will be a separate QUEUE item if Q21 produces a signal that needs it).

**(d) Cross-asset signed-flow lead-lag** at 1m / 5m / 15m on BTCUSDT × ETHUSDT. Spearman + bootstrap CI; placebo: shift the target by one bar. BH-FDR over the 3-horizon × {BTC, ETH, BTC-ETH} family (≤ 9 cells ⇒ no BH needed but recorded).

## Hypotheses

- **H-20261016-005 — BTCUSDT signed-flow imbalance has a one-bar predictive content on 1m returns after costs.**
  Mechanism: temporary order-flow pressure that mean-reverts (Easley-O'Hara microstructure). Falsification: Spearman ρ insignificant after BH, or sign of effect inconsistent with hypothesis, or effect < 1 bp/bar after 3× taker cost. Numeric prediction: |ρ| ∈ [0.02, 0.05], positive return following buy pressure, **net of 3× cost = 0**.
- **H-20261016-006 — ETHUSDT follows BTCUSDT signed flow at 5m lead-lag.**
  Mechanism: information diffusion through the spot BTC→ETH channel. Falsification: lead-lag ρ < 0.01 or sign-reversed at 5m. Numeric prediction: ρ ≈ 0.03, ETH follows BTC by 1 bar.

## Falsification bar

- Sample: pre-cutoff 2022-01..04 BTC (already on disk) + 2022-05..08 BTC (if needed) + 2022-01..04 ETH (if needed).
- Block bootstrap (block = max(50, h), 50 draws) on the per-bar metric; CI excludes zero ⇒ survives.
- Placebo: shuffle trade order within bar ⇒ null.
- Cost: 3× taker proxy (10 bps/side + √impact, conservative) ⇒ net must be positive in 95 % CI.
- If **none** of (a) signed-flow imbalance, (b) Kyle λ slope, (c) Amihud-vs-return, (d) cross-asset flow lead-lag, (e) VPIN asymmetry → next-bar return is significant net of cost, the test fails ⇒ no candidate.
