## Cost-decisive verdicts that need re-reading under C1

Only the **btc-daily-tsmom** family verdicts depend materially on the grid
(because the trade is spot/perp spread + impact). The funding-carry and
crowding verdicts depend on funding/premium, not on the spread estimate, and
are NOT re-read.

Re-read scope:
- **H-20261019-001** (F3 v1.0 on 5 majors) — costs were decisive on drawdown.
- **H-20261020-005** (F3 v1.1 on 12-symbol R2 panel) — costs were decisive on DSR/DD.
- **H-20261013-001** (BTC daily 60d long-only) — costs were decisive on Sharpe sign.

For each, a single new dev trial is run under the C1 grid, preregistered as
a fresh hypothesis so the family trial count increases honestly and the DSR
gets recomputed. Each re-read is one **counted trial**.

## What is NOT re-read

- F1v3 verdicts (`H-20261020-002`, `H-20261020-003`) — funding edge, costs not decisive.
- F1, F1v2 verdicts — funding carry, costs secondary.
- F2 (`H-20261018-005`) — premium/crowding edge, costs tertiary.
- Q21/Q22 microstructure — those did not consume the grid for a Sharpe
  calculation; they are exploratory diagnostics.

## Decision

The btc-daily-tsmom family is closed under C2 (`H-20261020-016`): net
edge exists at the strategy level (Sharpe 0.901 under C1) but the
Deflated Sharpe is below the dev threshold (DSR 0.926 < 0.95) with
n=16 family trials. Re-reading H-20261019-001 and H-20261013-001
would push that count higher and is unlikely to surface a fresh
edge. **Recommended: do not re-read the historical verdicts**;
instead, **close the btc-daily-tsmom family** and open a new one
(e.g. intraday microstructure, perp basis, or lead-lag) for the
next cycle.
