# F1 — Funding carry, delta-neutral (preregistration)

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Item**: F1 (research/QUEUE.md, Fast Track).
**Skills**: `aster-research-loop`, `chan-machine-trading` ch05, `hull-derivatives` ch06.
**Family**: `funding-carry-dn` (new family; the prior BTC-long/cash trend
family is `btc-daily-tsmom` and is unrelated).

**Mechanism**.
Crypto perpetual swaps on Binance USDⓈ-M charge a funding rate every 8
hours that is paid by longs to shorts when positive, and the reverse
when negative. The funding is anchored by the premium of the perp
above the index; the rate is therefore persistent on horizons of hours
to days (Hull ch. 6 on convenience yields; Chan ch. 5 on crypto
funding as a term-structure carry). Leveraged longs pay the carry
that funds the hedge for a delta-neutral market-maker, so a strategy
that systematically **longs spot / shorts perp when funding is
positive** (and the reverse when negative) earns the carry minus
costs. Risks: a sharp crash drives the perp basis negative (perp
discount to spot) AND funding flips sign, so the carry is paid by
both legs simultaneously — this is the basis-blow-out tail that has
to be measured, not papered over.

**Statement**.
On the 4h bar, for each symbol in {BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT}, the
delta-neutral pair (long spot / short perp) earns the predicted
funding rate over the next bar when the predicted funding (7-day
rolling mean) is positive, and the reverse (short spot / long perp)
when the predicted funding is negative. Position size scales with
the expected funding minus a 2 bps cost cushion; a position is held
for at least 1 bar and exited when the expected carry drops below
the cost cushion or the basis z-score exceeds 3 (crash regime).

**Universe / data**.
Spot close: R2 1m → 4h (12 brief symbols, pre-cutoff).
Funding rate: USDⓈ-M monthly Vision, 12 symbols × 66 months (Q3 import).
Perp close: same as spot for the dev sample (the desk treats spot
and perp 1m as identical in pre-cutoff exploration, which is wrong
in real life but bounded — the basis drift is what the funding rate
captures; the spot/perp spread itself is not in the dev sample).

**Falsification**.
The dev run fails (REJECT) if any of:
1. `statistics.dsr` < 0.95 on the dev sample.
2. `base.sharpe_ann` < 0.8 after costs at AUM=1M USD.
3. The ×2 cost stress test (`stress.costs_x2.sharpe_ann`) is below 0.
4. The walk-forward worst-fold Sharpe (`walk_forward.stability.worst_fold_sharpe_ann`)
   is below 0 (the strategy must be profitable in every calendar quarter
   of the dev sample, not just on average).
5. PBO across a small lookback grid (`{3d, 5d, 7d, 10d, 14d}`) is
   above 0.5 (overfit to the dev sample).

**Prediction**.
A first-pass version (7d rolling-mean funding forecast, 4h bars,
1M USD AUM, BTC/ETH/SOL, 2022-01 → 2024-06) will report:
* `sharpe_ann` in [0.8, 1.5] (carry trades are low-Sharpe in
  absolute terms; the desk's threshold is met but no blockbuster).
* `dsr` in [0.90, 0.99] (high autocorrelation of funding makes
  the effective number of trials small).
* `capacity_max_AUM_usd` in [3M, 15M] (BTC and ETH dominate the
  capacity; SOL is small and noisy).
* `walk_forward.stability.worst_fold_sharpe_ann` in [-0.3, +0.3]
  (the carry occasionally flips sign in crashes, so at least one
  fold is allowed to be negative).
