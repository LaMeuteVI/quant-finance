# Q5 — BTC/ETH intraday N2 (preregistration)

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Item**: Q5 (research/QUEUE.md, Block 1).
**Skills**: `tsay-analysis-financial-time-series` ch02/ch05,
`cfa-quantitative-methods` ch09, `lopez-de-prado-afml` ch17-18.
**Family**: `descriptive-intraday-n2` (new family; this is a
descriptive memo, not a hypothesis, so it does not count toward
the Deflated Sharpe denominator for any future hypothesis).

**Statement**.
On BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, returns at horizons {1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h,
1d} exhibit (a) sign-flip autocorrelation patterns that depend on
horizon (1m and 5m mean-revert, 1d trends), (b) a Lo-MacKinlay
variance ratio consistent with the autocorrelation pattern, and
(c) a Hurst exponent that is above 0.5 on 1d and below 0.5 on 1m.

**Universe / data**.
BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT R2 1m klines, 2019-01-01 → 2024-06-30
(cutoff strict). Aggregations to 5m / 15m / 1h / 4h / 1d via
`materialise-bars` (already in place from P2).

**Falsification**.
The memo will be flagged as **non-informative** if any of:
1. The 1m and 1d returns have the **same** sign of autocorrelation
   (the horizon-dependence is the main expected result).
2. The Lo-MacKinlay VR test fails to reject the random-walk
   hypothesis at all horizons (no time-series predictability of any
   kind — the brief would have nothing to leverage).
3. The Hurst exponent is 0.5 ± 0.05 at every horizon (the scaling
   story is missing).

**Prediction**.
A first-pass analysis on the 1m bars (no frozen-minute exclusion
yet) will report, on the BTCUSDT 5y sample:
* 1m return autocorrelation ≈ -0.05 to -0.10 (mean-reverting on
  the smallest scale, a known microstructure effect).
* 1d return autocorrelation ≈ +0.05 to +0.10 (mild trend on the
  largest scale).
* 1m absolute-return autocorrelation ≈ +0.20 to +0.30 (volatility
  clustering at every horizon, as expected from GARCH effects).
* Lo-MacKinlay VR(2) for 1m returns < 1 (mean-reverting at short
  lags).
* Hurst exponent on 1m returns: 0.45-0.49 (anti-persistent at the
  smallest scale).
