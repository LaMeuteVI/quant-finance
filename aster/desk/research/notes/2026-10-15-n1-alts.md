# Study Note — N1 panel alts (TA descriptive), 2026-10-15

## Verdict
**Complete, no candidate.** 0 retained cells across 640 evaluated cells (5 assets × 128 cells, of which 480 are primary) under the preregistered protocol (HAC Newey-West standard errors, 5 000 circular block bootstraps, dual BH-FDR asset/panel, inverse dominance check, and three strict placebos: shift, timing, and horizon).

## Method
* **Scope**: Level N1 descriptive formalised technical analysis on five secondary panel assets (`BNBUSDT`, `XRPUSDT`, `SOLUSDT`, `LINKUSDT`, `DOGEUSDT`).
* **Source**: Local R2 Binance spot 1-minute klines aggregated to UTC daily bars via the canonical close-labelled primitive (`[t-1d, t)`), sealed at the exclusive cutoff `2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`.
* **Grid**: 32 signals × 4 horizons (1, 3, 7, 14 days) = 128 cells per asset (96 primary, 32 placebo-level Fibonacci controls).
* **Inference**: HAC t-test on the event-conditional grid score with Newey-West bandwidth `max(h, 1)`, combined with a joint circular moving-block bootstrap (5 000 draws, block `max(5, h)`).

## Panel findings
Even though several primary cells (e.g. moving average crossovers and Fibonacci retracements on `SOLUSDT`) achieved nominal asset-level BH q < 0.05 and bootstrap significance, **all of them failed the strict placebo controls** (specifically the horizon and timing placebos, whose p-values were significant in the same direction, showing that the apparent edge is driven by general serial dependence and macro trend persistence rather than the specific TA event).
