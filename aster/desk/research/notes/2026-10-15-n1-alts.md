## Limits
* Descriptive only. No cost model, no execution, no capacity, no holdout.
* No hypothesis registered; no backtest run.
* Signals use overlapping forward windows. HAC and the joint block bootstrap
  quantify sampling uncertainty; they do not create causal identification.

  correction is applied to the full declared asset/panel families.
