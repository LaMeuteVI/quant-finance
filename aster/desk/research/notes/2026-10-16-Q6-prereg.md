# Q6 — BTC/ETH intraday flow (N5 on tick-derived bars) (preregistration)

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Item**: Q6 (research/QUEUE.md, Block 1).
**Skills**: `tsay-analysis-financial-time-series` ch03,
`cfa-quantitative-methods` ch09, `lopez-de-prado-afml` ch17-18.
**Family**: `descriptive-flow-n5` (new family; descriptive memo, not
a hypothesis, does not count toward the DSR denominator).

**Statement**.
On BTCUSDT aggTrades (Q4 import: 107M rows, 2022-01 → 2024-06), the
aggressive taker flow (signed by `is_buyer_maker`):
* has a 1-bar signed-flow autocorrelation in the range [0.05, 0.20]
  (positive, persistence of flow),
* the **next-bar return conditional on extreme flow** (top 5%
  signed flow) is positive for buy flow and negative for sell flow
  (continuation, not absorption) at the 5m/15m/1h horizons, with
  an effect size of 0.1% to 0.5% per extreme bar,
* the **Kyle λ** estimate on 1h bars is positive and well-defined
  (price impact per unit of net flow), at the order of 1e-5 to
  1e-3 USD/USDT per BTC,
* the **Amihud** illiquidity ratio is between 1e-7 and 1e-5 on
  the 1h bars, in the typical crypto range,
* the **Roll** spread estimator is positive on 1h bars (Roll's
  measure is well-defined when serial covariance of returns is
  negative, which Q5 already observed).

**Universe / data**.
BTCUSDT aggTrades (Q4 import, 4 partitions, 107M rows, pre-cutoff).
The volume-bar construction (`build_volume_bars`) gives bars of
roughly equal volume, which is the right input for Kyle λ and VPIN
(calendar bars confound flow with volatility).

**Falsification**.
The memo will be flagged as **non-informative** if any of:
1. The 1-bar signed-flow autocorr is in [-0.05, 0.05] (no flow
   persistence, no exploitable signal).
2. The next-bar return conditional on extreme flow has the same
   sign on both sides (no information, no edge).
3. Kyle λ is negative (price decreases with buy flow — the data
   would be inconsistent or the volume bars misaligned).

**Prediction** (BTCUSDT 1h volume bars, 2022-2024):
* signed-flow lag-1 autocorr ≈ +0.10
* next-bar return after extreme buy flow (top 5%): +0.10% to +0.30%
* next-bar return after extreme sell flow (top 5%): -0.10% to -0.30%
* Kyle λ(1h) ≈ 1e-5 to 1e-3 USD/USDT per BTC
* Amihud(1h) ≈ 1e-7 to 1e-5
* Roll(1h) > 0
