# Q9 — N2 per-alt intraday descriptive grid

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Queue item**: Q9 — N2 per alt (BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, XMR; then AAVE, FET, NEAR, SUI in panel).
**Scope**: descriptive only; no ledger hypothesis and no backtest.
**Cutoff**: use only observations strictly before 2024-07-01T00:00:00Z.

## Question

Does the Q5 N2 descriptive pattern generalise across the alt universe and
multiple bar horizons: one-bar return dependence, absolute-return volatility
clustering, Lo-MacKinlay variance ratios, and Hurst persistence?

## Pre-registered grid

- Assets: BNBUSDT, XRPUSDT, SOLUSDT, LINKUSDT, DOGEUSDT, XMRUSDT,
  AAVEUSDT, FETUSDT, NEARUSDT, SUIUSDT.
- Timeframes: 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1d.
- Statistics: return autocorrelation at lags 1, 2, 3, 5, 10; absolute-return
  autocorrelation at the same lags; variance ratio q=2 and q=4; rescaled-range
  Hurst exponent.
- Data: local R2 spot kline store only; frozen minutes are retained and their
  limitation is reported. No post-cutoff data, holdout path, or new download.
- Multiple testing: this is a descriptive map, not a strategy claim; no cell
  is promoted by p-value. The full cell count and a sign summary are reported.
- Placebo/robustness: q=4 is the longer-horizon check against q=2; synthetic
  unit tests cover white noise, positive and negative serial dependence, and
  Hurst direction.

## Falsification

Reject the generalisation if the alt panel does not reproduce either (a)
negative one-bar return dependence at most timeframes or (b) positive absolute-
return dependence at most timeframes. A mixed result is retained as a regime /
asset map, not converted into an alpha claim.

## Economic gate

No cell becomes a ledger hypothesis. Any future candidate must separately
show an effect in bps, an economic mechanism, conservative round-trip costs,
capacity, and a preregistered `desk backtest` family. The exploration cutoff
and sealed-holdout boundary remain unchanged.
