# Q10 — N3 per-alt volatility preregistration

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Queue item**: Q10 — N3 per alt (GARCH/EGARCH/GJR, inverse leverage effect, HAR-RV, Diebold–Mariano vs naïve, compression → expansion).
**Scope**: descriptive pseudo-out-of-sample forecast comparison; no trading strategy.
**Cutoff**: strictly `< 2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout read.

## Fixed grid and data

- Symbols: BNBUSDT, XRPUSDT, SOLUSDT, LINKUSDT, DOGEUSDT, XMRUSDT,
  AAVEUSDT, FETUSDT, NEARUSDT, SUIUSDT.
- Input: local R2 spot 1m klines, active consecutive minutes only for RV/BV;
  frozen minutes are excluded from realised-volatility returns.
- Target: next valid daily log realised variance.
- Valid day gate: ≥720 active minutes and ≥1000 consecutive active pairs.
- Forecast origins: expanding recursive history, training through 2022-12-31,
  evaluation 2023-01-01 through 2024-06-30.
- Fixed models: 30-day mean realised-volatility naive, recursive HAR-RV
  (lag 1, 5-day mean, 22-day mean), Gaussian GARCH(1,1) on daily close
  returns. GARCH parameters are estimated once on the fixed pre-2023 training
  sample and then filtered recursively through the evaluation window; this is
  a fixed-parameter pseudo-OOS forecast and avoids re-estimating hundreds of
  overlapping daily fits. No parameter tuning after seeing results.

**Pre-result implementation amendment (2026-10-16):** the initial recursive-
re-estimation implementation exceeded the local compute budget without
producing an artefact. Before any successful data run, the GARCH estimator was
changed to the fixed-training-parameter formulation above. The target, cutoff,
models, loss functions, comparison family and falsification criteria are
unchanged; this amendment is recorded rather than silently changing the
experiment.
- Comparisons: HAR vs naive, GARCH vs naive, HAR vs GARCH; squared and
  absolute log-RV loss; DM-HLN with HAC variance and BH-FDR over the fixed
  10-symbol × 3-comparison × 2-loss family.

## Predictions and falsification

1. HAR should beat the naive forecast in squared log-RV loss for at least 7/10
   assets, with the sign stable in 2023 and 2024.
2. GARCH should not dominate HAR on the majority of assets; a failure to beat
   naive is not alpha.
3. The inverse-leverage diagnostic is descriptive: estimate whether positive
   daily returns are associated with higher next-day realised variance than
   negative returns of comparable magnitude. No sign will be tuned afterward.
4. Compression → expansion is reported only as a conditional volatility map;
   it is not promoted without a separate return P&L, cost and capacity test.

Whole-class rejection: if HAR is not better than naive in at least half the
assets under BH q<0.05 and sign-stability, the Q10 forecast prior is not
supported. A forecast win is never a strategy candidate.
