## Statement

F3 v1.1 (`vol_target_trend_ensemble`, vol-scaled trend ensemble, 5 majors)
keeps a positive net edge when each perp leg is charged the USDⓈ-M taker
fee (5 bps/side) instead of the spot fee (10 bps/side) it was wrongly
charged in C2.

## Mechanism

The book is perp-only. A perp round trip on BTC at 100k costs
2x5 + spread + impact = 12.95 bps, not 2x10 + spread + impact = 22.95 bps.
With turnover 9.0x/yr that is roughly 10 bps x 9 = 0.9 %/yr, consistent
with the 1.5-2 %/yr of phantom drag C2 carried. Removing it should lift
the net Sharpe by roughly 0.05-0.10 and reduce `cost_pnl_ann` from
4.22 % toward ~2.5-3 %.

## Falsification

F3 v1.1 is falsified and closed for good — no further cost or parameter
re-read of this configuration — if it fails **any** gate in
`config/desk.yaml` (`research/notes/2026-10-20-C2b-prereg.md` § gates).
DSR in particular: with n_trials_family = 17 the haircut grows, and the
dev threshold is `dsr_min: 0.95`.
