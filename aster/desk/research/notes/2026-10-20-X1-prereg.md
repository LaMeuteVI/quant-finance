## 1. Literature verification (required by the QUEUE item before preregistering)

The QUEUE item names BIS "Crypto carry" (Schmeling, Schrimpf, Todorov,
2023) as a lead to verify. **Verification result: the lead does not
support this hypothesis.** Verified from the primary PDF
(`bis.org/publ/work1087.pdf`, BIS Working Paper No. 1087, Monetary and
Economic Department, April 2023, revised October 2025):

| Claim checked | Verified finding |
|---|---|
| Instrument | **"funding rate": 0 hits. "perpetual": 0 hits.** The paper studies **dated-futures basis** on BTC/ETH, not perpetuals and not the funding mechanism X1 trades. |
| "Cross-sectional" | Means **across exchanges** (carry summary stats by venue: Binance/OKEx/FTX/Huobi/BitMEX/Deribit/CME), *not* long-low-funding / short-high-funding across coins. X1's construction is absent from the paper. |
| Transaction costs | **2 mentions, both reporting Sharpe "before transaction costs".** No bps, no maker/taker, no bid-ask anywhere in the paper. Gross returns only. |
| Sample | Daily, **March 2019 – January 2022** (CME sub-sample Aug 2020 – Jan 2022). BTC + ETH only. |
| Claimed magnitude | Carry "can become very large (up to 60% p.a.)" and varies strongly over time — a **level** statement, not a rankable spread. |

**Consequence for the desk.** The literature lead is recorded as
**not applicable**: wrong instrument, wrong construction, gross returns,
and a sample that ends before the perp era this strategy depends on. X1
is therefore preregistered on its own mechanism, with the literature
recorded as an unverified-and-now-rejected lead. Nothing in X1's
expected value may cite WP 1087.

## 2. Statement

Cross-sectionally rank the USDⓈ-M perpetuals by trailing funding, long
the lowest-funding third and short the highest-funding third,
dollar-neutral and beta-neutral to BTC, weekly rebalance with a
no-trade band. The edge, if any, is the **funding spread** between the
two terciles, net of C1b taker costs.

## 3. Mechanism

Crowded leveraged longs pay funding and sit one forced-deleveraging
step from liquidation; the short leg of a crowded name collects that
funding while its long counterparty unwinds. If funding is persistent
over 7–30 days (a preregistered claim of this desk, to be re-verified
by this trial), ranking on trailing funding and holding both tails
captures the spread without taking directional risk.

## 4. Falsification — declared before the run

X1 is **falsified** if any of the following holds on the dev sample
(cutoff 2024-07-01). Gates are `config/desk.yaml` **only**:
`min_obs` 500, `sharpe_ann_min` 0.8, `max_drawdown_floor` -0.35,
`dsr_min` 0.95, `stress_sharpe_ann_min` 0.3.

In addition, X1 is falsified by the desk's own cost bar, declared
here before the run:

> **Zero-net bar.** If the amortised round-trip cost of holding the
> prescribed book exceeds its gross funding spread at the prescribed
> rebalance horizon, the strategy has no economic mechanism regardless
> of what its return series does. Such a strategy is rejected without
> running it, because a positive backtest would then be an artefact of
> the cost model rather than of the market.

This is the same logic as the Q8 carry bar: a null threshold declared in
advance, not a statistic discovered afterwards.

## 5. Declared configuration (fixed before the run, no tuning after)

- **Universe**: *all symbols with perp funding history*, as the QUEUE
  item prescribes. Measured pre-cutoff coverage: **12** symbols —
  AAVE, BNB, BTC, DOGE, ETH, FET, LINK, NEAR, SOL, SUI, XMR, XRP —
  each with ≥ 424 daily fixings before 2024-07-01. A symbol enters the
  cross-section only when it has ≥ 365 fixings, so the panel is
  stable; FET and SUI drop out early on that rule, which is recorded
  as a **constituent effect**, not a selection.
- **Survivorship**: stated explicitly as the QUEUE item requires. XMR
  is kept for its whole listed window (continuous funding 2020-02-01 →
  2024-06-30, no gap). No symbol is dropped for having been delisted;
  the panel is *as-listed*, which is a **survivorship bias in favour of
  the strategy** and is disclosed as such.
- **Signal**: trailing mean of daily funding fixings over **30 days**
  (`min_periods=15`), computed causally from fixings up to and
  including bar *t*.
- **Ranking**: cross-sectional percentile rank each day; long the
  lowest tercile (`pct <= 1/3`), short the highest (`pct >= 2/3`).
  A day is tradable only if at least one name falls in each tercile.
- **Sizing**: dollar-neutral (sum of long notional = sum of short
  notional = AUM/2 each side), equal weight within a tercile, beta to
  BTC neutralised by scaling the short side.
- **Rebalance**: **weekly**, with a no-trade band on tercile
  membership (a name is only traded when it crosses into or out of a
  tercile, not on rank jitter inside one).
- **Cost model**: C1b grid, **taker, no maker rebate**, perp legs only
  at 5 bps/side fee. Maker execution is out of scope until a fill
  model exists.
- **AUM**: 250 000 USD.

## 6. Numeric prediction (registered before the run)

Measured descriptively on the pre-cutoff panel *before* preregistration
(this is a property of the signal, not of the strategy P&L):

- gross cross-sectional funding spread (long-low minus short-high),
  tercile method, 30-day trailing mean: **2.11 bps/fixing**
  (median 1.57, p90 4.22), positive on **100 %** of 1 623 tradable
  days, ≈ **23 %/yr** if held constant.
- mean C1b perp round-trip cost across the 12 symbols at 250k:
  **42.9 bps** (BTC 17.5, ETH 22.2, BNB 38.7, SOL 49.7, XRP 50.0,
  DOGE 63.6; thin alts 29.9–59.7).
- average legs in the book: **7.11**.

**Predicted outcome**: amortised cost at the prescribed weekly horizon
is `7.11 × 42.9 / 2 / 21 fixings` ≈ **7.27 bps/fixing**, which
**exceeds** the 2.11 bps/fixing gross spread by a factor of ~3.4.
Predicted net: **strongly negative**, ≈ **−56 %/yr**.

**This preregistration therefore expects X1 as prescribed to fail.**
The prediction is recorded in advance so the result cannot be
reinterpreted afterwards. X1 is run anyway, because a negative trial
closes the family at n=1 with evidence rather than with an argument.

## 7. Universe-subset note (post-hoc, explicitly NOT gated)

If the prescribed 12-symbol universe fails, restricted universes will be
**reported but not gated**, because selecting the universe after seeing
which one clears the cost bar is exactly the selection this desk's
pre-registration protocol exists to prevent. Measured for the record
(pre-cutoff, same method):

| Universe | Gross spread | Mean RT | Break-even hold | Net at weekly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all 12 (prescribed) | 2.11 bps/fixing | 42.9 bps | 72.3 d | **−56.5 %/yr** |
| 5 majors (BNB BTC ETH SOL XRP) | 2.88 bps/fixing | 35.6 bps | 18.6 d | +3.7 %/yr |
| 3 majors (BTC ETH BNB) | 1.87 bps/fixing | 26.1 bps | 20.9 d | +0.1 %/yr |

The lesson to carry forward, if X1 is rejected: **the cross-sectional
funding spread is real but the impact cost of thin alt perps (42–64 bps
round trip) exceeds it at any tradable horizon.** Restricting to majors
is a *new hypothesis*, not a re-read of X1, and would need its own
preregistration and its own n=1 family.
