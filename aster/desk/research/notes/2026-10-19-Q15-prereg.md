## Methodology (pre-registered)

### Definition of the signal

A **3-bar fair value gap down** (FVG down) on bar `t` requires:
- `low[t] > high[t-2]` (gap between `t-2` and `t`)
- **Entry at bar t+1 close** (`fvg_down_entry_h1`)
- Forward return `fwd_k = close[t+1+k] / close[t+1] - 1` for k ∈ {1, 2, 5, 10} bars.

Equivalently in price gap detection: an N-bar **gap down** of size ≥ X is defined as `close[t] < low[t-N] * (1 - X)` where X ∈ {0.0, 0.001, 0.003}. We use `gap_size = X = 0.001` (10 bps minimum gap) as the primary spec; placebos on `X ∈ {0.0, 0.003, 0.01}`.

### Universe

Spot klines from `data/ohlcv/binance/{BTC-USDT, ETH-USDT, BNB-USDT, SOL-USDT, XRP-USDT}/{1d, 4h, 1h}.parquet`. Pre-cutoff 2024-07-01. **No new download** — green per D5.

For each (symbol, timeframe), count the number of valid FVG-down events after warmup of N bars (= max(50, 2 × 4 × 252)).

### Hypotheses

- **H1 (single-cell retention):** the ETH `fvg_down_entry_h1` survives BH-FDR on the **panel** (q < 0.05 on ~60 cells), not just on daily ETH. The original daily cell was q=0.026 on 112 cells; on a fresh split it should either retain or vanish cleanly.
- **H2 (mechanism coherence):** the **direction** of fwd_1 is **sign-consistent** across ≥ 4/5 symbols at the daily timeframe (positive = continuation up after gap-down noise, negative = reversal down). Mechanism-ambiguous elsewhere.
- **H3 (size dependence):** larger gaps (X = 0.003) produce **stronger** fwd_1 than smaller gaps (X = 0.001) at the daily timeframe, monotone: a placebo for noise-floor artefacts.

### Verdict rule

- **RETAIN** if H1 ∧ H2 ∧ H3.
- **NEUTRAL** if exactly 1/3.
- **FALSIFIÉ** if all 3 fail.

A **FALSIFIÉ** verdict closes the ETH FVG cell for good and frees up the open question. A **RETAIN** verdict does not auto-promote to F-hypothesis because the BACKLOG noted the mechanism is ambiguous and ETH-only — promotion to F would require a separate ledger hypothesis registration with explicit carry bar (≥ 30 bps/yr net of base costs per DECISIONS D5).

### Statistical correction

- Benjamini-Hochberg FDR @ q = 0.05 within each `(timeframe, horizon)` family of 5 symbols.
- Bootstrap 95 % CI on the panel mean using 1000 circular moving-block bootstraps with block size = max(20, sqrt(n)).
- Placebos are mandatory: gap-size placebo (`X = 0.0` = no gap required, `X = 0.003`, `X = 0.01`) and direction placebo (`fvg_up` instead of `fvg_down`).
