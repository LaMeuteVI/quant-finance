# F2 preregistration — Funding and premium as a crowding signal (D5, ORANGE auto-approve)

**Written before any backtest.**

## 1. Why F2 exists

F1 and F1v2 (carry-based, H-20261016-002 and H-20261018-003) were
**both falsified on the dev sample**. The carry is real (+1.234 bps /
fixing mean, +6.1% received /year on the BTC 4h backtest) but it is
too small to cover the spot leg's variance + rebalance cost on a
delta-neutral book.

F2 takes a different angle. The perp-vs-spot **premium** is a
direct proxy for crowded leveraged positioning:

- A persistently positive premium (perp > spot) means leveraged
  longs are paying up for the synthetic exposure.
- A premium spike coincides with retail and over-leveraged
  positions entering the long side. When the spike mean-reverts
  the longs are usually right (carry has not changed), but when
  the regime flips (e.g. a 24 h liquidation cascade) the long
  squeeze can be sharp and predictable a few bars in advance.
- Open-interest changes **condition** the signal: an extreme
  premium **with** rising OI = positioning still being added = a
  stronger reversion signal. An extreme premium **without**
  rising OI = positioning already cleaning up = weaker.

The mechanism is in the brief and in the Q8 (premium persistence)
and Q21 (microstructure) families. The hypothesis is that a
**short-perp / long-spot** book, entered when the premium z-score
crosses a high threshold (perp rich), conditional on OI rising,
earns a positive basis P&L net of costs.

F2 is a **directional** strategy (not delta-neutral). The risk
profile is fundamentally different from F1: the beta to BTC is
non-zero and the strategy earns on the basis reversion, not on
funding.

## 2. Methodology

### 2.1 Signal

For each symbol s at bar t:

```
premium[t] = (perp_close[t] - spot_close[t]) / spot_close[t]
premium_z[t] = (premium[t] - rolling_mean(premium, 30d)) /
                rolling_std(premium, 30d)
oi_change_z[t] = (oi[t] - oi[t - 1d]) / rolling_std(oi.diff(1d), 30d)
signal_short_perp[t] = +1   if  premium_z[t] > +entry_z
                              and oi_change_z[t] > +oi_z
                         = -1   if  premium_z[t] < -entry_z
                              and oi_change_z[t] < -oi_z
                         =  0   otherwise
```

Default params: `entry_z = 2.0`, `oi_z = 0.5`, lookback 30 days.

### 2.2 Position

The strategy takes a **perp-only** directional position with
`max_gross = 1.0` (no spot leg). The position is closed when
`premium_z[t]` reverts inside `[-exit_z, +exit_z]` with
`exit_z = 0.5`. The position sizing is `signal * max_gross`.

The strategy emits a single weight column per symbol using the
perp leg convention (`BTC-USDT:USDT`).

### 2.3 Family

`crowding-premium`. Separate from `funding-carry-dn` (F1 family)
because the signal is on the **basis** (premium), not on the
**funding** rate, and the position is **directional** (long
vol-adj basis reversion), not delta-neutral carry. Deflated
Sharpe will use the new family count starting at 1.

### 2.4 Universe

BTC-USDT only (same scope as F1v2 dev). ETH-USDT, SOL-USDT will
be tested in V2 once the BTC leg clears the gates. The Q3b
premium + metrics stores carry all 5 panel symbols but F2 starts
narrow (the prior says "extreme premium" is rare; dilution will
hide it).

### 2.5 Sample and data

Dev sample: pre-cutoff, BTC-USDT 4h bars, 2017-08-17 to
2024-06-30. Inputs: spot close (R2 store), perp close
(`data/r2/market/futures/um/klines_1h/symbol=BTCUSDT/...`),
premium (`data/r2/market/futures/um/premium_index_klines_1h/...`),
metrics (`data/r2/market/futures/um/metrics/...`).

### 2.6 Costs

Binance perp taker 5 bps/side (VIP 0), grid impact on top. Spot
leg is not held so no spot cost (unless ETH V2 ships with a
short-spot leg, then 10 bps/side). The base round-trip is
**10 bps**, stress ×2 = 20 bps, ×3 = 30 bps.

### 2.7 Statistics

Base + stress (×2 costs, ×3 costs, lag=2) + walk-forward
(252/63/5, expanding) + PBO across the (entry_z, oi_z) grid.

## 4. Falsification

F2 is **falsified** if the dev backtest on BTC-USDT 4h
pre-cutoff returns:

- `sharpe_ann < 0.8` (the desk gate), **or**
- `max_drawdown < -0.35` (the desk gate), **or**
- `dsr < 0.95` against the new `crowding-premium` family
  count (starting at 1), **or**
- the **basis P&L is not the dominant contribution** to the
  gross P&L (i.e. the strategy is mostly a directional bet on
  BTC, not a basis reversion trade — falsification of the
  mechanism, separate from the Sharpe gate).

A FALSIFIED F2 closes the line of inquiry: the desk's
"crowding via premium" prior is not strong enough to ship.

## 6. Open questions / risks

- The premium panel is hourly (Q3b klines_1h). The bar bridge
  will resample to 4h. This is a **loss of information**:
  premium spikes happen between the 4h boundaries. F2 v2 may
  move to 1h to recover it.
- The metrics panel is **daily**. The 4h OI z-score will use
  the daily OI as a "step" (forward-fill). The lag-1 OI diff
  in bars will span 6 4h-bars per day. The OI z-score
  conditioning may be weak at 4h. F2 v2 may drop the OI
  conditioning.
- The strategy is directional. The dev Sharpe must beat a
  long-only BTC buy-and-hold Sharpe after costs; otherwise
  the basis P&L is just noise on top of a beta position.
  The memo will report both.
- The F1 family (`funding-carry-dn`) is **not** in F2's
  family count: the two strategies have different signals
  and different P&L sources. DSR is computed per-family.
