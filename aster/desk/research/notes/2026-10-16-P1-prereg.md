## Motivation (verbatim from the audit, line 12)

> Le contrat de stratégie ne reçoit que les prix de clôture. Impossible de backtester le flux taker, le volume, le funding, le premium, l'OI, donc tout le N5 et la section 6 du brief.

The brief section 6 (inter-asset) and section 7 (carries / crowding / flow / seasonality / lead-lag) all require features that are not in the close price alone. Until P1 lands, every `desk backtest` call effectively tests a close-only strategy even if a strategy author wanted to consume flow.

## Predictions (testable; falsifiable)

- **F1 (numeric, structural)**. A new dataclass `MarketData` exists at `src/aster/strategies/market_data.py` with at least 11 fields: `close`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `volume`, `quote_volume`, `taker_buy_base`, `taker_buy_quote`, `trades`, `funding`, `open_interest`. Premium may be included or noted as future work (no public-archive source pre-cutoff, see Q3). Each field is a `pd.DataFrame` with a tz-aware UTC `DatetimeIndex` (the bar close), columns are symbols, and the alignment is consistent across fields (same index modulo the optional fields that may be missing).
- **F2 (numeric, structural)**. The `Strategy` abstract class keeps `target_weights(prices: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame` working for every existing strategy (5 strategies total in `src/aster/strategies/`, exercised in the regression test suite). The new signature `target_weights(market_data: MarketData) -> pd.DataFrame` is also supported via a `MarketDataStrategy` mixin that the existing strategies do not need to inherit from.
- **F3 (numeric, test)**. A `check_market_data_lookahead` test runs the truncation invariance test on every present field of `MarketData` and returns a per-field pass/fail dict. The existing `check_truncation_invariance` is kept untouched.
- **F4 (numeric, count)**. Total pytest count grows by ≥ 8 (1 dataclass, 1 dual-sig adapter, 1 alignment invariant, 5 per-field look-ahead = 8). The 375 baseline must stay green.

No edge claim, no Sharpe claim, no PnL claim. This is a pipeline refactor, not a hypothesis.

## Falsification

- Any existing strategy's regression test fails after the migration → revert and re-design.
- `MarketData` accidentally allows a future observation through a non-causal field → the truncation test must catch it.
- `target_weights(market_data)` raises on a non-trivial `MarketData` populated from the R2 store → fail.

## Numbers / bounds (record-only)

- A bar at time `t` carries information from `(t - period, t]`, where `period` is the timeframe. The truncation test truncates the input at `t` and asserts the strategy's output at `t` does not change.
- `MarketData` constructor does no I/O. Construction is pure assembly. Loading from the R2 / funding / aggTrades stores is the responsibility of `backtest/pipeline.py` (next iteration: P2).
