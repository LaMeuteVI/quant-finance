# Q7 — Intraday lead-lag grid (preregistration)

**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Item**: Q7 — Intraday lead-lag grid (section 6.2).
**Scope**: descriptive exploration only; no ledger hypothesis, no backtest, no holdout.
**Cutoff**: strictly `< 2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`.

## Question
Does BTC/ETH information lead altcoin returns at intraday horizons, after removing the contemporaneous market factor, or are apparent lead-lags common-flow artefacts?

## Universe and horizons
Use the already materialised pre-cutoff R2 bars for BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, BNBUSDT and XRPUSDT at 5m, 15m, 1h and 4h. The 1m grid is not materialised by the current bridge and will not be fabricated. Test directed pairs BTC→alt, ETH→alt and alt→alt where data exists; lags are 1, 2, 5, 15 bars, corresponding to the available timeframe rather than mixing clock units.

## Pre-specified statistics
For each directed pair and lag:
1. Pearson correlation of leader return at t−lag with follower return at t.
2. OLS coefficient of follower return on lagged leader return and contemporaneous BTC/ETH market control where applicable.
3. Block-bootstrap 95% CI using non-overlapping blocks of 24 bars.
4. BH-FDR across the complete directed-pair × lag × timeframe grid.
5. Stability sign by calendar half-year and economic translation in basis points per follower trade.

The primary grid is on raw returns. A residual grid de-means each alt return against BTC and ETH contemporaneous returns; it is a robustness check, not a new family.

## Predictions
- BTC/ETH lead-lag correlations are positive but small, concentrated at lag 1–2 bars.
- After BTC/ETH residualisation, fewer than 5% of cells survive BH-FDR at q=0.05.
- Surviving gross effects are below the conservative round-trip cost in `research/atlas/costs.md` for the relevant asset and horizon.

## Falsification / decision rule
The descriptive claim is rejected if the raw grid has no positive stable lead-lag, or if the residual grid has a broad (>5%) stable significant effect. Any nominal survivor is not a strategy candidate unless its bps effect exceeds costs, survives the half-year sign check, and receives a separate ledger preregistration before any backtest.
