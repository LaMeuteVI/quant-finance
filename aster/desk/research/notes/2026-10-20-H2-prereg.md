## Pre-registration
Before running the audit, test the exact H1 strategy logic on the pre-cutoff dev
panel only. The audit has three fixed components:

1. truncation invariance on **at least 300** deterministic cuts, including the
   rebalance-day and pre-rebalance-day schedule; pass requires zero violations;
2. weekday diagnostic with the same signal and parameters, with rebalance days
   restricted to Monday, Wednesday, and Friday in turn;
3. lag-2 diagnostic using the existing engine stress definition; the annualised
   Sharpe must remain within ±0.20 of the H1 base Sharpe.

No tuning, no holdout data, and no new universe. Weekday and lag diagnostics are
reported as robustness checks, not as new alpha candidates. The result is a
failure of the H2 audit if the truncation check has any violation or if either
weekday/lag check moves Sharpe outside the preregistered ±0.20 band.
