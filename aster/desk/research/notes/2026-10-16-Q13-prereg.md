# Q13 preregistration — Dependence structure (former 6.1)

**Written before the Q13 data run.**

- Cutoff: strict exclusive `2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`; no holdout.
- Universe: the approved 8-asset alt panel: BNB, XRP, SOL, LINK, DOGE, AAVE, SUI, NEAR (no BTC/ETH to keep this a pure cross-section). The panel is fixed; no post-result additions.
- Frequency: daily log-returns on the intersection window 2021-01-01 → 2024-06-30, all inputs strictly pre-cutoff.
- Three sub-blocks, pre-declared:
  1. **Raw correlation panel + Marčenko–Pastur denoising** (`mlam` ch02): eigenvalues vs. the MP upper bound; keep the 2 largest structural components; report the raw vs. denoised correlation comparison.
  2. **Detoning** (`mlam` ch02): remove the dominant market mode; report residualised correlations and the largest off-diagonal residual.
  3. **Clustering** (`mlam` ch03–04): angular distance, hierarchical clustering with average linkage, optimal number of clusters via silhouette and Gap statistic; report the cluster allocation table.
- Fixed sub-block 4 (corollary): pairwise lower-tail dependence via empirical lower-quadrant dependence ratio on the bottom decile of joint returns, by regime (above/below median realised volatility) and unconditionally. No tail copula fitting; this is a robust diagnostic.
- Falsification: any denoised/detoned correlation above 0.50 in absolute value, or any pair whose cluster allocation is stable across 80% of the bootstrap replicates, is reported as a candidate. A candidate still requires a separate preregistration, a `desk backtest` and cost/capacity evidence before it can become a ledger hypothesis.
- BH-FDR is applied across the declared pairwise grid where applicable. Bootstrap is circular moving-block with block=5 and 1,000 replications. Random seed: `0xDADA1331`.
- Artefacts: `research/atlas/q13_dependence.json`, `research/memos/2026-10-16-Q13-N6.md`; targeted tests under `tests/test_q13_dependence.py`. No holdout, paper, live, ledger hypothesis or download is permitted for this descriptive item.
