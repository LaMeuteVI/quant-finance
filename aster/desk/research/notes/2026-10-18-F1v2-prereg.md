# F1v2 preregistration — Funding carry, delta-neutral, properly tested (D5, ORANGE auto-approve)

**Written before any backtest.**

## 1. Why F1v2 exists

F1 v1 (H-20261016-002) was rejected on **infrastructure**, not on
the signal:

> the pipeline.run path does not inject the funding panel into the
> strategy. A close-only Strategy has no funding channel;
> MarketDataStrategy.target_weights_market_data consumes
> `md.funding` but MarketData aligns funding columns to close columns
> (P1 invariant) so the perp-suffixed funding columns are dropped.

P6 (commit `54f979c`) wired the funding panel from
`data/r2/market/futures/um/funding_rate/` into
`pipeline.run → run_backtest → engine`, applied at the 00/08/16 UTC
fixings only. Q3b (commit `e375879`) wired the perp close panel from
`data/r2/market/futures/um/klines_1h/` and forwarded it to the engine
(`perp_close`) and the strategy (`perp_close` kwarg). So the
infrastructure that blocked F1 v1 now exists. **F1v2 re-runs the
signal with the wiring fixed**.

## 2. Mechanism (unchanged from F1 v1, recap)

For each symbol the book is delta-neutral: long spot
`BTCUSDT` and short perp `BTCUSDT:USDT` (and the reverse when the
predicted funding is negative). The signal is the sign of the
lookback-mean of the funding rate, net of an amortised cost cushion.
Position size scales with the magnitude of the predicted funding
relative to the cushion.

The hypothesis the desk is testing is whether **funding rate is
persistent on horizons of hours to days (term-structure carry)**.
Q8 measured the prior at +1.234 bps mean per 8h fixing (≈ 13 %/yr
gross), 84 % positive fixings, lag-1 autocorrelation 0.74. That is
the strongest prior on the desk.

The new piece in v2 vs v1 is that the **basis guard** now uses the
actual perp close (from Q3b), not the spot close as a proxy. The
basis = (perp_close - spot_close) / spot_close. The z-score of basis
over 30 bars is a better crash-warning than the spot-return z-score
F1 v1 used.

## 3. DoD — quantitative gate

The carry bar comes from the desk gates in
`config/desk.yaml`, **not** from a bps-per-year threshold:

```yaml
gates:
  min_obs: 500
  sharpe_ann_min: 0.8
  max_drawdown_floor: -0.35
  dsr_min: 0.95
  stress_sharpe_ann_min: 0.3
holdout_gates:
  sharpe_fraction_of_prediction: 0.5
  psr_min: 0.80
```

A dev backtest on BTCUSDT (the only symbol whose perp data Q3b
imported) passes only if:

* `sharpe_ann_net` ≥ 0.8 (cost-grossed),
* `max_drawdown` ≥ −0.35,
* `dsr` ≥ 0.95 with the true family count
  (F1 v1 + F1v2 + any F1v2 variants after this one),
* `stress_sharpe_ann` (×2 costs) ≥ 0.3,
* `min_obs` ≥ 500,
* `lookahead_clean` = True.

`pipeline.run` computes all of these; the only piece that belongs
to this prereg is **the new strategy version** (`funding_carry_dn
v1.1.0`).

## 4. Hypothesis (single, preregistered)

**F1v2**: The funding carry trade (long spot, short perp when
expected funding > 2 bps/bar; flat otherwise) earns a net
sharpe_ann ≥ 0.8 on the BTCUSDT pre-cutoff sample, after Binance
spot taker 10 bps and USDⓈ-M taker 5 bps per side (VIP 0 base),
plus the per-AUM grid impact, with a drawdown ≥ −0.35 and a DSR ≥
0.95 against the F1 family's true trial count.

## 5. Falsification

If the dev backtest returns a `sharpe_ann_net < 0.8`, or
`max_drawdown < −0.35`, or `dsr < 0.95`, F1v2 is rejected without
tuning, the holdout is not read, the hypothesis moves to
`rejected`, and the lesson is recorded on H-20261018-001.
