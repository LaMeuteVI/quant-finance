## Why Q19 exists

After F1v2 (REJECTED), F2 (REJECTED), and F3 (this run), the desk
needs a final synthesis of which signals survived and how to allocate
the cross-strategy capital. Q19 is the N7 step in the research ladder:
synthesise, allocate, decide.

Per `research/QUEUE.md` Q19: "Per asset: signal map (horizon, IC with
CI, corrected t, IC decay, bps vs cost, regime dependence, capacity,
correlation to other signals). Register the 1–3 most mature hypotheses
(ORANGE), dev backtests (ORANGE), Skeptic and Breaker, capacity at
100k/1M/10M/50M. **Holdout read and paper are RED.**"

## Mechanism

Capital allocation across surviving F-strategies using:
1. **Risk-parity** as the base case: weight_i ∝ (Sharpe_i / σ_i).
2. **Min-variance** as the comparison: weight_i ∝ 1/σ_i.
3. **Equal-weight** as the fallback.

The allocator is a **post-dev** synthesis. It does not generate new
signals; it picks weights on a portfolio of strategies whose dev
backtests have already been judged (REJECTED, IN_PROGRESS, or PASSED).

## Guard-rails

- Q19 does NOT place, size or cancel any order. It only proposes
  portfolio weights for human review.
- No live, no key, no order, no paper, no holdout.
- The portfolio weight is descriptive; the actual `desk promote`
  command is RED (human-only).

## What Q19 will NOT do

- It will not re-run rejected strategies with different parameters
  (F1v2 family closed; F2 family closed; F3 has 3 trials left).
- It will not register a new F-hypothesis. New hypotheses come
  from Q18 (N6 ML, BLOCKED) or from a fresh research direction
  not in QUEUE.
- It will not change the desk gates. Gates stay as
  `config/desk.yaml` says.
