**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Item**: Q8 — Funding, basis and carry.
**Skills**: `chan-machine-trading`, `hull-derivatives`, `grinold-kahn-apm`.
**Scope**: descriptive exploration only in this pass; no hypothesis, backtest, holdout or paper.
**Cutoff**: strictly `< 2024-07-01T00:00:00Z`.

## Question
Does pre-cutoff USD-M funding contain persistent, seasonal or cross-sectional information that could support delta-neutral carry, and does an extreme funding reading predict the next spot/perp return?

## Data
The existing Q3 funding-rate Parquet store for the 12 brief symbols is the sole input. No premium, open-interest or mark-price data is assumed if absent. The exploration must report exactly which columns and symbols are available. Funding is treated as cash-out from the long side; positive funding means longs pay shorts. No post-cutoff data and no sealed reader.

## Grid
For each symbol and funding fixing (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC):
- mean, median, standard deviation and annualised carry equivalent;
- lag-1, lag-3 and lag-7-fixing autocorrelation;
- cross-sectional rank persistence and dispersion;
- next-fixing funding conditional on top/bottom quintile;
- spot return alignment only if the existing pre-cutoff spot store can be joined without widening the sample.

All cells are reported with sample count and time bounds. BH-FDR is applied to the complete conditional-predictability grid. A moving-block bootstrap CI is required for conditional means. Effect sizes are compared with `research/atlas/costs.md`; this is a carry description, not a net strategy P&L.

## Predictions and falsification
Prediction: funding has positive persistence at one fixing and a positive mean for most symbols, but the cross-sectional next-fixing predictability is weak and does not survive costs. Falsify the descriptive prior if the panel mean is not positive, if lag-1 persistence is absent across the major symbols, or if extreme funding robustly predicts a cost-exceeding reversal.
