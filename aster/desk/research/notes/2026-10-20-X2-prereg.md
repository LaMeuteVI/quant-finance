## 1. What X2 is fixing

Q22 preregistered a hurdle of **6 bps gross** — stated three times in
`research/notes/2026-10-16-Q22-prereg.md`, including the explicit formula
"3× taker hurdle". The memo `research/memos/2026-10-16-Q22-N11.md`
nevertheless applied **60 bps** and reported every axis as failing by
45–58 bps.

The gap is a factor of **10**, and it was applied in the direction that
kills the result. This item re-tests the same hypothesis with:

1. **an actual strategy** run through `desk backtest`, not a descriptive
   quintile table. Quintile spreads are not trade P&L: they ignore
   holding, turnover, and the round trip you actually pay to get from
   one quintile to the other.
2. **the real C1b cost grid** for each instrument, at each notional —
   not a flat 6 bps and not a 3× multiplier.
3. **a consistent hurdle**, preregistered below, and the same on every
   axis.

This is a strategy test, not a re-reading of the quintiles.

## 2. Data — the binding constraint, stated up front

The only signed-flow source on disk is Binance Vision spot aggTrades:

| Symbol | Coverage | Raw rows |
|---|---|---|
| BTCUSDT | **2022-01-01 → 2022-04-30** (4 months) | 107,730,009 |
| ETHUSDT | **2022-01-01 → 2022-02-28** (2 months) | 40,943,091 |

Columns: `agg_trade_id, price, quantity, transact_time, is_buyer_maker`.
Signed flow is `(1 - 2*is_buyer_maker) * quantity`, i.e. taker-side
aggression. **This is spot, not perp.** There is no perp aggTrades store
on disk, so:

- the strategy trades the **spot** book, and is priced at
  `round_trip_bps_spot`;
- Q22's own header described the 60 bps hurdle as a *perp* taker cost
  while the data is spot. X2 does not repeat that: the venue charged is
  the venue traded, and it is stated in every reported number.

**ETH has 2 months, not 4.** That asymmetry is inherited from the disk,
not chosen here, and is reported per axis.

`data/r2/market/spot/aggtrades_bars/volume` — the derived bar store Q22
built — is **empty**. X2 rebuilds the bars from the raw aggTrades.

All data is **pre-cutoff** (`2022-01`…`2022-04`, cutoff `2024-07-01`).
No holdout exists on this store; none is requested.

## 3. Strategy, fixed in advance

A single causal rule, applied per (symbol, bar size):

1. **Signal.** Bar `t`'s signed flow, normalised by that bar's total
   traded volume: `sf_t = signed_flow_t / total_volume_t`, in
   `[-1, +1]`. Positive = net taker buying.
2. **Entry.** On bar `t`, if `sf_t` is in the **top quintile** (`sf_t ≥`
   its own trailing 500-bar 80th percentile), go **long** for the next
   `H` bars. If in the **bottom quintile**, go **short**.
3. **Holding.** `H = 3` bars, no overlapping positions: at most one
   position at a time, exited after `H` bars.
4. **Hysteresis / no re-entry.** After an exit, the same quintile
   cannot be re-entered for `H` bars. Without it the book re-shuffles on
   every noise crossing and pays the round trip again.
5. **Sizing.** Fixed notional fraction of AUM, flat across the book.
   Gross cap 1.0.
6. **Execution.** Taker on both sides, at the C1b grid cost for the
   traded venue and the traded notional. No maker assumption — the
   prereg declares maker execution **out of scope** because no fill model
   exists on this desk.

Bar sizes: **15m and 1h** on **BTC and ETH**, four axes. 1m and 5m are
dropped from the preregistered Q22 perimeter with the reason recorded:
at 5m Q4−Q0 was 4.13–6.59 bps gross against a spot round trip of
12.68–14.28 bps at 100k, i.e. below cost by construction. 1h is added
because Q22's own memo computed that clearing the hurdle needed a
4× larger bar.

The **thresholds are trailing quantiles, not fixed cut-offs.** A fixed
bps cut-off would encode the 2022 volatility level into the rule; a
trailing percentile re-estimates it as conditions change and is computable
causally bar by bar.

## 4. Hurdle — the consistent one, preregistered

The single number every axis is judged against:

> **Net Sharpe ≥ 0.8** (the `config/desk.yaml` gate), where net means
> after the C1b round trip at the traded notional.

Plus the arithmetic pre-computed before the run, which is what decides
the item:

| Axis | Q4−Q0 gross bps (Q22, unchanged data) | C1b spot RT @100k | Verdict on arithmetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTC 15m | 7.16 | 22.68 | fails by 15.5 bps |
| ETH 15m | 14.37 | 24.28 | fails by 9.9 bps |
| BTC 1h | not measured | 22.68 | open |
| ETH 1h | not measured | 24.28 | open |

A single quintile-to-quintile round trip pays the full round trip.
Holding `H = 3` bars amortises it by at most 3× — **only if the signal
persists for the whole hold**, which is the thing under test.

At **1M notional** the spot round trip is 27.46 (BTC) / 32.18 (ETH)
bps, so 15m fails by more, and 1h must carry 2.3–2.8× the measured
15m gross to break even.

**Prediction, written before the run:** all four axes fail
`sharpe_ann_min`. The mechanism is real (Q22 measured Spearman 0.130–0.268
with the correct sign, Kyle λ t-stats 16.6–47.3 on every axis), but a
14.37 bps gross spread cannot pay a 24.28 bps spot round trip, and no
plausible holding period closes a 1.7× gap. The 1h axis is the only one
where the arithmetic is not already decisive, and it will fail on sample
size if on nothing else: 4 months of BTC is ~2 880 hourly bars, and ETH
is ~1 440 — the latter below the `min_obs 500` gate once a 500-bar
trailing quantile warm-up is subtracted.

## 5. Falsification

Rejected if **any** of:

- `sharpe_ann_min` (0.8) fails on the axis — the primary bar;
- `min_obs` (500) fails, on any axis, on its own;
- net P&L after C1b is negative while gross Q4−Q0 is positive, on any
  axis — i.e. the strategy trades the signal and the cost eats it, which
  is the exact failure mode X2 exists to document.

The placebo is carried from Q22 and is **binding**: shuffling the trade
order within each bar must collapse the quintile spread to ≤ 1 bp. If it
does not, the signal lives in bar-end timing rather than in signed flow
and the axis is void regardless of its P&L.

Maker execution, any holdout, any live/paper action: out of scope.

## 6. Gates

`config/desk.yaml` only: `min_obs 500`, `sharpe_ann_min 0.8`,
`max_drawdown_floor -0.35`, `dsr_min 0.95`, `stress_sharpe_ann_min 0.3`.
No PSR, no lag-2, no time-in-market gate. `time_in_market` is reported
but not gated. Family starts at **n = 1**.
