# Glossary — Passarelli, Trading Option Greeks

**Assignment** — the writer's obligation to deliver (call) or take (put) the underlying after the holder exercises; American shorts can be assigned any day (ch01, ch08)
**At parity** — an option trading at pure intrinsic value, with no time value (ch01, ch08)
**Average-delta rule** — a move of ΔS changes value by the mean of start and end deltas × ΔS = `Δ·ΔS + ½Γ·ΔS²` (ch02, ch05, ch13)
**Back month / front month** — later vs nearest expiry (ch03, ch11)
**Backspread** — more long than short options of one class and expiry, e.g. sell 1 × 70 call, buy 2 × 75 calls; long vol above the short strike, short vol below (ch16)
**Basis (parity)** — the interest and dividend terms in put-call parity; interest favors calls, dividends favor stock (ch06, ch07, ch08)
**Box** — long call vertical + long put vertical on the same strikes; a riskless loan worth strike width minus interest (ch06, ch09)
**Break-even IV move** — `|Θ| × days / vega`, the IV rise needed to pay for theta in a long-vega trade (ch12, ch15)
**Butterfly** — 1 × 2 × 1 at three equidistant strikes; maximum profit at the body strike (ch10)
**Calendar (time) spread** — same strike and type, different expiries; long calendar = −Γ, +θ, +vega (ch11)
**Cash-secured put** — short put backed by `(K − premium) × 100` cash; effective purchase price `K − premium` (ch01, ch05)
**Combo** — synthetic stock: long call + short put at one strike and expiry (ch06)
**Condor / iron condor** — four-strike wing spread of two verticals; a long condor is equivalent to a short iron condor (ch10)
**Conversion / reversal** — long stock + long put + short call (rho negative) / its mirror (rho positive); isolates interest and dividend risk (ch06, ch07, ch08)
**Covered call (buy-write)** — long stock + short call; same Γ, θ, vega as the naked call, net delta `1 − Δcall` (ch01, ch05)
**Covered put** — short stock + short put; a naked call in disguise (ch05, ch06)
**Crush** — the 10–20+ point IV collapse after a known event (ch12, ch14)
**Delta (Δ)** — `∂V/∂S`; also share equivalent, rough ITM probability; call 0 to +1, put −1 to 0 (ch02)
**Delta lean** — deliberately under- or over-hedging to offset hidden directional risk from vega changing with price (ch16)
**Delta neutral** — net position delta zero via stock; leaves a gamma, theta, vega position (ch12, ch13)
**Diagonal / double diagonal** — different strikes and expiries; the double diagonal is an iron condor with front-month guts and back-month wings (ch11)
**Direction neutral vs indifferent** — neutral expects no trend (short gamma); indifferent wants movement or IV in either direction (ch04, ch12)
**Dividend dates** — declaration, ex-date (the one that matters), record, payable (ch08)
**Double whammy** — a falling stock hurts a short put through delta and vega at once, so stops are hit early and gap (ch05)
**Early exercise rule (ITM call)** — exercise the day before the ex-date if `Dividend − Interest > Put bid` (ch08)
**Effective price** — stock price implied by assignment net of premium, e.g. `K₁ + D` for a bull call (ch09)
**Gamma (Γ)** — `∂Δ/∂S`, deltas per $1; long options +Γ, short −Γ; peaks ATM (ch02, ch04)
**Gamma scalping** — re-hedging a long-gamma position on swings, selling strength and buying weakness; revenue = scalps, expense = theta (ch13)
**Going home flat** — covering residual delta at the close (ch13)
**Guts / wings** — middle strikes / outer strikes of a wing spread (ch10)
**HAPI (Hope and Pray Index)** — contra-indicator: the more you hope, the worse the trade (ch17)
**Historical (realized) volatility** — annualized standard deviation of daily log returns, usually 30 trading days (ch03, ch14)
**Implied volatility (IV)** — vol input that makes the model equal the market price; the price of options in vol points (ch02, ch03)
**Indifference point** — `K + call premium` for a covered call (ch05)
**Intrinsic / time value** — ITM amount / premium minus intrinsic; only time value is exposed to θ and vega (ch01, ch02)
**Jelly roll** — same strike across two months, long synthetic in one and short in the other (ch06)
**Know Thy Risk** — know trade greeks, net portfolio greeks and up/down risk including 3σ gaps (ch17)
**LEAPS** — listed options of 1 to 2.5 years; material rho (ch01, ch07)
**Mean reversion of IV** — IV returns to its 6–12 month range unless a regime change resets it (ch03, ch12)
**Mesa (realized-vol)** — one-day RV jump that stays 30 days then drops out of the window (ch14)
**Moneyness** — how far ITM/OTM, measured proportionally (ch02)
**Negative scalping** — hedging short gamma; each hedge realizes a loss (ch11, ch13)
**OCC** — Options Clearing Corporation, counterparty to every listed option (ch01)
**Open interest vs volume** — contracts outstanding vs traded in a period (ch01)
**Pain-point conversion** — option stop to stock price: `ΔS = ΔV / (|Δ| + Γ/2)` (ch05)
**Pin risk** — unknown assignment at a strike near expiry (ch06, ch15)
**Portfolio margining** — risk-based margin that makes hedged books affordable; Reg T is rule-based (ch12, ch09)
**Position delta** — `contracts × Δ × 100` shares (ch02)
**Price-time matrix** — grid of option value by stock price and days left, used to plan exits (ch04)
**Put-call parity (trader form)** — `Call + Strike − Interest + Dividend = Put + Stock` (ch02, ch06)
**Ratio spread / ratio vertical** — unequal long/short quantities; the ratio vertical (short more) is the mirror of the backspread (ch16)
**Rho (ρ)** — change in value per +1 point in rates; calls +, puts −; ≈ strike × time (ch02, ch07)
**Risk reversal** — delta-neutral long call/short put (or reverse) to trade excess skew (ch16)
**Rule of 16** — 1-day σ = IV/16, n-day σ = (IV/16)√n (ch03, ch10)
**Rush** — pre-event IV bid-up as insurance demand rises (ch12, ch14)
**Short-stock rebate** — interest earned on short-sale proceeds (ch02, ch06)
**Skew** — vertical (IV by strike; equity puts richer) and term (IV by expiry) structure (ch03, ch11, ch16)
**Skating** — surviving a day without assignment on a short ITM call before the ex-date (ch08)
**Smiley / frown** — pre-expiry P&L shapes of long / short gamma (ch13)
**Strangle swap** — long back-month strangle vs short front-month strangle (ch11)
**Straddle / strangle** — call + put at one strike / at two strikes; guts strangle uses ITM options (ch15)
**Synthetic** — option or stock rebuilt from others; after the basis only delta differs (ch06)
**Taking the day out** — the market prices tomorrow's decay into today's session (ch02)
**Theo (theoretical value)** — model value calibrated inside the bid-ask via IV (ch02)
**Theta (θ)** — value change per calendar day (7-day week convention); the rent paid for gamma (ch02, ch04)
**Vega** — value change per 1 vol point; equal for call and put; peaks ATM, grows with √T (ch02, ch03)
**Vertical spread** — two options, same type and expiry, different strikes; the short strike is the target (ch09)
**Vol chart** — IV overlaid on 30-day RV; nine RV/IV patterns (ch14)
**Volatility buyer / seller** — +Γ,+vega,−θ vs −Γ,−vega,+θ (ch04)
**Would I Do It Now?** — re-decide each position as if flat; if not, close (ch05, ch09, ch17)
