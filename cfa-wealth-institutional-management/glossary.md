# Glossary — CFA Wealth and Institutional Management (L3 R21-R24, R28-R30)

**Active risk budget** — tracking-error allowance versus the policy portfolio for active management and TAA; the endowment case raises it from 100 to 250 bp (ch 05, ch 07)
**After-tax excess return** — after-tax portfolio return minus after-tax benchmark return; tax alpha is this minus the pre-tax excess return (ch 02)
**Annuity due with growth** — payments starting now and growing at g, valued with the adjusted rate (1+r)/(1+g) − 1 in annuity-due mode (ch 03, ch 06)
**Annuity puzzle** — retirees avoid annuities despite longevity protection (loss of control, perceived cost, hope for upside) (ch 01, ch 03)
**Asset location** — placing assets in the account where their tax treatment is best (tax-efficient assets taxable, tax-inefficient sheltered), subject to goals and horizon (ch 01, ch 02)
**Asset owner** — pension fund, SWF, endowment or foundation, distinguished from banks and insurers by long horizon and low liquidity need (ch 04, ch 07)
**Balance sheet leverage model** — bank or insurer equity as the residual A − L; asset and liability shocks scale by A/E (ch 04)
**Bond-like vs equity-like human capital** — stable tenured pay (low risk adjustment) vs volatile or bonus-linked pay (high risk adjustment) (ch 03, ch 06)
**Build-operate-transfer (BOT) / PPP** — private party builds and runs a public facility for a concession period, then hands it to the state (ch 07)
**Canada model** — high alternatives, active, mostly internal, with a reference portfolio and the total portfolio approach (ch 04)
**Capital sufficiency analysis** — deterministic or Monte Carlo test of whether capital funds the goals; guidance 75%-90% success (ch 01)
**Cash drag** — return lost while money moves between managers or waits to be invested (ch 05)
**Charitable remainder trust (CRT)** — irrevocable trust paying income to named beneficiaries, remainder to charity; tax-free sale inside (ch 02)
**Commitment pacing** — annual private-fund commitment level that targets an allocation given modeled calls and distributions (ch 05, ch 07)
**Completion portfolio** — index-based portfolio that, with a retained concentrated stock, replicates benchmark exposures (ch 02)
**Concentrated-position approaches** — sell, stage with completion portfolio, hedge and monetize, exchange fund, charitable, deferral or step-up (ch 02)
**Constructive sale** — hedge removing essentially all risk, treated as a taxable sale (ch 02)
**Consumption gap** — retirees spend less than models predict (ch 01)
**Counterparty risk (annuity)** — the provider cannot honor payments (ch 06)
**Denominator effect** — total assets fall more than stale private marks, so the private share jumps (ch 07)
**Direct vs fund investing** — direct saves GP fees and gives exit control but needs a team and adds concentration, governance and liability risk (ch 07)
**Donor-advised fund (DAF)** — account for the donor's giving; appreciated assets are sold tax-free inside (ch 02)
**Economic (holistic) balance sheet** — financial capital, pension PVs and human capital against debts and PV of consumption and bequests (ch 03, ch 06)
**Endowment model** — high alternatives, active, externally managed (ch 04, ch 05)
**Endowment spending rule** — `S = w·S_prev(1+π) + (1−w)·s·AUM_avg`; the case's Yale-style version weights prior spending 66% and 5% of last market value 34% (ch 04, ch 05)
**Enterprise risk management (ERM)** — board risk tolerance, IPS limits, management implementation, risk team measurement and reporting, action on breaches (ch 07)
**Equity duration (bank or insurer)** — `(A/E)D_A − ((A/E)−1)D_L(Δi/Δy)` (ch 04)
**Equity monetization** — hedge a stock position then borrow against it to take cash without a sale (ch 02)
**ESG integration** — explicit, systematic inclusion of ESG factors in analysis and decisions; contrasts with exclusion (ch 05, ch 07)
**Exchange fund** — partnership pooling low-basis stocks tax-free; 7-year minimum, 20% qualified assets (ch 02)
**Family governance** — constitution, assembly, council, board, office, foundation (ch 02)
**Funded ratio** — fair value of plan assets / PV of obligations; the DB risk measure is surplus volatility (ch 04)
**Gate** — a fund manager's right to limit withdrawals in stress; lengthens time-to-cash (ch 05, ch 07)
**Geltner unsmoothing** — `r* = (r_t − ρr_{t−1})/(1−ρ)`; Okunev-White adds lags, GLM models a moving average (ch 07)
**Goals-based investing** — assign capital to goals with own horizon and success probability; aggregate may sit off the efficient frontier (ch 01, ch 06)
**Human capital** — mortality-weighted PV of after-tax labor income at a risk-adjusted rate (ch 03, ch 06)
**Human life value (HLV)** — PV of the insured's net after-tax income grossed up to pre-tax, less existing cover (ch 03, ch 06)
**Illiquidity premium** — expected compensation for tying up capital; rises with horizon; estimated by put-option approach (ch 05)
**Investment policy statement (IPS)** — operating manual of objectives, constraints, allocation with ranges, rebalancing and duties (ch 01, ch 04)
**Just transition** — sustainability measures designed to limit harm to workers and poor households (ch 07)
**KYC** — know-your-customer facts, including wealth source and objectives (ch 01)
**LDI (liability-driven investing)** — hedging portfolio matched to liabilities plus a growth portfolio (ch 04)
**Liquidity budget** — minimum weights for fast buckets and a maximum for illiquids; read cumulatively (ch 05)
**Liquidity management plan (five steps)** — parameters, assessment, cash-flow model, stress test, emergency plan (ch 07)
**Liquidity risk band** — `(NAV of illiquids + uncalled commitments)/total assets`; breach triggers fewer commitments or secondary sales (ch 04, ch 07)
**Longevity risk** — outliving assets; transferred by annuities (ch 01, ch 03, ch 06)
**Mixed asset** — a home with both consumption and investment features (ch 03, ch 06)
**Mortality credit** — gain to annuity survivors from capital of members who died early (ch 03)
**Needs analysis** — cash needs plus PV of survivor spending minus survivor income minus capital available (ch 03, ch 06)
**Norway model** — mostly passive public stocks and bonds with tight tracking error (ch 04)
**Overlay** — derivative positions that change exposure while external managers stay in place (ch 05)
**PCGE** — net unrealized gains over net assets of a fund (ch 02)
**Physical vs transition climate risk** — damage from climate itself vs cost of the shift to low carbon; plus opportunities (ch 07)
**Post-liquidation return** — after-tax return deducting tax on embedded gains at the horizon (ch 02)
**Principal-agent problem** — conflict between asset owners and internal or external agents, notably fee structures (ch 04)
**Rebalancing corridor** — range around target; breach triggers rebalancing to target or edge; wider for volatile classes (ch 05)
**Reference portfolio / total portfolio approach** — passive risk-equivalent benchmark; keep total risk intact as investments change (ch 04)
**Relative value (gift vs bequest)** — ratio of after-tax future values; 1/(1−T_e) when returns and rates are equal (ch 02)
**Risk tolerance** — willingness plus ability to bear risk; use the lower in practice (ch 01)
**Santiago Principles (GAPP)** — voluntary governance and transparency standards for SWFs (ch 04)
**Self-insurance** — retaining a risk with a savings buffer, e.g. six months of spending (ch 06)
**Smoothing effect** — lagged private valuations raise the private share when public assets fall (ch 05, ch 07)
**Social license to operate** — community acceptance that sustains a company's activity (ch 07)
**Sovereign wealth fund types** — stabilization, savings, reserve, pension reserve, development (ch 04, ch 07)
**Step-up in basis** — basis reset to fair value at death (ch 02)
**Tax alpha** — after-tax minus pre-tax excess return (ch 02)
**Tax-efficiency ratio (TER)** — after-tax annualized return over pre-tax (ch 02)
**Tax lot** — a purchase with its own date and basis; FIFO, LIFO, HIFO, specified (ch 02)
**Tax-loss harvesting / wash sale** — realize losses to defer tax; a repurchase within 30 days either side disallows the loss (ch 02)
**Time-to-cash table** — buckets by time to convert holdings to cash without moving price (ch 05, ch 07)
**Total-wealth allocation** — `FC equity % = (TW × target − HC × HC equity %)/FC` (ch 03)
**Unfunded commitment** — capital promised to private funds but not yet called (ch 07)
**Universal owner** — large diversified investor exposed to externalized environmental and social costs across holdings (ch 07)
**Unsmoothing** — removing serial correlation from reported private returns; or use a public proxy (ch 05, ch 07)
**Vehicle tracking error (mispricing)** — ETF premium or discount and cash drag; futures supply-demand, dividend and rate differentials; swap none (ch 05)
