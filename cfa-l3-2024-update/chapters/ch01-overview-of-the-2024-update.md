# Chapter 1: Overview of the 2024 CFA L3 PM Pathway Update

> CFA Institute, *2025 CFA Program Curriculum Level III — Portfolio Management Pathway Box Set*, 2024.

## Core Idea

The **CFA L3 2024** refreshes the 2023 PM Pathway. The seven volumes cover Asset Allocation, Portfolio Construction, Performance Measurement (new skill), Derivatives and Risk Management, Ethics, and the two PM Pathway volumes (Equity, Credit/Trade/Case). The 2024 update reflects the post-COVID market, FRTB, ESG standardisation, LDI evolution, and GIPS 2020.

## Key 2024 Changes

1. Post-COVID macro outlook.
2. FRTB-aware risk management.
3. Updated factor models (Fama-French 5-factor, q-factor).
4. ESG integration standardisation.
5. GIPS 2020 standards.
6. Updated trading cost estimates.
