# Chapter 4: Volume 3 — Performance Measurement (3 LMs) — NEW SKILL

> CFA L3 2024, V3 Performance Measurement. **NEW SKILL**.

The 3 LMs cover: (1) Portfolio Performance Evaluation, (2) Investment Manager Selection, (3) GIPS Overview.

The 2024 update reflects the GIPS 2020 standards (replacing GIPS 2010) and the institutional use of the Fama-French 5-factor attribution.

This volume is the basis for the `cfa-performance-measurement` skill. The three LMs provide the institutional framework for performance attribution (Brinson-Fachler, Carino smoothing, factor attribution), manager selection, and GIPS compliance.
