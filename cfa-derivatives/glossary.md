# Glossary — CFA Derivatives (L1 · L2 · L3)

**Accrued interest (AI)** — coupon earned since the last coupon date, (NAD/NTD)(C/n); clean price + AI = dirty (full) price (ch 03)
**Advanced set, settled in arrears** — a rate fixed at the start of the period and paid at its end (swaps, caplets); a FRA is advanced set, advanced settled (ch 03, 04)
**American option** — exercisable any time up to expiration; European only at expiration. Style has no link to geography (ch 01, 02, 04)
**Arbitrage** — riskless profit with zero capital from identical payoffs priced differently; merger "risk arbitrage" is not arbitrage (ch 01, 02, 04)
**Arbitrage-free pricing / law of one price** — identical payoffs must have one price; the derivative price is the one that rules out riskless profit (ch 01, 02, 04)
**Basis (futures)** — spot price minus futures price; converges to zero at expiration (ch 03). **Bond basis** = cash price − futures price × CF (ch 06)
**Basis risk** — the hedge and the hedged exposure do not move one-for-one; also the unstable correlation of a cross/macro hedge (ch 06, 07)
**Binomial model** — tree of up (u) and down (d) moves; values options by replication or by risk-neutral expectation; recombines when u, d are constant (ch 02, 04)
**Black model** — BSM variant for options on futures/forwards and, with adjustments, caplets, floorlets and swaptions (ch 04)
**Black–Scholes–Merton (BSM)** — continuous-time European option model; call = leveraged stock (S·e^(−γT)N(d1) − X·e^(−rT)N(d2)) (ch 04)
**Bull / bear spread** — bull: buy low strike, sell high strike; bear: buy high strike, sell low strike. Debit or credit depending on calls vs puts (ch 05)
**Calendar (time) spread** — same strike, different expiries; long calendar = sell near, buy far (ch 05)
**Caplet / floorlet / cap / floor** — interest rate call / put on one period; cap / floor = strip of them (ch 04)
**Carry arbitrage / reverse carry** — forward too high: sell forward, buy spot, borrow; too low: buy forward, short spot, lend (ch 03)
**Carry (cost of carry)** — net of costs (storage, insurance) and benefits (dividends, coupons, foreign interest, convenience yield) of holding an asset (ch 02, 03)
**Carry trade** — borrow the low-yield currency, invest in the high-yield one; earns positive roll yield, negative skew (ch 07)
**Cash equitization** — buy index futures (or long call + short put) on idle cash to remove cash drag (ch 06)
**Cheapest-to-deliver (CTD)** — deliverable bond that costs the short least after the CF adjustment (ch 03, 06)
**Clearinghouse** — middleman that guarantees performance of exchange-traded contracts; marks positions daily (ch 01)
**Collar** — long asset + long OTM put + short OTM call; zero-cost when c₀ = p₀ (ch 05). FX version: short risk reversal (ch 07)
**Contingent claim** — derivative giving a right, not an obligation: options, credit derivatives; nonlinear payoff, premium paid (ch 01, 04)
**Convenience yield** — non-monetary advantage of holding an asset (ch 02)
**Conversion factor (CF)** — scales a deliverable bond to the contract's notional coupon (6%); F₀ = Q₀ × CF (ch 03, 06)
**Covered call** — long asset + short call; yield enhancement, sells the right tail; breakeven S₀ − c₀ (ch 05)
**Covered interest parity (CIP)** — forward FX rate implied by spot and interest differential; no-arbitrage (ch 06, 07)
**Credit default swap (CDS)** — periodic premium for compensation after a credit event; CLN, TRS, credit spread option are relatives (ch 01)
**Cross-currency basis swap** — exchange of notionals at S₀ at start and end plus floating coupons in two currencies; basis quoted on the non-USD leg (ch 06)
**Cross hedge / proxy hedge / macro hedge** — hedging with a correlated instrument / back to home currency / against risk factors or scenarios (ch 07)
**Currency overlay** — separate program managing portfolio currency exposure: passive hedging, directional latitude, or FX as an asset class (ch 07)
**Delta / gamma / theta / vega / rho** — sensitivity of option value to S, to delta, to time, to volatility, to r (ch 04, 05)
**Delta hedge** — N_H = −portfolio delta / delta of hedge; fixes small moves only, gamma first, then delta (ch 04)
**Derivative** — instrument whose performance derives from, and transforms, an underlying (ch 01)
**Digital (binary) option** — pays fixed amount if in the money; speculators' tool (ch 07)
**Effective fed funds rate (FFE)** — traded overnight rate; fed funds futures settle on its monthly average (ch 06)
**Equity swap / total return swap** — exchange of equity (index) return for fixed/floating; equity leg resets to notional (ch 02, 03, 06)
**Escrow method** — strip PV of known dividends from S, build tree on the remainder (ch 04)
**Exercise (intrinsic) value** — max(0, S − X) call; max(0, X − S) put (ch 02, 04)
**Fed funds futures probability** — P = (FFE implied − current)/(after − current), using midpoints (ch 06)
**Fiduciary call / protective put** — call + zero-coupon bond face X / asset + put; both pay max(S_T, X) (ch 02, 05)
**Forward commitment** — forward, futures or swap: both parties obliged, linear payoff, zero initial value (ch 01)
**Forward contract / NDF** — OTC agreement at F₀(T); NDF is cash-settled (ch 01, 07)
**Forward points / forward premium** — (F − S) in last decimal; positive = base currency at premium (ch 07)
**Forward rate agreement (FRA)** — forward on a rate; long pays fixed, receives floating, hedges rising rates; X × Y notation (ch 02, 03, 06)
**Forward rate bias** — forward premium/discount overstates or misdirects subsequent spot moves; UIP fails (ch 07)
**Forward value vs price** — price: fixed term; value: PV of (F_t − F₀), starts at 0 (ch 02, 03)
**Futures contract** — standardized exchange-traded forward with daily settlement, margin and clearinghouse guarantee (ch 01)
**FX swap** — spot and opposite forward in the same pair; rolls hedges (matched or mismatched); not a currency swap (ch 07)
**Gamma risk** — risk that a jump leaves a delta-hedged book unhedged (ch 04)
**Hedge ratio** — binomial: h = Δoption/ΔS. Currency: hedge notional / asset value; minimum-variance h* = ρσ(R_DC)/σ(ΔS) (ch 02, 04, 07)
**Implied volatility** — σ that matches model price to market price; quoted across strike (smile/skew), expiry (term structure), both (surface) (ch 04, 05)
**Initial / maintenance margin** — performance bond at entry / floor that triggers a call back to initial margin (ch 01)
**Interest rate swap** — fixed vs floating exchange; rate s = (1 − DF_n)/ΣDF; value = N(s_t − s₀)ΣDF (ch 02, 03)
**Moneyness** — S vs X: in, at, out of the money (ch 01, 02)
**Notional principal** — amount on which swap payments are computed; overstates risk (ch 01)
**Offset** — closing a futures position by the opposite trade (ch 01)
**Off-market forward** — struck away from the zero-value price, settled by an up-front payment (ch 02)
**Open interest** — contracts outstanding (ch 01)
**Option premium** — price paid by the buyer for the right (ch 01)
**Payer / receiver swaption** — right to pay fixed / to receive fixed; callable bond = straight bond − receiver swaption (ch 04)
**Price limits** — daily band around the prior settlement; limit up, limit down, locked limit (ch 01)
**Put–call parity** — S₀ + p₀ = c₀ + X/(1 + r)^T; with forward: F₀/(1 + r)^T + p₀ = c₀ + X/(1 + r)^T (ch 02, 04, 05)
**Risk reversal** — long OTM call + short OTM put (long), trades skew; FX long risk reversal = long call, short put (ch 05, 07)
**Risk-neutral probability (π)** — π = (1 + r − d)/(u − d); a pricing device, not a forecast (ch 02, 04)
**Roll yield** — gain/loss from the forward converging to spot; positive when selling base at a premium or buying at a discount (ch 07)
**Seagull spread** — short: long ATM put + short OTM put + short OTM call; long: short ATM + long OTM wings (ch 07)
**Settlement price** — official daily price used for marking to market (ch 01)
**Straddle / strangle** — long call + put at one strike / at different OTM strikes; long volatility (ch 05, 07)
**Swap** — OTC exchange of cash flows; a strip of off-market forwards sharing one fixed price (ch 01, 02, 03)
**Time value** — option price minus exercise value; decays to zero; not the time value of money (ch 02, 04)
**Uncovered interest parity (UIP)** — high-yield currency expected to depreciate by the yield gap; often fails (ch 07)
**VIX / VIX futures** — 30-day S&P 500 implied vol; not investable, trade futures (contango, negative roll) (ch 05, 06)
**Variance swap** — realized variance vs strike K²; N_var = N_vega/(2K); convex long-vol tail hedge (ch 06)
**Volatility smile / skew** — IV high on both wings / high on OTM puts, low on OTM calls (ch 04, 05)
